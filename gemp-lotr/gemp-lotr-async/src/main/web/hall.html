Open Bot Table

Welcome to the future! Here you may open a match against a neural-net bot trained on playing the LOTR-TCG. This is an experimental feature, and so can go wrong in many ways. Remember:
  • Bots will concede if they get confused or have an error.
  • Bots will sometimes make dumb decisions that a human never would. Don't give them the blue ATAR ring or they will self-corrupt, for example.
Please report a bug for any matches where you encounter a crash or other concession by the bot. But don't bother reporting bad gameplay; we already know they are dumb. :)

Open Casual Table

Open a casual table to play another human 1v1 in your format of choice. Remember:
  • If you have to leave early for any reason, go to Options -> Concede game (and then pass your current action, if necessary)
  • If you have no deck of your own yet, pick one from Select Library Deck: the Deck Library has many starter, sample, and championship decks from years past.
  • Always be courteous to your fellow players! Use the description field to avoid misunderstandings.

Open League Table i

League games are ranked: each win or loss counts toward the league's standings, within the league's limits on games per serie and per opponent. They always use the Competitive timer (40 minutes each, 5 minutes per decision), and cannot be private or invite-only.
Leagues are multi-week or multi-month events where players compete in many matches across days to improve their skills and hone their craft.

Join leagues below or using the Events tab at the top of the main hall. If you enter a Limited league (Sealed or Draft), you will need to receive your cards and construct a new deck using those cards before you can participate.

Create Tournament

Tournaments are competitive events where players fight to prove their skills against other players! Anyone may create or enter a tournament, but know that others will be playing to win! :)
  • Constructed: Players provide their own constructed decks with no limits beyond the selected format.
  • Sealed: Players will receive standardized starter decks with digital boosters and 30 minutes (by default) to crack those packs and build a deck for the tournament.
  • Live Draft: Players will sit around the table and open digital packs, selecting 1 card and passing the rest to their opponents. After all packs have been drafted, players have 15 minutes (by default) to construct a deck for the tournament.
  • Solo Draft: Players will each draft against the computer, meaning that they have a limited selection but the remainder is not passed to a human opponent. After drafting, players have 30 minutes (by default) to construct a deck for the tournament.

Number of Players: i
The tournament starts when this many players have signed up (or earlier, if Early Start is on and you start it). With 2 or fewer players there is no ready check and no pairing choice.
Pairing Type: i
Swiss: every round pairs players with similar records, and everyone plays every round; the best record wins. Single-elimination: a loss knocks you out, and the last player standing wins.
Deck-building Duration (minutes): i
How long players have, after opening their packs or finishing the draft, to build and register a deck before the first round.
Competitive Mode: i
(Named "Competitive …" instead of "Casual …"; no spectators)
A competitive tournament is named "Competitive <format>" instead of "Casual <format>". Its games have no spectators, the game chat is limited to the two players, and player names are hidden while the tournament is still signing players up.
Early Start: i
(Tournament owner can force start without all player slots filled)
You, as the first player in the queue, get a Start button in the hall to begin before every seat is filled.
Ready Check: i
When the tournament is about to start, every player must confirm they are present within this time. Players who do not are removed from the queue (and refunded), so nobody starts against an absent opponent.
Draft Timer: i
How long each player has to make each pick during the live draft.
