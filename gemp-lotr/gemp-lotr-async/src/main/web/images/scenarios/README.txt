Pictures a scenario can show that are not cards.

A message in a scenario shows one with   zoom: image:<file name>   (see the scenario README, and the authoring tool's
"Shows large" field, which lists what is in this folder).  The picture is shown as large as the window allows, with
the message beside it.  Parts of it are picked out with   focus: rect(left, top, width, height)   in percent of the
picture, and it is enlarged with   magnify.

- .png, .jpg, .gif or .webp; names made of letters, digits, spaces, dots, dashes and underscores.
- Any shape works.  A card is 357 x 497, and placeholder.png is that shape, with a grid every 10 percent to judge
  rectangles by.
- Files here are served to anybody, like every other picture of the site's.
