PC Errata i

All errata are marked in-game with a red slash in the card's upper-left corner. Errata issued by the Player's Council instead of Decipher also carry the errata date in the lower-left corner of the card.

Every released erratum has a full write-up of the PC's thoughts and reasons on the LOTR-TCG wiki.
