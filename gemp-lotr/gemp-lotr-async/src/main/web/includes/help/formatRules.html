A 'format' is a shorthand for what cards are legal to use and what ruleset is being followed. Every format that can be played in the Game Hall is listed below. i

  • X-list: cards that can't be used in the format.
  • R-list: cards a deck may hold no more than 1 copy of.
  • Limit 2 / Limit 3: cards a deck may hold at most two / three copies of.
  • Also legal: cards from outside the format's sets that it allows.
  • PC errata: the format plays the Player's Council's updated versions of some cards (see Help › PC Errata).
Click (or right-click) a card to see it larger, or tick "Card images" to see the lists as pictures. The box above the format list finds a format by name, or every format that lists a card.
