My Scenarios

Scenarios are games of one player against a scripted opponent, set up and played out as their author wrote them: tutorials that teach the game a step at a time, puzzles with a position to find the way out of, and sandboxes to try things in. Everybody plays them from Play > Play Scenario or Tutorial.

You can write your own here. A new one opens in the authoring tool, where you set the table up and play it through as you want the player to, and the tool writes the script. Your scenarios are offered to you alone. When one is ready, submit it for review: an admin plays it, and either approves it, making it a public scenario that every player is offered, or sends it back with the changes they would like. To improve a public scenario, copy it with Copy Public: approved, your version replaces it.
