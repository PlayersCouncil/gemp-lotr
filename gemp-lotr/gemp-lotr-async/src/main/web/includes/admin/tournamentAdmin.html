Running tournaments i

Every tournament that has started and not finished, including the daily and player-made ones that are not on the calendar. Open one to change its stage or seat players at tables by hand. Anything else (an older or finished tournament, or a queue) can be opened by its id.

World Championship i

Manual kickoff i

						
					

					

						The tournament waits at "Awaiting Kickoff" once it starts, until an admin sets its stage to Preparing below.
						Useful when players sign up to register decks and the rounds are run by hand (see "Tables" below).
					


					

						
Prizes i

						

							Extra prizes handed out when the tournament finishes, on top of the automatic daily prizes. Each tier says
							who qualifies (a range of final places, ties included, or a minimum number of games played in the tournament)
							and what they get. An item is a card or pack id (9_1 is set 9 card 1; add * for
							foil or T for tengwar) or, when the card does not exist yet, "to be announced" with a description:
							players receive a placeholder card that is swapped for the real one once it is resolved on the Prizes tab.

Stage i

				

					
    
						
  • Starting - first stage of a tournament, which handles any setup (such as issuing packs in a sealed event).
    • 
						
  • Drafting - a Draft tournament is actively issuing cards to players.
    • 
						
  • Deck-building - a Sealed or Draft tournament is permitting players to construct decks with their event material.
    • 
						
  • Registering Decks - a brief period of leeway for players to register the decks they have constructed during limited events.
    • 
						
  • Awaiting Kickoff - a scheduled tournament will idle until an admin sets the status here to Preparing.
    • 
						
  • Preparing - a tournament that has been kicked off or unpaused and is about to resume play.
    • 
						
  • Playing Games - the tournament will generate tables for the current round and then wait for them to complete.
    • 
						
  • Paused - the tournament will idle until an admin sets the status here to Preparing.
    • 
						
  • Finished - a tournament which has reached its final round and will present the results.
    •

Tables i

				

					Seats players at game tables by hand, outside the automatic pairing - the way a World Championship round is run:
					players sign up to a tournament (one per format) so their decks are registered, and admins seat each round here.
					Each player plays with the deck they registered in the deck source tournament, and the games are reported
					under that tournament. Type the names (they autocomplete from the deck source's players; Enter moves to the next
					box and adds a table), or paste a round's pairings. After the tables are created, a number at the end of the
					series name is advanced for the next round.
				


				

					
Series name i

					
				

				
The description shown on each table, so players and spectators can tell which round it belongs to.


				

					
Deck source i

					
				

				

					
Deck source id

					
				

				

					The tournament whose registered decks the players use; it is also the tournament the games are reported under.
					This tournament comes first; for a two-part event pick the tournament of the other format for the second part.
					Running and recently finished tournaments are listed; "Other tournament id..." reaches any other.
				

				


				

					
Format i

					
				

				
The format the games are played in. Picking a deck source selects its format.
