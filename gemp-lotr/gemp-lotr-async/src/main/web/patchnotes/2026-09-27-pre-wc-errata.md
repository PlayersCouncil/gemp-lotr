First up, the changes themselves:

- **[[52_46|Uruk Captain]]** (2R46): now exerts a *unique* Uruk-hai to play an Uruk-hai from your discard pile.
- **[[53_66|Orthanc Berserker]]** (3R66): back to its original text, spotting 4 burdens instead of 5.
- **[[60_20|Final Strike]]** (10U20): playing Gollum from your discard pile at site 9 is now limited to once per phase.
- **[[Ritual Oath of Enmity]]** (V2_5): adding a [dunland] token needs 25 twilight tokens in regions 2 and 3 (still 18 in region 1).
- **[[War-beacon]]** (V3_35): its third ability now reveals the top 4 cards of your draw deck.
- **[[Cover of Darkness, Omen of Gloom]]** (V3_65): its Shadow ability is replaced with "Each time your minion is killed, you may remove (1) and hinder a twilight condition to shuffle that minion into your draw deck."
- **[[Gondor Calls For Aid!]]** (V3_83): its Maneuver action is now a Fellowship action.
- **[[Ominous Sky]]** (V3_97): no longer hinders itself. Each time you play an Orc or Troll, add (1) (limit once per phase).


# Adjusting Previously Errata-ed Uruk-hai

Our V3 errata batch included numerous buffs to Isengard Uruk-hai aimed at making them more competitive in both FOTR and Movie. Two of these went too far and are being scaled back.


## Uruk Captain

Allowing Captain to exert any Uruk-hai to play another from the discard pile often led to endless chains of Uruk-hai swarm in both formats. This was amplified in Movie with two interactions:

- Cirith Ungol Uruks who are stronger than FOTR Uruks for the same cost, all have 2 vitality and in the case of Sentinel are practically free.

- [[Endless Night]] allowing for a swarm of low cost damage +1 Uruk-hai archers. Which is devastating even outside of [[Brownlands]].

To address this Captain is being changed to:

> **Shadow**: Remove (1) and exert a unique Uruk-hai to play an Uruk-hai from your discard pile.

This still allows for Captain to bring out a strong showing of Uruk-hai and recycle key minions more easily than the original, but removes the possibility of chaining cheap, low strength, damage+1 Uruk-hai who might all also be archers.

## Isengard Berserker

The original never saw serious play as few would risk 5 burdens (apart from an initial high bid to kill Frodo) and lacked synergy with corruption decks. The change swung too far the other way. The volume of cheap or free Isengard support conditions made the cost of a very strong effect practically negligible.

We've decided instead to remove the card spotting requirement, reverting to the original but lowering the burden requirement to 4, essentially making Berserker a Toldea for Isengard serving as a similar threat against the classic "bid 3, go first, get Aragorn" start, for another culture.

# The Shadow Pyramid
The shadow pyramid introduced in V3 has proven a bit too consistent to set up and a bit non-interactive for the free peoples player along the way. Omen of Gloom was able to pull any pyramid piece down, and the self-hinder mechanic in particular means that once used, [[Ominous Sky]] is nearly impossible to remove once put in place, which has the hinder acting more as protection than cost.

The intention of the pyramid was for it to be a powerful game state that took investment from the shadow player to pull off. It wasn't meant to be an inevitability or create a non-interactive game state for the free peoples player facing it. To that end we're making a few adjustments.

## Ominous Sky
The self hinder cost on the secondary twilight adding ability is being changed to an automatic "once per phase" instead of hindering. This has the same twilight benefit while allowing the possibility of the Free People player discarding it in a later phase.

## Omen of Gloom
The accelerating element of the Gloom, which was generally used to summon endless night, made it very nearly the only version of Cover of Darkness used. The accelerant ability is being removed and replaced with:

> Each time a minion is killed, you may remove (1) and hinder a twilight condition to shuffle that minion into your draw deck.

This should put it on roughly the same power level as all of the other Omens and since minions are usually killed in the Archery or Skirmish phases, it leaves the free people player with an opportunity to attack it in the maneuver phase, like all of the other Omens.


# The Free Peoples Pyramid

Rohan/Gondor has been the predominant Free Peoples side in PC-Movie, and this is largely on the back of the Beacon pyramid.  We are thus nerfing a couple pieces to make it less consistent.

## War-beacon

The bottom of the pyramid, [[War-beacon]]'s ability to filter the top of the deck is being slightly reduced, with the total number of cards revealed being reduced from 5 to 4.  We may further reduce this to 3 in the future depending on how this goes.

## Gondor Calls for Aid!

The capstone of the beacon pyramid, [[Gondor Calls for Aid!]] is having its ability changed from Maneuver to Fellowship.  Besides ensuring that the twilight spent is more available for Shadow to utilize, it also cancels certain abuses such as repeatedly recycling [[Horn of Boromir]] to pump Tom Bombadil up +9 or more.


# Ritual Oath of Enmity
A site control effect for flood insurance is inherently tricky, given that sites cannot be controlled until neither player is there. We went through numerous iterations of this card before landing on it being an artifact with a two step approach that capitalised on early flooding for the sake controlling sites later.

What has emerged with this card however is that the mid-game dynamic is dramatically easier to pull off than the other flood insurance cards.

All of the others still trigger off of 18 twilight in region two, but require the card to be in hand, where Ritual Oath sits in the support area permanently, waiting for the twilight. The net effect of this is that against anything but site control, the free peoples player really only has to be worried about flood insurance in the moves from 1 to 2 and 2 to 3. Thereafter it's a long shot. But for a site control deck the very real threat of 18 twilight remains all the way through site 6, and except for in a very lean deck, 18 twilight is all but guaranteed in the move from 4 to 5. This also allows shadow players to sit back and constantly dare the free people player to go again all the way to site 6. This is particularly brutal in Dunland who can so easily recur Elders with [[Constantly Threatening]] or [[Ready to Fall]]: the shadow player sits back, playing their cheap conditions and [[Hides]] while freely discarding their minions until the FP floods or double moves. This also gives that player a significant Free People advantage, they can run ahead or move slowly knowing their opponent isn't likely to double move.

To remedy this, the requirement for getting the token in region 2 is being raised to (25) twilight. This should put it back on par with the other flood control devices and require Dunland to work a little harder during the midgame.

# Final Gollum Doom Strike

There was an unforeseen interaction between [[Final Strike]], [[Doom Causeway]], and [[Gollum, Dark as Darkness]],  which resulted in a guaranteed kill of the Ring-bearer if they enter Doom Causeway with odd numbered vitality (unless they are using Isildur's Bane as their ring).  Play Gollum, use his ability to exert the Ring-bearer, follow up the exertion with a wound from Causeway, Gollum self-wounds twice from Causeway and dies, use Final Strike to pull him back out, wash, rinse, repeat.

To halt this degenerate loop, the play Gollum ability on Final Strike is now restricted to once per phase, which shouldn't affect any legitimate uses and only affect this interaction.
