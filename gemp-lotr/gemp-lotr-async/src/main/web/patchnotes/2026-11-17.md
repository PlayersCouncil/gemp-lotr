### Game Changes

- **New Feature**: When you are offered a card or ability that would do nothing (it has nothing to act on, it has reached its limit, or it is a skirmish action that reaches nobody in the current skirmish), it now has a pale yellow double border instead of the usual blue one. You can still use it; the border only tells you it will not change anything.
*Example*: While another companion is skirmishing, [[Gimli, Son of Gloin]]'s own ability highlights in yellow, as he is not in the skirmish and making him strength +2 is unhelpful.
*Example*: While a non-Hobbit is skirmishing, [[Hobbit Stealth]] shows in yellow as there is no currently active skirmish that it would cancel/pump.
*Example*: [[Gwemegil]] shows in yellow when Arwen is not skirmishing (but of course it can still be used anyway to force a discard if the owner so chooses).
- **New Feature**: After the Ring-bearer is killed, the One Ring now stays on the board marking where they once were. This more starkly highlights the Shadow victory (and more easily prompts the user to make Sam go pick it up, if available).
- **New Feature**: 2-player infinite loop support.  When both players keep using the same two abilities against each other and nothing on the board changes (such as [[Sméagol, Slinker]] skirmishing at [[7_341|Anduin Banks]]), the game now stops the loop as the Comprehensive Rules describe: the Free Peoples player names how many more times it will run, and the Shadow player agrees or chooses a lower number. The game then carries the loop out at once, showing only its last one or two actions, and the two abilities cannot be used again for the rest of that phase or skirmish.
- **New Feature**: 1-player infinite loop support.  When one player keeps using the same ability (or the same two abilities in turn) in his own phase and nothing on the board changes (such as [[1_310|Sam, Faithful Companion]] with no Bill the Pony to play, or two Hobbit Party Guests healing each other), the game lets him do it three times and then asks how many more times to repeat it. It carries those out at once, showing only the last, and the ability cannot be used again for the rest of that phase.

- The right-click card readout now has a card's "Affecting card" list naming the card that each strength, vitality, resistance or site number bonus printed on a borne card comes from ("Glamdring: Strength +2", "Narya: Vitality +1") instead of "System"; it also shows a vitality bonus as a number, and no longer lists a +0 for a borne card that adds nothing.
- The right-click card readout no longer lists a token with a count of 0, such as 'WOUND: 0' on a character whose wounds have all been healed.
- You are no longer asked a question whose answer cannot matter: a number when only one number can be chosen, a choice between options none of which could do anything, or whether to pay to prevent something that would not happen (such as a draw from an empty draw deck or past the Rule of 4).
- When a companion or ally is killed with no threats on the dead pile, you are no longer asked to put an empty, unlabelled entry in order among the required responses. With threats, that entry now reads "Assign threat wounds".
- Revealing a random card from a player's hand no longer shows that player a pop-up of their own card to dismiss.
- Playing a minion from your discard pile by its own text (such as Gollum, Plotting Deceiver) no longer counts as a special ability, so cards like [[Gandalf, Lathspell]] can no longer answer it.
- The deck builder now lists each site that is not from a legal set on a line of its own.
- A card already in play now counts its printed twilight cost when another card reads it (such as [[Tormented Revenant]]); cost changes apply only while a card is being played as per the rules.
- The Shadow strength total on a skirmish now highlights in red if the Shadow side will be overwhelmed, mirroring how it turns red on the Free Peoples side already.
- Overwhelming is now decided minion by minion: a minion protected from being overwhelmed (such as by [[Isengard Grunt]]) is spared by its own strength alone, and the other minions in its skirmish can still be overwhelmed.
- The Hobbit Draft Game cards now show their printed game text and lore, and their titles, subtitles, twilight costs and uniqueness now match the printed cards (for example, [[31_43|Skillful Negotiator]] is no longer spelled Negociator, [[30_62|Old Forest Road]] now has a shadow number of 6, and [[33_29|Ancient Grudge]] is now unique).
- When a card must exert a character twice to pay its cost (such as [[Spied From Above]]), only characters who can exert twice are offered.
- A card discarded as it comes into play (such as [[Reaching Tentacle]] away from a marsh) no longer sets off its own text or "each time you play" texts on other cards.
- Cards that play an event from your discard pile, a stack, or your draw deck no longer offer response events, which could crash the game.
- Cards dealt with together by one effect (such as "wound each minion") are now always taken in the order they came into play, not a different order each game.
- A choice of cards from a discard pile now shows the card's own instructions where it has them, and the game log names the player who chose.
- An event printed with several phases (such as [[Sword-wall]]) now counts only as an event of the phase it is played in, so it no longer sets off "each time you play a skirmish event" in the regroup phase.
- A game no longer runs an end of turn before the first turn, so nobody is asked questions before play begins and cards in a starting fellowship no longer leave play then.
- An ally that a card bars from archery fire or skirmishes (such as a [rohan] ally at [[Streets of Edoras]]) can no longer be made to take part by another card.
- A possession that cannot be transferred (such as one borne by [[Warrior of Rohan]]) is no longer offered by cards that transfer, only to go nowhere.
- An action that has a minion assign itself to a character is no longer offered when that character already has a minion, whatever his defender bonus.
- A minion that is killed no longer counts as discarded for cards that answer a discard (such as [[Wildman's Oath]]), as the rules have it.
- A Free Peoples event that needs initiative to be played no longer counts itself among the cards in your hand.
- When a site is replaced, cards stacked or played on it now move to the new site, and the old site no longer stays under a player's control.
- When one card can answer the same moment more than once (such as [[This is My Hour]] after two Nazgul win one skirmish), each offer now says which card it is for.
- Once you have drawn 4 cards in your fellowship phase (the Rule of 4), or while a card bars you from drawing, a free optional draw (such as [[Old Toby]]'s) is no longer offered, since it could draw nothing.
- When the fellowship moves, "each time the fellowship moves" texts are now carried out before it reaches the next site, as the rules order a move, so the site being left still counts as the fellowship's site (for example, [[Race Text 92_18]] now discards 1 companion, not 2, on the move from site 6).
- A card's own "when this is discarded" text now goes off whenever it is discarded from play, including the discard of minions at the end of the turn (such as [[Orc Footman]]).
- Cards that reach "support cards" now also reach the cards borne by a card in a support area, such as a condition on an ally.
- When a card reveals cards from a hand and there are none to show (such as [[Foul Creation]] against an empty hand), no pop-up is shown; the game log says so instead.
- When a card lets you choose between two things to do and you choose to wound a character twice, you may now pick only a character able to take both wounds (such as with [[Here Lies Balin, Son of Fundin]]).
- A response (such as [[Storied Homestead]]) is no longer offered against a special ability or event that has already been cancelled.
- A draw deck searched to play a card as the cost of an event or ability (such as [[Captured by the Ring]]) is now reshuffled when no card is played from it, as it already was when the play is the effect.
- A wound that the Ring-bearer's One Ring (or any other card) turns into burdens or something else "instead" now counts as paid: when such a wound is the price of an action or of preventing something, what you paid for now happens, as when Faramir, Bearer of Quality wears The One Ring.
- When a card makes the Free Peoples player assign a minion and leaves him only one way to do it, that assignment now counts as the Shadow player's: cards and keywords that answer the Free Peoples player assigning, such as [[Wold of Rohan]] and ambush, no longer answer it. Example: [[Barbaric Uruk]] made to skirmish the only unwounded companion no longer adds (1) at [[Wold of Rohan]].
- When a skirmish ends because the last character on one side was removed from it, you are no longer asked to put an empty "Resolve skirmish damage" entry in order beside the cards that answer the win or the loss.

### Hinder Fixes
- Fixed fellowships with no unhindered companions being able to choose to move again in the regroup phase.
- Maps and Race to Mount Doom meta-sites can no longer be hindered.
- Choosing a card to restore now says "Choose cards to restore", not "Choose cards to hinder".
- A card hindered while its ability is being carried out now finishes that ability, even when it names its bearer (such as [[Gift of the Evenstar]] hindered by [[Pass of the Spider]]).
- When another player's card hinders or discards one of your cards, that card's own "when this is hindered" or "discarded" text is now yours to carry out.
- A card that hinders itself to pay for its text is still bound by "cannot" effects (such as [[Cover of Darkness, Omen of Dread]] adding a burden while the Ring-bearer is hindered).
- A hindered Ring-bearer can no longer be discarded or returned to hand, and The One Ring can no longer be discarded, even while hindered (such as at [[Doom Causeway]]).
- When you reconcile, your hindered cards are now restored before you discard, not after.
- A token is no longer added to a card that has been hindered, even by that card's own ability when it was hindered in response (such as by [[Great Gate of Minas Tirith]]).
- A card borne by a hindered character no longer counts as borne by a companion, a Hobbit or any other kind of character (such as for [[Seven We Had]] or [[Gorgoroth Wastes]]); cards borne by a hindered Ring-bearer are still his.


### Card Fix Summary

- Fixed 295 cards offering an ability or trigger, or asking a question, that would do nothing. Example: [[Aragorn, King in Exile]] no longer asks you to heal if there are no wounded companions with the Aragorn signet.
- Fixed 20 cards treating an ally as standing where the fellowship is, rather than at his home site, for a bonus or effect that depends on the kind of site he is at. Example: [[Swift Steed]] now limits an ally's wounds only while his home site is a plains.
- Fixed 19 cards making you take a card once you looked through your draw deck; you may now come up empty as per the rules. Example: [[To Honour That Allegiance]] no longer makes you take a card once you look through your draw deck.
- Fixed 19 cards offering an action or a choice when its cost could not be paid in full. Example: [[To Highest Peak]] no longer asks the Free Peoples player to prevent it when fewer than X companions can exert.
- Fixed 18 cards offering to play a card from outside your hand when you could not pay for everything. Example: [[Horsemen Took Your Lands]] can no longer be played when you cannot pay for both the event and the minion.
- Fixed 16 cards that did not let you decline a "you may". Example: [[Stewards' Legacy]] now asks whether you want to exert a minion.
- Fixed 16 cards not reaching or counting hindered cards. Example: [[Lying Counsel]] now counts and returns hindered cards borne by the companion.
- Fixed 14 cards that could not be played or used when they would do nothing; you may now play them anyway, as with any card. Example: [[Breaching Shot]] can now be played when no Uruk-hai can be assigned.
- Fixed 13 cards letting you play from your discard pile a card you had just discarded from hand to pay for it. Example: [[Days Growing Dark]] can no longer play a Sméagol you discarded to pay for it.
- Fixed 11 cards not shuffling a draw deck after it was looked through or shuffled into. Example: [[Flotsam]] now shuffles your draw deck even when you shuffle no cards into it.
- Fixed 10 cards giving more than their limit when used more than once. Example: [[Sting, Sharp Elven-blade]] no longer gives more than +6 strength in a phase.
- Fixed 9 cards whose characters came into play fresh and were exhausted only after their play had been answered; they now come into play exhausted. Example: [[Goblin Domain]] now puts a companion played at site 4 or higher into play already exhausted, so [[Out of Sight and Shot]] can no longer make him exert.
- Fixed 8 cards making you choose an exact number where the card says "up to" or "any number". Example: [[Erkenbrand, Kinsman of the House of Eorl]] now lets you remove fewer than 3 companions from the game.
- Fixed 7 cards offering their choice or action to the wrong player. Example: [[Race Text 91_10]] is no longer offered to the player who does not hold it.
- Fixed 7 cards reaching an ally where the card says a companion. Example: [[Savage Fury]] no longer hinders cards borne by an ally.
- Fixed 7 cards giving their "first ... played" bonus to a later card as well. Example: [[Morannon]] no longer discounts a second [sauron] Orc played at site 4 in a later phase of the same turn.
- Fixed 7 cards that could be played by exerting nobody where the card says to exert X characters. Example: [[Let Folly Be Our Cloak]] can no longer be played without exerting a companion.
