- Massive overhaul of the tournament system back-end.  Too many changes to list.
- Pending and active tournaments now have a clickable number where it shows how many people are in the queue.  That will bring up a pop-up showing the names of all participating players, so people can confirm whether they are in or not.
- Added ability for tournaments to require manual kickoff by an administrator.  Such tournaments can have last-minute changes performed to the roster before starting.
- Added ability for admins to force active and pending tournaments to refresh their data from the database.  This means that admins can now add or remove players to tournaments and/or make them abandon or revoke an abandon.  Each change and refresh will restart the 2 minute timer between rounds (which is the only time changes can safely be made, besides the Pending Kickoff phase).
- Added ability to foil V1 cards in the merchant
- Card definition reloads are now faster
