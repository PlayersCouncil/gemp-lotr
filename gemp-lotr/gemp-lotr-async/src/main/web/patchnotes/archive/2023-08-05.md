date: 2023-08-05

- Fixed Sealed formats choking on foil cards of alias cards (cards that are carbon copies of cards from earlier sets, such as Hobbit Sword or Elven Bow).  You should now be able to include those cards in your sealed deck with no validation errors.
- Fixed a Sealed bug which permitted decks to use twice as many copies of a card as the player actually had.  Thanks to Aaron_Brutcher for the discreet bug report.
