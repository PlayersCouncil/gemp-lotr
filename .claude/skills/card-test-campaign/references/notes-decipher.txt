NOTES FOR THE DECIPHER SETS (begun 2026-10-10, before the phase: things earlier work left for it. The orchestrator
adds to this file; a batch's `notes` argument points its agents here.)

THE DECIPHER CARDS: WHAT A BATCH'S FILES HOLD (queue-decipher-existing.json, worked out; queue-decipher-3k.json)
Ketura, 2026-10-10: "After the at suite conversion has been completed, resume upgrading the existing non-stat
tests, which I believe now only has about 150ish remaining." The first queue held the Decipher cards of sets 1 to
19 whose files held more than a stat check (509 tests between them), a commented-out play draft counted as more:
some of them, and every card of queue-decipher-3k.json that batches now come from, have only the generated stats
test and a commented-out placeholder, or that and a lone test or two (below). What follows holds for all of them:
a stub has no behavioural test to account for, and the erratum's file is still where stage 2 starts.
- The text is the card's row of `gen_en_standard.tsv` (reviewed; Decipher's own errata are in it). The packet
  prints it against the definition.
- Every existing test is accounted for in stage 2, as the brief says: it claims a check, or is redundant, or knows
  something your ledger missed, or contradicts it (then it is a question: an old test often encodes a ruling).
  Many of these tests were written before the rig had its present methods: bring each to the Standard's form
  (the rig's own methods, a reading of what the game is waiting for, real cards on a board a game could reach),
  keeping what it shows.
- Many of these cards have a PC ERRATUM that is landed already (51_x for 1_x, 54_x for 4_x and so on). Stage 1 is
  blind as always: the erratum's test file and ledger stay shut until stage 2, as your own card's files do. In
  stage 2 read the erratum's landed test file and its ledger
  (`/mnt/user-data/outputs/card-tests/ledgers/<set>/<id>.txt`: `ledgers/51/51_11.txt` for the erratum of 1_11,
  `ledgers/1/1_11.txt` for the original): where the original has the same sentence, the rulings, the
  boards and the helper cards are settled there, and a finding the erratum's batch fixed "in the original too" is
  already in your card's definition (`packet.py` prints the text a definition had before a campaign commit).
  Where the two differ, the difference is the erratum's and not a finding on your card.
  In stage 2, START the test file from the erratum's (Ketura, 2026-10-12: "refer to the tests for those errata,
  since in most cases the functionality is very close, and so can be a quick copy-paste with a few adjustments"):
  copy its tests, change the ids, aliases and helpers to your card's, and change what the original's text makes
  different; then check each against YOUR stage-1 ledger, which was written blind and stays the judge of what is
  owed. A test copied over that your text does not support is dropped, not kept.
  A "cost paid for nothing" test can turn round: where the erratum TAKES a card into hand that the original
  PLAYS from the discard pile, the original's play is itself a cost (Q-0062) and its ability is not offered with
  nothing there to play (68_12's WithNoElvenConditionInTheDiscardPileTheTokenIsRemovedForNothing became 18_12's
  WithNoElvenConditionInTheDiscardPileTheRegroupAbilityIsNotOffered).
  A reading Q-0279: E2 now forbids (the pool after a card the event plays from a pile, a plain cost) is not
  copied, though an erratum's file may still have it (landed before E2: `helper-pool` is left off landed files);
  the claim it carried goes to another test of the clause that shows it some other way, or the
  check gets a `no` line saying why none can: 4_289's R2 + ("You must still pay any costs and meet
  requirements") moved from the pool after each play onto the played possession's own bearer requirement (Frodo
  not offered as the Sword's bearer).
  Check each copied board against the original's own numbers as well as its words (burdens, resistance, vitality:
  59_31's fierce-round board corrupts or kills 9_31's Boromir, of 3 burdens and resistance 6), and where the
  erratum's test gives a keyword or a number by hand (fierce, a strength), take the printed helper `helpers.md` names
  instead (Uruk Pursuer 4_190 for a fierce minion; Morgul Whelp 7_202, strength 3 while the Ring-bearer has no wound,
  for a weak one, as Card_18_018_Tests lays them; the Standard's "A board a game could reach").
  An erratum's board is settled only for the zones the ERRATUM's text reads: where the original's text reads
  another zone (a draw deck, a discard pile), lay that zone yourself; the erratum's scenario may leave it empty.
  Where the erratum's ledger says on its Sources line that it is a new text sharing no sentence with the
  original (59_46 for 9_46), no clause, check or board carries over; its test file is still worth opening for how
  it lays a table the original needs too (a site put on the path, `MoveCardToAdventurePath` of the Shadow player's
  copy: 54_355 for 4_355) and for its helpers, which are chosen afresh by their definitions as any helper is. Where the texts differ and
  the Sources line does not say so (51_361 counts companions where 1_361 counts minions), compare them yourself:
  a sentence the two share carries its boards and helpers over; one of the erratum's own carries no clause, though
  its board may serve as a start.
- Hindering: NO hindered candidate is owed on a Decipher card, whether its text says "spot", "you can spot" or
  counts. No Decipher text speaks of hinder; that a hindered card cannot be spotted is shown once, in
  `mechanics/hinder/HinderedCardCannotBeSpottedTests`, and laying it again on every card that spots is the kind of
  test Ketura ruled out under Q-0213 ("a waste of time to continue searching for *theoretical* malformed targets
  in places and arrangements where they have been historically sparse"). The Notes of the ledger say so in a
  line. (Q-0237, kept by her, 2026-10-11: "That is already covered by the generic hinder tests, and such classes
  of mechanical interaction can stand to keep to mechanical tests instead of incessantly checking them on all
  cards.") That includes the hindered Ring-bearer as the `C-` of an "add a burden" cost: the Standard's line on
  that board (103_115) is for the V-sets' cards. A rules example that names the card ITSELF is still owed as an
  R clause though it hinders (Gwemegil 1_47 in CR 5.0 "hinder"; the Standard's R-clause line).
- A site's BLOCK is the same: a text that says "a site" or "each site" with no block named owes no second
  scenario on another block's path (also Q-0237).
- A card that SWITCHES something on other cards (The White Council 101_18 on Saruman's first sentence) is tested
  with ONE card that answers the switch, and each card that answers a switch shows in its OWN tests that it does
  (Q-0236, 2026-10-11: "we only need to ensure that the *outgoing* mechanism has been enabled, and it's up to
  each of those individual cards to check their side responds to the *incoming* mechanism"). So a Saruman whose
  first sentence can be switched off (3_68, 4_173, 5_56, 4_33, 17_37) owes a test of it, under The White
  Council 101_18 or Saruman's Staff 4_174, in his own file; and a test of the switching card lays one of them.
  The model is `Card_03_069_Tests.WithHisFirstSentenceMadeNotToApplyBySarumansStaffHeTakesAnArcheryWoundAndIsAssignedToASkirmish`
  (under the Staff, the smaller board; 3_69, 53_68, 101_30 and 102_29 have it since engine pass 26): shapes.md,
  "A ruling that a switching card has NO effect", says how it is laid and what its ledger clause is.
- No more copies of a title in play than its dots allow, counted by title whatever the subtitle, hindered
  copies included (Q-0239, 2026-10-11: "no cheating in more copies of a card than their uniqueness level
  allows"): one of a unique title,
  two of a two-dot title. A board that needs more is not a board; say so in the ledger's Notes.
- No invented card. Nothing is added to any definition file, in the cards or in the tests, for a test (Q-0241,
  2026-10-11), and no definition text is stapled onto a card to stand in for one. A card test uses printed cards;
  if no printed card can make the board, the test is not written and the Notes say what could not be shown. A
  test shows what a card that exists does ("we're trying to make the cards robust, not *necessarily* the design
  language they're built in"): a definition word your card does not use is not your card's to test.

WHAT IS ALREADY IN A DECIPHER CARD'S FILE
- The conversion of the old `at` tests put LONE TESTS into many Decipher cards' files (a test of an interaction,
  written to the Standard, with no ledger). The record is in the campaign's STATE, not the working tree:
  `/mnt/user-data/outputs/card-tests/orchestrator/at-convert/<batch>.md` names each batch's homes and tests
  (`grep -l "Card_<set>_<number>_Tests"` there finds your card's). A lone test may also be older than that
  conversion and in no list (Card_01_009_Tests has two): it is told by its form and its comment. They are existing
  tests: account for each in stage 2 as the brief says (it claims a check, or it is a clause of its own, usually
  `Interaction`), keep it active, and do not rewrite one that passes the conformance check. Renaming an alias
  `card` to the name lint asks for is no rewrite; moving the test onto the card's merged scenario is one (the
  decks under it change): it keeps its own Get...Scenario().
- A rule shown in a `mechanics/` class is not shown again card by card unless the Standard asks for it on the card
  (`ls mechanics/`; the class comment says what each is about).

CARDS THAT SHARE A TEXT (Q-0220: H)
- 153 Decipher cards in 65 groups have the same game text but for names and cultures (Desert Soldier 4_220 and
  its four kin are the largest group). The queue keeps a group in one batch where every card of it is in the
  queue; a card of a group that is in no queue (Morgul Tormentor 12_90, the twin of Orc Tormentor 12_98, line for
  line the same definition) is worked from the first's ledger and tests when its set comes up. And
  `python3 .claude/skills/card-test-campaign/scripts/orchestrator/twins.py <your ids>` prints the group of each of
  your cards that has one, with the status of any card of it that is worked already. The SECOND card of a group
  starts from the FIRST's ledger: its clauses, checks, boards and Notes with the names, cultures and ids changed,
  and its tests from the first's in the same way. The second's own definition stays shut in stage 1 and is read in
  stage 2 as any card's: twins by text are not always built alike (one may write `memorize` where its twin writes
  `memorizeActive`), and where two behave differently and their texts do not differ, one of them is
  a finding.

WHAT THE EARLIER PHASES GOT WRONG MORE THAN ONCE
- Read every helper card's definition before resting a board on it (`packet.py --defs <ids>`); comments in older
  test files name helpers wrongly in places.
- Where a player is read as NOT offered an ability that has a cost, he must be able to PAY that cost on that
  board, or the reading shows nothing.
- A phase action whose text has no "your" is open to both players on purpose where an older test asserts it; that
  is no finding (`lookup.py F-0312`).
- A candidate left out because its own text keeps it off the board, when another card lifts that. Set 4, the
  [dunland] cards ("a [dunland] Man wins / loses / is in a skirmish"): the printed [dunland] minion who is no Man
  IS on the board: Saruman, Rabble-rouser 4_33, whose "may not be assigned to a skirmish" Saruman's
  Staff 4_174 lifts (`lookup.py 4_33 --in helpers`). 4_32 and 4_34 have him
  (IsNotOfferedWhenADunlandMinionWhoIsNoManWins, IsNotOfferedWhenTheCompanionLosesToADunlandMinionWhoIsNoMan), and
  each of those tests is the ONLY one that notices `man` taken out of its definition. A Dunland card with those
  words owes the same board.
- An optional trigger or ability with no cost that is offered when it would do nothing was the commonest finding
  there was (Q-0034; about one card in eleven). Since engine pass 26 the game withholds most such offers itself
  (Q-0224; shapes.md, "An optional trigger or ability that costs nothing", lists the kinds of effect it can ask):
  lay the board, test that the offer is NOT made, file nothing and write no requirement. Only where the offer is
  still made there is it a finding (name the kind of effect), corrected by a requirement as before. The exceptions
  stand: an optional DRAW is still offered with an empty draw deck (Q-0029), an action with a cost is offered, and
  flagged where the engine can tell its effect will do nothing, and a "you may" INSIDE an event's or ability's
  text is not reached by the engine (F-0379: by hand).

WHAT WILL BE RAISED OTHERWISE AND IS NO FAULT (Q-0220: J; each line ends with the number that settles it. Test the
game as built and file nothing.)
- A RESPONSE EVENT of set 7 or later has no "Response:" in its text: from set 7 the word is printed in the type
  line (the sheet's `card_subtype: Response`), and `responseEvent` in the definition is right. Sets 1 to 6 print
  it in the text. (No ruling was wanted: Q-0220: J.)
- "Draws a card (or takes a card into hand)" (Verily I Come 2_94, Betrayal of Isengard 3_29 and 12_25) is ONE
  trigger, `playerDrawsCard` with `orTakesIntoHand: true`: no second trigger is owed for the take. "Draws a card"
  alone has no such field and answers a draw only. (Q-0205, F-0376; mechanics/deck/DrawnOrTakenIntoHandTests.)
- "Make each ... until" on an EVENT is a snapshot, a site's Shadow number included: Deft in Their Movements 2_99
  ("each site's Shadow number -2") is a `memorizeActive` of `site` with the `modifyCost` over the memory, and a
  site laid after it keeps its number (CR 4.2 "event" names the card; F-0555). Not where it changes the twilight
  COST of cards not yet played: a card in play costs its printed number (CR 4.2 "twilight cost", F-0109), so a
  snapshot would do nothing, and We Must Go Warily 3_48 ("each minion's twilight cost +1") is rightly a standing
  `modifyCost` over `minion`, as Rohirrim Road 7_332 is as an ability. (F-0217 is on abilities; Q-0011.)
- "Wears The One Ring until the regroup phase" (every One Ring, Saruman's Reach 1_137, Resistance Becomes
  Unbearable 2_79, Narchost 7_362) has no `until:` in the definition: `putOnRing` takes none, and the game takes
  the Ring off itself as the regroup phase starts, or as it ends where the Ring was put on in it.
  (mechanics/ring/WearingTheOneRingTests, Q-0220: C.)
- A `your` that the text has and the definition's filter lacks is no finding where the other player's cards of
  that side cannot be active: "your Uruk-hai wins a skirmish" (Uruk-hai Rampage 1_159), "each of your companions"
  (Gildor Inglorion 14_4) and the like, 20 definitions. No board tells the two apart. It IS one where the filter
  reaches a card that is not in play: "your [isengard] mounts are twilight cost -1" was read off a mount in the
  other player's hand (Strength of the Pack 102_30, F-0065).
- F-0444, CORRECTED (engine pass 26, item engine, 2026-10-11): a killed minion does not set off another card's
  "each time a minion is discarded" (CR 4.2 "killed"); Last Days 12_71 and Throne of Minas Tirith 17_36, whose
  texts say "killed or discarded", answer both by two triggers. Test a "discarded" trigger with a discard and
  with a kill that does not set it off; a test parked on F-0444 in a card's file goes live.
- F-0424, CORRECTED (engine pass 28, item stacked, 2026-10-11; Ketura: "Let's do B for now"): "a card stacked on
  a [dwarven] condition" (Axe of Erebor 4_41, More to My Liking 5_9) is one stacked on the player's own, active
  condition; a card stacked on the other player's condition neither pays the cost nor is offered. No `your` goes
  into a definition for it. Lay the other player's condition with a card stacked on it, as shapes.md says ("A
  card stacked on a [culture] condition"), and assert the ability not offered: nothing is parked on F-0424 now.
- `shuffle: false` on a take, a discard or a putting back of cards that a reveal or a look at the top of a draw
  deck has just named (`select` on a `memory(...)`) is right: that is no search of the deck, and only a look
  through the whole of it is followed by a shuffle. (Q-0013, F-0034.)
