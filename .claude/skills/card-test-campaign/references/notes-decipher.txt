NOTES FOR THE DECIPHER SETS (begun 2026-10-10, before the phase: things earlier work left for it. The orchestrator
adds to this file; a batch's `notes` argument points its agents here.)

THE FIRST DECIPHER CARDS: THE 152 THAT ALREADY HAD BEHAVIOURAL TESTS (queue-decipher-existing.json)
Ketura, 2026-10-10: "After the at suite conversion has been completed, resume upgrading the existing non-stat
tests, which I believe now only has about 150ish remaining." These are Decipher cards of sets 1 to 19 whose files
held more than a stat check before the campaign (509 tests between them, most files fewer than five).
- The text is the card's row of `gen_en_standard.tsv` (reviewed; Decipher's own errata are in it). The packet
  prints it against the definition.
- Every existing test is accounted for in stage 2, as the brief says: it claims a check, or is redundant, or knows
  something your ledger missed, or contradicts it (then it is a question: an old test often encodes a ruling).
  Many of these tests were written before the rig had its present methods: bring each to the Standard's form
  (the rig's own methods, a reading of what the game is waiting for, real cards on a board a game could reach),
  keeping what it shows.
- Many of these cards have a PC ERRATUM that is landed already (51_x for 1_x, 54_x for 4_x and so on). Stage 1 is
  blind as always. In stage 2 read the erratum's landed test file and its ledger
  (`/mnt/user-data/outputs/card-tests/ledgers/<set>/<id>.txt`: `ledgers/51/51_11.txt` for the erratum of 1_11,
  `ledgers/1/1_11.txt` for the original): where the original has the same sentence, the rulings, the
  boards and the helper cards are settled there, and a finding the erratum's batch fixed "in the original too" is
  already in your card's definition (`packet.py` prints the text a definition had before a campaign commit).
  Where the two differ, the difference is the erratum's and not a finding on your card.
  An erratum's board is settled only for the zones the ERRATUM's text reads: where the original's text reads
  another zone (a draw deck, a discard pile), lay that zone yourself; the erratum's scenario may leave it empty.
- Hindering: NO hindered candidate is owed on a Decipher card, whether its text says "spot", "you can spot" or
  counts. No Decipher text speaks of hinder; that a hindered card cannot be spotted is shown once, in
  `mechanics/HinderedCardCannotBeSpottedTests`, and laying it again on every card that spots is the kind of
  test Ketura ruled out under Q-0213 ("a waste of time to continue searching for *theoretical* malformed targets
  in places and arrangements where they have been historically sparse"). The Notes of the ledger say so in a
  line. (Q-0237, kept by her, 2026-10-11: "That is already covered by the generic hinder tests, and such classes
  of mechanical interaction can stand to keep to mechanical tests instead of incessantly checking them on all
  cards.")
- A site's BLOCK is the same: a text that says "a site" or "each site" with no block named owes no second
  scenario on another block's path (also Q-0237).
- A card that SWITCHES something on other cards (The White Council 101_18 on Saruman's first sentence) is tested
  with ONE card that answers the switch, and each card that answers a switch shows in its OWN tests that it does
  (Q-0236, 2026-10-11: "we only need to ensure that the *outgoing* mechanism has been enabled, and it's up to
  each of those individual cards to check their side responds to the *incoming* mechanism"). So a Saruman whose
  first sentence can be switched off (3_68, 3_69, 4_173, 5_56, 4_33, 17_37) owes a test of it, under The White
  Council 101_18 or Saruman's Staff 4_174, in his own file; and a test of the switching card lays one of them.
- No more copies of a title in play than its dots allow, counted by title whatever the subtitle (Q-0239,
  2026-10-11: "no cheating in more copies of a card than their uniqueness level allows"): one of a unique title,
  two of a two-dot title. A board that needs more is not a board; say so in the ledger's Notes.
- No invented card. Nothing is added to `testCards.hjson` or to any definition file for a test (Q-0241,
  2026-10-11). A card test uses printed cards; if no printed card can make the board, the test is not written and
  the Notes say what could not be shown.

WHAT IS ALREADY IN A DECIPHER CARD'S FILE
- The conversion of the old `at` tests put LONE TESTS into many Decipher cards' files (a test of an interaction,
  written to the Standard, with no ledger). The record is in the campaign's STATE, not the working tree:
  `/mnt/user-data/outputs/card-tests/orchestrator/at-convert/<batch>.md` names each batch's homes and tests
  (`grep -l "Card_<set>_<number>_Tests"` there finds your card's). A lone test may also be older than that
  conversion and in no list (Card_01_009_Tests has two): it is told by its form and its comment. They are existing
  tests: account for each in stage 2 as the brief says (it claims a check, or it is a clause of its own, usually
  `Interaction`), keep it active, and do not rewrite one that passes the conformance check.
- A rule shown in a `mechanics/` class is not shown again card by card unless the Standard asks for it on the card
  (`ls mechanics/`; the class comment says what each is about).

WHAT THE EARLIER PHASES GOT WRONG MORE THAN ONCE
- Read every helper card's definition before resting a board on it (`packet.py --defs <ids>`); comments in older
  test files name helpers wrongly in places.
- Where a player is read as NOT offered an ability that has a cost, he must be able to PAY that cost on that
  board, or the reading shows nothing.
- A phase action whose text has no "your" is open to both players on purpose where an older test asserts it; that
  is no finding (`lookup.py F-0312`).
- An optional trigger or ability with no cost that is offered when it would do nothing was the commonest finding
  there was (Q-0034; about one card in eleven). Since engine pass 26 the game withholds most such offers itself
  (Q-0224; shapes.md, "An optional trigger or ability that costs nothing", lists the kinds of effect it can ask):
  lay the board, test that the offer is NOT made, file nothing and write no requirement. Only where the offer is
  still made there is it a finding (name the kind of effect), corrected by a requirement as before. The exceptions
  stand: an optional DRAW is still offered with an empty draw deck (Q-0029), an action with a cost is offered and
  flagged, and a "you may" INSIDE an event's or ability's text is not reached by the engine (F-0379: by hand).

THE COST OF ASSIGNING (set 7's Morgul Orcs; built by engine pass 26, item assigncost, on Ketura's Q-0221: A)
- Morgul Regiment 7_197, Morgul Spawn 7_200, Morgul Spearman 7_201 ("the Free Peoples player must exert a
  companion to assign this minion") and Úlairë Nertëa 69_38 are on one build, `extraCostToAssign`. Nothing is
  asked ahead: the Free Peoples player pays as he makes the assignment (at his own assigning after his answer,
  the charged ones in the order of the minions' ids; under a card that has him assign, once the pair is chosen).
  A cost not paid in full means that one assignment is not made (at his own assigning the minion is a leftover
  for the Shadow player); while the cost cannot be paid the assignment is not his to make; the Shadow player's
  assignments are never charged; a lone candidate exerts unasked and the choice of who exerts has no "none".
  The cards that have him assign (Éowyn 17_96, Alcarin 5_31, Sméagol 7_71 and nine more) can now take a Morgul
  Orc. `mechanics/CostOfAssigningTests` and `orchestrator/engine-pass-26/assigncost.md` have the whole of it.
- The three Orcs' old test files pass and read the game as built, but are NOT worked to the Standard (no ledger,
  the alias `card`, one table built inside a test): work them as any card of this phase.
- With Ketura, not to be filed again or corrected: F-0425 (Morgul Regiment is his to assign while ONE companion
  can exert, however many Nazgûl there are: test the game as built) and Q-0247 (the payment's source is the
  charging card; an assignment already paid for is not asked to pay again).
