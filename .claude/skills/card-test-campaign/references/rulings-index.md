- Reinforcing more than one token is one token per choice (F-0113, F-0620)
- "Its skirmish" on a minion is the skirmish that minion is in (F-0114, F-0321)
- "By another card" is a card other than THAT card (Q-0079, Q-0395)
- Where a "spot" stands decides effect or requirement (Q-0085, Q-0058, Q-0393; F-0022, F-0440, F-0492, F-0557, F-0599, F-0615; F-0119, F-0261, F-0711)
- A bare cost sentence with no "to", then effect sentences (Q-0058; F-0562, F-0569, F-0585, F-0591) [Q-0148]
- "Remove X ... to <do something to> a card costing X or less" (Q-0087, F-0133; F-0132)
- "The <other> player may make you <do X> to prevent this" (Q-0088)
- "A <thing>" is one: a choice of one, not all that match (F-0136)
- "Items" and "a possession or artifact" take artifacts as well as possessions (F-0045, F-0796)
- "The second time / the first X ... each Shadow phase" needs a Shadow-phase requirement (F-0130, F-0390; F-0163; F-0156, F-0751)
- "May X, and must Y for each X"; "copies of that card"; "is considered a <race>" (Q-0091, Q-0092)
- "A minion" on a Skirmish event that reaches only minions in the skirmish (Q-0095, F-0527)
- "<Exert> a <kind> to <do X to> a character he is skirmishing" on a Skirmish event (Mordor's Strength 1_255) [F-0527]
- "Make a <kind> strength +N (or +M if in a fierce skirmish)" conditions on the card chosen (F-0550, Sentinels of Númenor 2_37)
- A possession's "while bearer is skirmishing ..." filters `bearer`, never `self`: a possession is never in a skirmish (F-0713)
- "Wound X twice" written as an exertion is a definition fault (F-0148, Take it Down! 103_89)
- `count:` is how many cards, `times:` is how often one card (F-0108)
- "Make allies take wounds from archery fire instead of companions" (Q-0136, Q-0137, Q-0147, F-0243, F-0244, F-0275) [Q-0034]
- "<X> instead" replaces the effect only when X can happen; otherwise the original effect occurs (F-0537, F-0610)
