# Shapes already settled

What the campaign has learned about particular shapes of card text: what a ruling means for a definition and a test, what the engine now does, and what to read on the board. Each entry was first written in the orchestrator's notes to authors and moved here so that every phase has it. A definition that departs from an entry here is a finding (fixable at landing unless the entry says otherwise); do not raise these shapes as questions again. The words of the rulings themselves are in `rulings-log.md`; `rulings-index.md` lists both files' entries.

## How card text is read

**Any X of exertions is at least 1 (Q-0076).** Any X of exertions is at least 1: "exert X <cards>" as well as "exert X times" (Q-0076). X of zero by spotting, or of anything else, is still allowed.

**A card that makes itself a minion (Q-0077, Q-0078).** A card that makes itself a minion from the support area offers that ability from the support area only (Q-0077, the Bladetusks); its definition's requirement is right. A sentence after the ability that calls the card "she"/"it" as the minion is part of the ability (Q-0078).

**Reinforcing more than one token (F-0113).** Reinforcing more than one token is one token and one choice of card at a time (F-0113: `Repeat` round `ReinforceTokens`; `times:` on ReinforceTokens puts them all on one card and is a finding unless the text says "reinforce a token twice").

**"Its skirmish" on a minion (F-0114).** "Its skirmish" on a minion is the skirmish that minion is in; assigned and waiting, its text is not active (F-0114). A modifier counting over `InSkirmish` with only `filter: self` is a finding.

**"By another card" (Q-0079).** "By another card" in a card's text is a card other than THAT card (Q-0079, Corsair Helmsman 103_46): a [raider] minion hindering itself when played IS answered by a Helmsman.

**A spot that stands after "to" is an effect (Q-0085).** A spot that stands after "to" is an effect: the ability is offered though nothing can be spotted (Q-0085). A definition that has such a spot among its costs is a finding of its own.

**"Remove X ... to <do something to> a card costing X or less" (Q-0087, F-0133).** "Remove X ... to <do something to> a card costing X or less" has no cap on X but what there is to remove (Q-0087). A cap by "the highest cost" is a finding; ForEachTwilightCost over HighestTwilight adds up ties (F-0133).

**"The <other> player may make you <do X> to prevent this" (Q-0088).** "The <other> player may make you <do X> to prevent this": "you" chooses what X is done to (Q-0088). Built the other way is a finding.

**"A <thing>" is one (F-0136).** "A <thing>" is one: a choice of one, not all that match (F-0136).

**"The second time ... each Shadow phase" (F-0130).** "The second time ... each Shadow phase" needs a Shadow-phase requirement (F-0130): a finding where missing.

**"May X, and must Y for each X"; "copies of that card"; "is considered a <race>" (Q-0091, Q-0092).** "May X, and must Y for each X" is not a price: X is done, then Y as far as it can be (Q-0091). "Copies of that card" counts the card itself and goes by title (Q-0092). "Is considered a [culture] <race>" ADDS the race.

**"A minion" on a Skirmish event that reaches only minions in the skirmish (Q-0095).** "A minion" on a Skirmish event that reaches only minions in the skirmish: the TEXT is changed to "a skirmishing minion" (Q-0095, done on 103_84). On another card of the shape, say so as a finding of kind gametext, naming Q-0095.

**"Wound X twice" written as an exertion (F-0148).** RULED 2026-10-07 (F-0148, Take it Down! 103_89): "wound X twice" written as an exertion in a definition is a definition fault (a wound can kill and can fall on an exhausted character, an exertion cannot): fixable at landing.

**"Make allies take wounds from archery fire instead of companions" (Q-0136, Q-0137, Q-0147, F-0243, F-0244, F-0275).** Built with the one modifier `AlliesTakeArcheryFireWoundsInsteadOfCompanions` (Come Down 4_146 and 54_146), which the engine reads wound by wound: an ally while one can take it, otherwise as usual. Not with `AllyCanParticipateInArcheryFire` beside `CantTakeArcheryWounds` on companions: the first also counts an archer ally for the fellowship archery total, which "take wounds" does not say, and the second loses the wounds no ally can take. The ability has no cost, so its definition requires an ally it can send a wound to (`canSpot` with `ally,not(barredFromArcheryFire)`; Q-0034), and tests show it withheld with no ally in play and with only a barred one. An ally who "may not participate in archery fire" (Streets of Edoras 4_340, `AllyMayNotParticipateInArcheryFireOrSkirmishes`) takes none of these wounds: a bar on participating bars taking archery wounds as well as adding to the total (Q-0147, F-0275). The test of that pair of cards is an `Interaction` clause owed in 4_340's file when that card is done (Streets is the card named first), with a line in each Come Down's Notes; `mechanics/AlliesTakeArcheryWoundsTests` shows it meanwhile.

**"Participates in archery fire and skirmishes" against "may not participate" (Q-0150, F-0281).** Nothing for a definition to say: the engine answers that no card allows an ally another card bars (`AllyMayNotParticipateInArcheryFireOrSkirmishes`, Streets of Edoras 4_340 only), so `AllyCanParticipate...`, `EnableParticipation...` and an assignment with `ignoreAllyLocation` (Breaching Shot 102_21) all pass a barred ally over, for the archery total, archery wounds and assignment by either player or by an effect; the rest of the granting card (a strength bonus) still happens. Not a finding against the granting card. The test of a granting card with the Streets is the Interaction clause owed in 4_340's file (Streets is the card named first), with a line in the other card's Notes; `mechanics/AllyBarredFromTakingPartTests` shows the rule, and 53_42 and 102_21 each carry one test of it as an `R` clause, written by engine pass 18. A no-cost ability whose whole effect is to have an ally fight (Dunlending Ravager 4_15, Dunlending Warrior 4_18) is still offered with only a barred ally in play (F-0283, candidate): there is no filter word for "barred from skirmishes" yet.

**"Choose X ... (or you may play it ...)" plays the card chosen (F-0152).** RULED 2026-10-07 (F-0152, Ominous Sky 103_97, fixed): "choose X ... (or you may play it ...)" plays the card chosen; a definition that asks a second time which card is a finding, fixable at landing.

**Ruled 2026-10-08 and built by engine pass 15 (F-0197, F-0196, Q-0115).** F-0197 (Q-0113): "If a minion loses this skirmish to that <character>, <effect>" is ONE effect however many minions lose (read as "at the end of this skirmish, if at least 1 minion lost ..."); built with `limitPerPhase: 1` on the `addTrigger` (1_29, 51_29), and a definition of that shape without it is a finding. F-0196 (Q-0112): a shuffle FROM PLAY of "any number" with none chosen shuffles the deck all the same; Mumakil Commander 18_71 requires a [men] mount of his to be spotted. Assert both; park nothing on them. Q-0115: a Decipher ruling about a WORD ("free") does not reach an erratum that dropped the word.

**"While ..., the move limit is +1 (-1)" with no "for this turn" (Q-0179, open).** CR 4.2 "move limit": a change to the move limit "is in effect for the whole turn, even if the conditions for the modification change". Decipher's cards are built so whether they say "for this turn" or not, as a once-a-turn trigger that adds the modifier `until: endOfTurn` (The Number Must Be Few 1_260, Derndingle 4_330, Radagast 9_26, Théoden 13_137 "While you can spot Théodred, the move limit is +1"). Three definitions are a plain `ModifyMoveLimit` with a `requires`, which turns off and on with its condition: 51_260, by its own second sentence (Q-0122), and Race Text 91_8 and 91_12, whose texts have no such sentence; Q-0179 asks which they should be. Until it is answered the as-built reading stays active on those two (91_8: `WhenAFifthCompanionDiesTheMoveLimitIsTwoAgainAndTheFellowshipMayMoveAgain`), nothing is parked, and the doubt is a rider to Q-0179, not a new question. The amount itself is shown on a board the floor of 1 cannot hide (pitfalls, "The move limit never reads below 1").

## What is offered and what is asked

**The no-effect flag: which cards assert it.** The no-effect flag exists (gemp-testing, "The no-effect flag"; test-generation, "Rulings, interactions and formats"): `FreepsActionNoEffect(card)` / `ShadowActionNoEffect(card)` (and with words of the action's text) answer "false", "effect", "limit" or "skirmish". Do NOT assert it on every card: only where the definition declares `noEffectUnless`, or the rulings log names the card for it. A no-cost ability or optional trigger that is offered though it would do nothing is still a finding (Q-0034), flag or no flag.

**A choice whose every branch has nothing to act on (F-0116).** A choice whose every branch has nothing to act on is not put to the player (F-0116): a pointless prompt is a finding. Since engine pass 19 (F-0293) the word `choice` withholds the question itself where every branch is a single effect that can say it will do nothing as it comes up (exert, heal, wound, discard, hinder, modifyStrength, addKeyword, removeBurdens, removeThreats, addBurdens, addThreats, drawCards, returnToHand, liberateSite, takeControlOfSite and their like): such a definition needs no guard, and the test asserts that "Choose action to perform" is not asked and nothing happened. A branch of several effects, one that holds others (`if`, `costToEffect`, `multiple`, a nested `choice`) and one whose effect cannot tell (a play from a pile, `chooseActiveCards`) still ask: there the prompt is still a finding, mended in the definition by an `If` around the choice (Savage Fury 103_39).

**A parenthesis with its own price (Q-0089).** A parenthesis with its own price the player elects to pay ("(or X if you also exert ...)") is the player's choice (Q-0089). Do not ask.

**One thing left to do is done without a second question (Q-0090).** Where taking an offer leaves only one thing to do, it is done without a second question (Q-0090). A second yes/no there is a finding.

**A yes/no before a choice whose minimum is 0 (Q-0143, F-0277).** "<Player> may <do X to> any number of / up to N cards" built as a yes/no (`Optional`) and then a choice of 0 to N lets the player say no twice, and is a finding: the choice is asked alone, and its text says plainly that none need be chosen (Ketura: "let's get rid of the pop-up and just make sure the message displayed to the user is sufficiently clear"). Read the choice's minimum of 0 and, where the definition gives the choice a `text` of its own, the words that say so (`FreepsDecisionAvailable("you may choose none")`, The Palantir of Orthanc 53_67); no other wording of the game's is an expectation. Taking the `Optional` away takes its context away too: what was inside it ran as the player it named, so check afterwards who is asked each further thing (53_67's order of the cards on the deck went to the wrong player until `PutCardsFromHandOnTopOfDeck` was mended). Gwaihir 101_15 was the other such card (F-0278): `putCardsFromDiscardIntoHand` takes a `text` for it, and a choice from a discard pile is asked in a definition's own `text` only since engine pass 18 (F-0285; with none it reads "Choose card from discard"). With no card to choose from, a 0-to-N choice is not asked at all. NOT this shape: an optional trigger's own yes/no before "shuffle up to N ... into your draw deck" (Orkish Smith 61_132). Its two noes differ under Q-0102 (declined: no shuffle; taken with none chosen: the deck is shuffled), so both steps stay and no finding is raised.

**A number that can only be one number is not asked for (F-0147).** A number that can only be one number is not asked for (F-0147, in this clone): where X runs from 1 to 1, or "how many" has one answer, the game takes it. Do not answer such a question in a test (the answer would go to the NEXT decision); to read the bounds of an X, arrange a board where two numbers can be named.

**"You may make <the other player> do X" (Q-0106).** Q-0106 (ruled): "you may make the <other player> do X" is asked of "you" as a yes/no, only when X can be done, and the other player still makes the choices the text gives him. Where the game never asks, that is an error: fix or park it as a finding, not a question.

**Known shapes from the Dwarven errata (F-0181, F-0194, F-0182).** KNOWN SHAPES from the Dwarven errata, each still a finding on its card (show the correction; it is fixed at landing): (1) "the first time <X happens> each turn" built as PerTurnLimit + IncrementPerTurnLimit is counted only when the offer is TAKEN, so a declined offer comes again; the requirement `playedCardThisTurn` (filter, max 1) is the right word (F-0181). (2) A `preventable` after a choice that can come up empty was asked "to prevent NONE"; since engine pass 14 the engine itself does not ask (F-0194), so this is no longer a finding. (3) An optional trigger with no cost whose whole effect would do nothing is to be withheld by a requirement (Q-0034; F-0182); for "you may take a <card> into hand from your discard pile" the requirement is `HasCardInDiscard` with the choice's own filter (Beorning Axe 68_18 and 18_18, F-0295: taking the empty offer also spent its once per turn). For "you may transfer this <card> to a <kind>" it is `canSpot` with the kind and `not(hasAttached(self))` (Brought Down from Inside 69_24 and 19_24, F-0304: the `transfer` effect leaves the present bearer out of its choice by itself, with or without `another` in `where`, and `checkTarget` narrows the choice without gating the offer). For a "Wear The One Ring" that costs nothing it is `not` round `ringIsOn` (The Great Ring 69_1 and 19_1, F-0303); a Ring whose wearing has a cost stays on offer (Q-0034).

**"You may <do X to a card>" on a required trigger wants its choice 0 to 1 (Q-0010, F-0191).** "You may <do X to a card of a kind>" on a REQUIRED trigger wants its choice 0 to 1 (Q-0010); a choice of exactly 1 there cannot be declined and is a finding fixed at landing (F-0191). Read the choice's minimum in a test. Where the thing done is a play from hand there is no 0-to-1 choice to write: `PlayCardFromHand` takes no count and always plays exactly one, so the correction is `optional: true` on the trigger (Memories of Darkness 60_2, F-0280). `optional: true` is also the correction where the "you may" is the WHOLE of a card's standing trigger ("Each time Legolas wins a skirmish, you may heal ...", Focus 65_15, F-0289: Q-0021 and F-0041 name the optional trigger, and the Decipher original has it). The 0-to-1 choice was the correction for a trigger an event adds for its skirmish (Ancient Enmity 51_29, F-0191: "If a minion loses this skirmish to that Elf, you may heal an Elf") and for a "may" in parentheses inside a larger effect (Q-0010). Under `optional: true` the tests read the offer with `FreepsHasOptionalTriggerAvailable` and a choice of exactly 1 after it. Every test that takes or declines the offer then asserts the offer and is parked on the finding, the tests of nothing offered stay active, and lint's parked-only warnings stand until landing. These are the corrections for a build that cannot be declined: a required trigger that already has the 0-to-1 choice can be, and is no finding even where the "you may" is the whole of a standing trigger (the notes for the errata, on Q-0010).

**"When you play this ..., you may add (N)" built as a required trigger (F-0009; Q-0165, open).** Goblin Runner 1_178 adds its (2) unasked and is ruled deliberate (F-0009). Mordor Fiend 60_91 is built the same way and is asked about (Q-0165); Goblin Flankers 2_61 and Isengard Scimitar 5_54 are built `optional: true`, as their text says, and raise nothing. On another card of the kind that is built required and not yet ruled, it is a question: the active tests read the game (no offer pending after the play, the tokens already in the pool), `O` is left out of the clause's brackets and a `no O:` line names F-0009 and the question (lint waives it), and no test of the asked reading is written, since the game passes the likelier one. The same build on another trigger is carried the same way, as a question of its own that names Q-0165: Demoralized 61_114 ("Each time a companion exerts, you may add (1) ...") is the only "Each time ..., you may add (N)" card built required (Q-0167, open); 11_114, 5_73, 5_78, 6_98, 7_145 and 12_91 ask. Before a question calls such a build deliberate, read the file's history: an `optional: false` is most often the old default (a trigger with no `optional` line was required) written out by the mass edit of 2024-11-08 (7994c9ba71), as 61_114's is, and not a word somebody changed.

**A prevention is asked only when the effect will happen (F-0194, Q-0114).** BUILT in this clone (engine pass 14, F-0194, Q-0114): "<effect>. <A player> may <pay> to prevent this" is put to that player only when the effect is going to happen. Not asked: a draw from an empty deck or past the Rule of 4, nobody chosen for the effect or the one chosen out of reach, burdens that cannot be added, threats at their limit, no site to control, a skirmish that will not be cancelled. Still asked: an effect that happens in part (draw 3 with 1 card left), and an amount that comes to zero (the engine cannot see that: there an `If` in the definition is still wanted, and its absence is a finding). Where the card can reach a board on which the effect cannot happen, one test reads `assertFalse(scn.ShadowDecisionAvailable("prevent"))` and the cost unpaid (ShadowAnyDecisionsAvailable() is true anyway in phases both players act in). Do not ask for a preventable to be wrapped in If/HasCardInMemory any more: the ones so written are harmless. mechanics/PreventionIsAskedOnlyWhenItsEffectWillHappenTests has boards.

**A choice made ahead of a preventable exert loses the exert's own check (F-0206).** "Exert a companion. The Free Peoples player may ... to prevent this" built as a `chooseActiveCards` with `select: choose(companion)` followed by a preventable `exert` of the memory offers exhausted companions, and with every companion exhausted the choice is still put; the `exert` effect alone would have offered only those able to exert. Write `choose(companion,canExert)` in the choice (Uruk Soldier 51_154, fixed). A definition of that shape without it is a finding, fixable at landing: test an exhausted companion beside the valid ones, and a board on which none can exert (nothing chosen, the prevention not asked, Q-0114).

**"Spot X to <effect>" on an optional trigger or ability is no cost for Q-0034 (F-0167, F-0209, F-0211, F-0219).** A spot pays nothing, so Q-0034's exception for "situations with a cost" does not cover it: where the effect would do nothing (nothing to heal, an empty draw deck, nobody to exhaust) the offer is withheld by a requirement, and a definition without one is a finding, fixable at landing. A spot that can never fail (Elrond spotting himself as an ally of home 3) gates nothing either. Events stay offered whatever they do (Q-0034). Count every sentence of the ability before calling its effect nothing: Under Foot 60_52's reconcile can find no hand and no draw deck while the same use still sets up the condition's discard at the regroup phase, so as built it is rightly offered there (whether that sentence is the ability's is Q-0164).

**An "or" between two cost-to-effect halves (Q-0034).** An "or" between two cost-to-effect halves ("exert a Dwarf to heal an Elf, or exert an Elf to heal a Dwarf") is not CR 4.2's "choose one of two actions you are fully capable of performing": either half may be used though it will do nothing while the other could be carried out in full (Q-0034). Assert that; not a question.

**A choice of cost-to-effect branches with no payable branch is not offered (F-0222, F-0259).** An event, ability or
optional trigger whose every branch is "pay X to Y" ("Choose one: exert A to ..., or exert B to ...") is not
offered when no branch's cost can be paid. A definition without a requirement for this (an Or of the branches'
costs) is a finding, fixable at landing.

**"The Free Peoples player must <pay> to assign <this minion> ..." (F-0306, F-0307; F-0308 and Q-0174, open).** As built, a cost of assigning is a yes/no put to the Free Peoples player when he starts his own assigning (after the assignment actions are passed, and again in the fierce assignment phase), paid at the yes whether or not he then makes the assignment, with the bar standing for that phase on a no: the engine's `assignmentCost` (the [wraith] Orcs Morgul Regiment 7_197, Morgul Spawn 7_200 and Morgul Spearman 7_201; a standing bar on the minion until the cost is paid) and the hand-built trigger on `fpstartedassigning` of Ulaire Nertea 69_38. Whether paying ahead, and being asked with nobody to assign her to, is meant is Q-0174: assert it as built and do not raise it again. Where the payment is hand-built it is the `cost` of an `Optional`, not an effect behind a `chooseYesOrNo`: the question is then not put when the cost cannot be paid (the `C-` board: the Ring-bearer hindered, for a burden) and the bar stands, where the effect-built one took a yes that paid nothing and lifted the bar (F-0306, fixable at landing). A payment that is prevented (Melilot Brandybuck 3_110 on the burden) is not paid and leaves the bar (Q-0038; probed, a test owed in 3_110's file). A bar that begins only at `fpstartedassigning` is not there during the assignment actions, so a card that has the Free Peoples player assign then (Éowyn 17_96 by his own action, Shadowy Mount 12_171 made to by the Shadow player) gets her past it for nothing: a finding on such a card, and which way it is closed (a burden collected at any assignment, or a bar standing throughout as `assignmentCost` has) is F-0308, open. A `{memory}` in the question's text is filled only from the memory of the action that asks: a name another trigger stored with `storeWhileInZone` printed "NONE" (F-0307).

**"You may discard up to N (X, any number of) cards from hand to draw as many" on an optional trigger (F-0300, Robin Smallburrow 68_111 and 18_111).** The discard stands before the "to", so it is the trigger's `cost` (F-0022), and its minimum is 1: `count: 1-N`, or `count: anyNonZero` for "X cards" and "any number". With an empty hand the trigger is then not offered (an optional trigger with nothing else to pay whose X can only be zero: Q-0015 as amended by Q-0034), and once the offer is taken at least one card goes. Declining the offer is the one way to discard none, so there is no second no behind the yes (the shape of Q-0143); "up to N" alone would allow none, and the minimum of 1 rests on those rulings. Built as an effect with `count: 0-N`, or as a cost with a minimum of 0, the card is a finding fixed at landing. The tests: `C-` is nothing offered with no card in hand though the draw deck has cards; and the choice's minimum is read as 1 with two or more cards in hand (pitfall "One candidate is chosen for you"). Same shape by their definitions, not yet changed and not probed: Nenya 3_23 (`count: 0-2`) and Uruk Slaughterer 12_154 (`count: any`). NOT this shape: an ability with another cost before the "to" ("Exert Ottar to discard up to 3 cards from hand and draw an equal number of cards", 1_80: the discard is an effect of 0 to 3, and a cost keeps the ability on offer under Q-0034), and an event.

**"You may" on a Race to Mount Doom modifier names its player (F-0311).** A meta-site `Trigger` with `optional: true` and no `player:` is offered to each player in turn, so the other player gets it when the owner declines; `requires: OwnerIsFreeps` or `OwnerIsShadow` does not stop that. The definition says `player: owner` for "you may" (91_2, 91_3 and 93_3, corrected at landing) and `player: shadow` where the text gives the choice to the Shadow player (91_5, 92_33, 93_7, 94_11, as they stand); `requires: IsOwner` (94_4) also holds it to the owner. A modifier built without one of these is a finding fixed at landing with `player: owner`, not an engine fault: an owner filter on meta-sites would shut out the triggers that are the other player's. The test is the decline, with the other player read as not offered. Read and not yet changed or probed: 93_10 (no `player:`; its cost is the owner's [dunland] possession).

**A phase action on a Race to Mount Doom modifier is its owner's where the text says "your" (F-0312).** The engine offers a meta-site's `Activated` to whichever player may act in the phase, whoever owns it. Where the text says "your", the definition holds it to the owner: `requires: OwnerIsShadow` or `OwnerIsFreeps` where the timeword is one side's (91_11, 92_2, 92_4, 92_28), and `requires: { type: IsOwner }` where both players act in the phase (91_10, "Skirmish: Discard 3 cards from hand to make your companion or minion strength +1", corrected at landing; 94_4 has it too). Where the text has no "your" (92_5 "Fellowship: Exert X companions to remove (X)", 92_13, 92_14, 92_21, 92_22) the action is left open on purpose, and those cards' old tests assert that the other player may use it (`NotOwnerGated`, `OpponentCanUseAbility`, `CanUseWithOpponentsMod`, `WorksWhenOwnedByFreeps`): the old test's reading stands and it is no finding. The test of "your" is the other player read as not offered beside the owner offered, with the cost in the other player's reach (pitfalls, "A meta-site's optional trigger").

## Limits

**Limits on uses and limits on what the effect gives, as built.** LIMITS, as built in this clone since 2026-10-07 (engine pass 8; Q-0041, Q-0082, Q-0093; hjson-reference "Limits" and test-generation's L check have the full text). A limit on USES ("limit once per turn", "limit once per phase", "limit N times": fields limitPerPhase / limitPerTurn / limitPerSite) WITHHOLDS the ability at its limit, cost or no cost: the test reads it not offered, with its cost still payable and something on the board the effect could still act on. A limit on WHAT THE EFFECT GIVES ("(limit +3)", "add a threat (limit 1)", "(limit 2 per phase)" where it counts things made: fields effectLimitPerPhase / effectLimitPerTurn) leaves an ability with a cost on offer at the limit, cost paid, nothing done, flagged `limit` with FreepsActionNoEffect / ShadowActionNoEffect, and that card gets the R clause for the flag. A cap written inside the effect (a CardPhaseLimit or CardAffectedLimitPerPhase value, limitPerCardThisPhase) also leaves the ability on offer. The engine sets the `limit` word for it only where the cap is on a strength bonus, on the amount of a keyword or on a number of cards drawn, and nothing else the action does would happen either (mechanics/NoEffectFlagTests shows the first two; only 33_48 and 33_49 cap a draw); a cap on anything else is not read (Sting 51_313, a capped RemoveTwilight after a reveal, reads "false" at its cap). Either way the card asserts no flag and writes no R clause for it: that is the mechanic's (test-generation, "Rulings, interactions and formats"); test the cap itself under L. A definition whose field does not match its text's kind of limit is a finding, fixable at landing. Park nothing on Q-0093.

**"Limit once per site" (Q-0084, F-0146, F-0145).** "Limit once per site" is once for each site in a turn (Q-0084) and is the engine's since 2026-10-07: `limitPerSite: 1` on the ability or trigger (or PerSiteLimit / IncrementPerSiteLimit), counted for the site the fellowship is at, cleared when it moves and at the end of the turn; an exertion on ARRIVING belongs to the site arrived at (F-0146). It is a limit on uses: withheld at the limit. A required trigger with a limit on uses is carried out no more often than its limit even when one event raises it twice at once (two companions exerting together draw a Cirith Ungol Watcher one card, F-0145). 103_92 and 103_103 are built so. A definition still built from per-phase limits and MoveCountMinimum is a finding.

**"(limit +N)" is the card's for the whole phase (F-0135, F-0125).** "(limit +N)" is the card's for the whole phase: cumulative over uses (F-0135, a per-use cap is a finding) and counted per source card, not per target (F-0125, FIXED in this clone). A cap built as `cardAffectedLimitPerPhase` counts for each card affected wherever it is used outside ModifyStrength (on AddKeyword it gave each Dwarf its own +3 damage, F-0213): a "(limit +N)" keyword bonus built so is a finding, fixable at landing with a `cardPhaseLimit` (limit N, amount 1).

**A printed "(limit (N))" or "(limit -N)" is the card's for the whole phase; "(limit X)" is per use (Q-0134,

## Costs, and playing cards from piles and hands

**"<do> a companion for each <thing>" may be spread (F-0224).** The player may affect several cards, or one card
several times (CR 4.2 "for each"). Built as `times` on one choice it is a finding; the right shape is a repeat
round a single choice (12_99, 17_128). "For each <thing> you spot" spots them all without asking (Q-0141).

**A reveal before "to" is a cost, and an empty draw deck cannot pay it (F-0022, F-0249).** "Reveal the top card
of your draw deck to <effect>" has the reveal as its cost, since everything before "to" is one (F-0022): it is
written in the definition's `cost`, and the event or ability is not offered with an empty draw deck. Built as an
effect, the card can be played for nothing with no deck: a finding, fixable at landing (Forearmed 56_16, F-0249;
the original 6_16 has it as the cost). The `C-` is the card not playable with the deck emptied. This is not the
optional look or draw of Q-0104 and Q-0029, which is an effect and is offered with an empty deck.

**A reveal that what follows reads must be able to happen (Q-0148, F-0284).** "Reveal the top card of your draw
deck. <Something that reads the card revealed: the card, its twilight cost, its type.>" has no "to", so by F-0022
the reveal is an effect; but the rest hangs on it. Ketura: "I would consider this either a requirement or a cost.
Either way, it has to happen for the rest to occur, so an empty deck should disallow the ability." So with or
without a "to" after the reveal, the ability is not offered (the event not played) with an empty draw deck. With
no "to", write `requires: {type: HasCardInDrawDeck}` on the ability and leave the reveal its first effect
(Glorfindel, Revealed in Wrath 59_16 and 9_16); without the requirement the card is a finding, already ruled. The

optional look of Q-0104 or the optional draw of Q-0029, which stay on offer with an empty deck. NOT ruled: a
reveal that a cost buys ("<cost> to reveal the top N cards ... <what reads them>": War-beacon 103_35, His Cruelty
and Malice 53_91; Q-0163, open: today offered with an empty deck and not flagged), and "you may reveal the top
card ..." on an optional trigger. Do not read Q-0148 onto those; ask.

**What bounds an X: the action's costs, never what its effect can use (Q-0069, Q-0087, Q-0094, F-0067,
F-0111).** The rulings on X look two ways and are one rule. X is NOT capped by what the effect could do with
it: an X of exertions is free up to those able to exert, whatever it buys (Q-0069, Great Gate 103_23: extra
exertions hinder nothing); "remove X threats to <do something to> a card costing X or less" has no cap but the
threats (Q-0087); a "(limit +3)" caps the bonus and not the X (the L check). X IS held to what the rest of the
action's COSTS can pay, a play from a pile or hand being a cost (Q-0062): "hinder X ... to play X cards from
your discard pile" stops at the cards that can be played (Q-0094); "remove X ... to play ...; its twilight cost
is -X" starts where the card becomes affordable (F-0067, `LeastDiscountToPlay`); "hinder X beacons and discard X
conditions to ..." stops at what both halves before the "to" can find (F-0111, Northern Signal-fire 103_30). So
ask of the thing X is matched against: is it before the "to", or a play? Then it bounds X, and a definition
that lets X run past it is a finding; otherwise X runs as far as its own cost can be paid. X of zero: Q-0015
and Q-0034, under "What is offered and what is asked" in the rulings log.

Whether the searcher may take fewer cards than the number printed is Q-0172, open: assert the game's exact number in an active test and park nothing.

## Boards, readings and tools

**FreepsGetCardChoiceCount() at a choice of phase actions.** The rig check `FreepsGetCardChoiceCount()` is NOT 0 at an ordinary phase-action decision (it counts the cards offering actions); to assert no card choice is pending use `AwaitingFreepsSkirmishPhaseActions()` and its kin.

**Check D on a change that lasts the turn: read it in a later phase than the one it was made in (Radagast 59_26).** Made in the regroup phase, the last of the turn, a change lasting the phase and one lasting the turn read alike: three tests of "When Radagast leaves play, this bonus is immediately lost" took him out there, and `until: endOfTurn` made `end(current)` survived them all. Make the change in an earlier phase (Saved From the Fire 8_20 places a companion in the dead pile in the fellowship phase), read it at once and again in a later phase, and on the next turn where it should be gone.

**Check D on "until the end of the turn" for a card the end of the turn removes.** Check D on "until the end of the turn" for a card the end of the turn itself removes (an artifact made a minion is discarded by the regroup discard of minions before the turn ends: Bladetusks 103_41, 103_42, 103_43): claim D by the before side (still so at the move decision and after a second move) plus the discard, and say so in the ledger's Notes. Do not park or invent an after side.

**Check D on "until the regroup phase" for an effect that can begin in a regroup phase (Q-0160, open).** A trigger that can go off inside a regroup phase ("when you lose initiative": Glimpse of Fate 60_12 and 10_12, by a regroup ability that discards from hand) makes its effect with `until: start(regroup)` (`until: regroup` is the same), which the engine keeps until the NEXT regroup phase begins: through the whole of a second move. Assert that in an active test (read it at once, in the second move's maneuver phase, and gone at its regroup phase) and name Q-0160 on the `Findings:` line as a rider; do not raise it again.

**Check D on a delayed "at the start of the regroup phase" that an ability sets up: fight a skirmish between (Under Foot 60_52, Flaming Brand 52_32).** Such a sentence is built as an `AddTrigger` with `until: end(Regroup)`. In a turn with nobody assigned no skirmish phase ends, so a lifetime mistaken for `end(skirmish)` passes: use the ability, assign and resolve a real skirmish, read the card still in play inside it (before the pair of passes that ends it), then gone at the first regroup decision. Whether the sentence is the ability's or a trigger of every turn is Q-0164 (open): a rider, not a new question.

**A filter that feeds a count.** Where a filter feeds a COUNT ("for each X stacked here", "for each X you can spot"), lay the board so the count under the right filter differs from the count under each neighbouring filter (the word dropped, the other clause's word, the card's culture): equal numbers of each kind hide a wrong filter. A hindered card and a "you can spot" count: a hindered companion is not spotted or counted (CR 5.0 "hinder"), and forEachYouCanSpot leaves him out unless the definition says `hindered: true`. Where a test has one hindered among the counted (Anduin Banks 61_227: 7 companions read 2 over 5, one hindered 1, two hindered 0), `mutate.py <id> --replace 'filter: companion' $'filter: companion\n<tabs>hindered: true'` is the mistake that shows the test notices; without such a candidate nothing does. In the errata phase (the orchestrator's notes for it, not a ledger's Notes) a hindered card is owed on such a count only where the card's text speaks of one, or where the erratum's own change is to the words "you can spot" (Anduin Banks 61_227); nowhere else (Pippin 69_30 owes none).

**A correction of several lines for mutate.py.** A correction of several lines (a block wrapped in an If) is typed on mutate.py's command line with bash's `$'...'` quoting, tabs and newlines written out (`mutate.py <id> --replace $'\t\t\t\tphase: skirmish\n\t\t\t\teffect: [' $'<the new lines>' --with-parked`), or built in a few lines of python that call mutate.py. A mutant of an `until:` must be a time the engine knows (`endofturn`, `end(current)`, `end(<phase>)`, `start(<phase>)`): one that names no phase (`end(turn)`) is no longer loaded, so every test of the card fails for want of the card and the mutant proves nothing. Every object of a correction is written over several lines, one field to a line: an unquoted HJSON value runs to the end of its line, so a one-line `{ type: HasCardInDiscard, filter: culture(gandalf),event }` does not parse and mutate.py says "made the card unloadable" (err-b68_14). A mutant that takes a whole block out is a `--replace` with an empty new text; the old text must be the block exactly, tab for tab and with its closing newline, which is easiest built in python (err-b68_111).

**"Use vitality to resolve that skirmish instead of strength" is two overrides, one for each side (Final Triumph 68_115 and 18_115).** The definition replaces the Free Peoples total and the Shadow total separately (`setFPStrengthOverride` and `setShadowStrengthOverride`), and a board on which a side wins or loses the same way by vitality as by strength cannot tell whether that side's override is there: with the Free Peoples one taken out of 68_115, eight tests still passed. Give EACH side a board where the number replaced and the number that replaces it give different winners: a companion of greater strength and less vitality than the minion, who loses (Aragorn with one wound, 8 and 3, against Uruk-hai Troop 12_157, 9 and 4), as well as a minion of greater strength and less vitality, who loses. The same holds for any text that replaces one number by another for both sides at once.

**A site can be controlled only once every marker has passed it.** A site can be controlled only once every marker has passed it: on player 1's first turn ShadowTakeControlOfSite() fails; SkipToSite(3) or later first.

**A required start-of-turn trigger with a 0-to-N choice.** A required start-of-turn trigger with a 0-to-N choice ("heal up to 3 allies") leaves its card choice pending when StartGame() returns, and again when SkipToOtherPlayersTurn() arrives at that player's next turn: answer it there. With nothing to choose it asks nothing.

**"At the start of each of your turns" reads BETWEEN_TURNS; read every counter before as well as after.** "At the start of each of your turns" is not "at the start of your fellowship phase" (CR 4.2 "turn sequence"): while a start-of-turn choice is pending the phase reads BETWEEN_TURNS. Read it; an old test's reading of it is not "the game's own" to drop. And read every counter BEFORE an action as well as after (wounds 0 then 1, pool 0 then N): the reviewer has had to add these in three batches running. "At the start of each turn" on a Free Peoples card (Prized Lagan 68_110) is the same trigger and takes the same `no -`: the other player's turns are ones on which the card is not active, so R reads the offer at its owner's next turn and no offer at the other player's turn between.

**The turn-skippers do not lay sites.** The turn-skippers (SkipToSite, SkipCurrentSite) do not lay sites, so they cannot walk a Shadows-era path; show a site number on the Fellowship or Tower path and say so in Notes. A Shadows-block site whose site number or region is the point is laid on a Fellowship-block path with `MoveCardToAdventurePath(scn.GetShadowSite(...))` after `SkipToSite(n - 1)` (pitfall "A Shadows-block site path"; Anduin Banks 61_227 at site 7, region 3). A Hobbit-set card may be a helper where no other card has the property (an ally whose home is 6H), if you have read its definition.

**cardtest.py run ends with a blank line.** `cardtest.py run` ends with a blank line: the result line is the one before it (`| tail -2`). Its `OK (N tests)` counts the active tests only: a parked (`@Ignore`) test is an `I` among the dots and is not in N.

**Grep before calling a property unique to a block (Aldor 4_262).** Before saying "no card of block X has this property, so a Hobbit-set helper it is", grep the definitions: Aldor 4_262 has homes 3T and 6T. The same before a ledger says "no printed card does X": one said nothing takes a companion out of play in the fellowship phase, and Saved From the Fire 8_20 does. Scan each card's whole block and match without regard to case: many older definitions have no `side:` line and write `race: wizard`, `type: companion` in lowercase (Q-0158), so `grep "race: Wizard"` beside a `side:` line misses them (Out of the High Airs 60_17: every Free Peoples Wizard is [gandalf]).

**A definition's `revision:` can be behind the sheet (F-0294, Shadowfax 67_24).** The packet prints the sheet row of the definition's own revision number, and since engine pass 19 any row of a higher revision under "A later row of the sheet", with the address of the picture the client shows today (from `PC_Cards.js`). Where the game departs from the text in a way that looks meant (the effects and an old test agree against the text), look there before asking. If there is one, the definition's labels and `gametext:` are what is stale: a `gametext` finding, fixed at landing as Ketura ruled for 58_57 (the live block labelled as the released revision, the earlier block kept above it as a History comment). Test the later text.

**A definition's game text may have been brought to the sheet's wording on 2026-10-05 (F-0297, The Faithful Stone 68_50).** Commit 15034aa3d2 changed the `gametext:` of 44 errata and V3 cards to the sheets' wording on Ketura's ruling ("The sheets"). On those cards "the sheet and the definition's game text agree" is one witness, not two, and a finding, question or note written before that day counted the card by the text it then had. The packet prints the text such a card had before, under "The game text before the campaign changed it" (for any commit of the campaign's that changed the wording, not that one alone), and the address of its picture. Before calling such an entry mistaken, or a departure from the original meant, read that and the picture (fetch it and look); `git log -p -S"<the words>" -- <the .hjson file>` shows the change itself. The Stone's game text carried the original's "you may spot a Man" until then; its image and sheet row have none, so its required trigger is right (Q-0017) and F-0041 stands for 13_75 only.

**Facts earlier authors got wrong.** FACTS earlier V3 authors got wrong: not every Free Peoples Wizard is a companion (Radagast 31_17 and Saruman, The White 32_24 are [gandalf] Wizard allies); Bilbo 12_119 and 13_142 are Ring-bound Hobbit companions; with no minion in play the game skips the maneuver, archery, assignment and skirmish phases outright, so an "until end of" one of those phases can outlive the turn's first regroup; a test that reads the Shadow player's actions while the Free Peoples player holds the decision asserts nothing (three old tests did); Shadow Wraith minions exist (Corpse Lights 6_99 and others), so "a Wraith" with no "your" reaches them; a prevented discard leaves the card chosen for it in play (Deceit 18_29, Gaze of the Eye 101_45); the helper comments of the Race to Mount Doom files are not to be trusted, so read each helper's definition even in a light pass (Gimli 1_13 is Son of Glóin, called "Dwarf of Erebor" in 91_9, 91_10, 91_12 and nine files of sets 92 and 94; in 91_11 1_130, No Ordinary Storm, was called Saruman's Ambition and 1_69, the Free Peoples ally Albert Dreary, was "The Palantir of Orthanc, shadow artifact", so that file's invalid candidates could not be played whatever the filter said).

**Rig methods newer than the generated list.** The generated list .claude/skills/gemp-testing/references/vts-api.md may lag the rig: read the source if in doubt: gemp-lotr-server/src/main/java/com/gempukku/lotro/virtualtable/. Control of a site is a direct cheat (`FreepsTakeControlOfSite()` and its kin, gemp-testing): do NOT use Down to the Last Child 4_148 as a workaround any more.

**lint's `second-copy` and `ruled` warnings.** `second-copy`: the card is not unique, its text says "this <type>" or "here", and no scenario has two aliases of its blueprint. Put a second copy in play wherever it could change a reading (the Standard's F), or say in Notes why none could; the warning then stands for the reviewer to read. `ruled`: a test is parked on a question, and the warning names the rulings-log entries that name the card or that question; an entry on the card's sheet row or revision answers nothing, one that names the question has answered it.

**lint's `title-kinds` warning, and a `no C-` it passes over.** `title-kinds`: the definition names a title (`name(Gollum)`, `title(Merry)`) that cards of more than one culture or type carry, and every card of that title the test file names is of one kind. Where the text has no culture or type word, give each kind a test of its own (the Standard's F; never two cards of one unique title on a board), or say in Notes why the text shuts the others out; the warning then stands for the reviewer. The Hobbit sets are not counted, nor a card naming its own title. A `no C-:` line under a clause whose only cost is the card discarding itself gives no `waived` warning, and the Standard no longer asks for the line.

**lint's `cardinfo` warning (Q-0145).** `lint.py` raises a warning of kind `cardinfo` when a definition's cardInfo block departs from the rest of the definition or from what the block is on the other cards of its kind (`cardinfo.py <id>` shows the same, `cardinfo.py --survey` what each kind's fields are). No test reads cardInfo and none is written against it: put the definition's line right and report it as a `data` finding. On a PC erratum `//id: <the original's id>.<revision>` is let pass beside `<id>.<revision>`: 143 errata of sets 1 to 9 have it, and Ketura has not yet said which is meant. An image name's revision number is not compared with `revision:`.
