# Shapes already settled

What the campaign settled about particular shapes of card text: what a ruling means for a definition and a test, what the engine does, and what to read on the board. A definition that departs from an entry here is a finding; do not raise these shapes as questions again. Ketura's rulings themselves, in Ketura's words, are in `rulings-log.md`; `rulings-index.md` lists both files' entries, and `scripts/lookup.py <id or words>` prints an entry whole. Every Q-, F- and G- id can be looked up in full in `../archive/` (questions.md, findings.tsv).

## How card text is read

**Any X of exertions is at least 1 (Q-0076; F-0552, F-0553, F-0593, Q-0341; F-0257, F-0445, F-0446, F-0447, F-0709).** "Exert X <cards>" and "exert X times" have X at least 1; X of zero by spotting is still allowed.
- Spotted and exerted X ("spot X burdens to exert X companions", They Will Find the Ring 2_81): the exertion's floor wins, `count: {type: range, from: 1, to: {type: forEachBurden}}` (no burden: maximum 0, nobody exerts), not a number prompt from 0 (F-0552, F-0553). Test: minimum 1, choose fewer than the burdens.
- One player spots X, the OTHER exerts X (Thrice Outnumbered 7_171): `chooseHowManyToSpot` asks from 0 and has no `from`, so build `chooseANumber` with `from: {type: min, firstNumber: 1, secondNumber: <the count>}` (a bare `from: 1` asks 1 to 0 with nothing to spot), `to` and `default` the count (F-0593, Q-0341). Test: `ShadowGetChoiceMin()` reads 1.

**"Up to N" and "any number" are counts from 0; a choice of exactly N is a finding (F-0074, F-0221, F-0229, F-0457, F-0667, F-0780).** Erkenbrand 102_47 ("up to 3") is `count 0-3`; Armor of Khazad 17_1 `count: 0-2`; Massing in the East 53_92 `count range 0 to fromMemory raceCount`; Malice 53_79's two plays `optional: true`; Rise of the Necromancer 33_51 "any number" `0-100`.
- Where one event raises a "you may <do X to> that minion" for several cards at once (two [uruk-hai] minions killed by one overwhelm), the choice takes any number of them (Uruk Reserve 13_173, `count: 1-100`).
- Test: the choice's minimum read, and a use that takes fewer than N.

**A card that makes itself a minion from the support area (Q-0077, Q-0078; Deceived 13_78, 13_79, 13_80; A Shadow Rises 11_216; F-0611).** Offered from the support area only (Q-0077, the Bladetusks); a following sentence calling the card "it" as the minion is part of the ability (Q-0078). For "... becomes a <keyword> <race> minion until the start of the regroup phase ..., and cannot take wounds or bear other cards. This card is still a condition." (11_216: no wound bar):
- Ledger: two `Restriction` clauses ("and cannot take wounds", "or bear other cards") and one `Effect` ("This card is still a condition.").
- Build: `turnIntoMinion`, `disableWounds` and an added `cantBear` modifier, each with its OWN `until: regroup` (F-0611), under a requirement AND an `If` both asking for the card in the support area. Definitions also discard what the card bears on becoming a minion (their comment cites a Decipher ruling in neither rules document); no printed card can be on it there, so Notes only.
- Test: minion in `Zone.SHADOW_CHARACTERS`, back in `SUPPORT` at the first regroup decision; read each limit in a LATER phase than the one it became a minion in (a wrong `end(maneuver)` passes a same-phase test). Wounds: a lost skirmish at 1 vitality (Ettenmoors), and Defend It and Hope 4_115's choice leaving it out. Bearing: a [gondor] fortification's transfer leaves it out (First Level 7_92 in maneuver; Third Level 7_125 in a skirmish, other minions on the same [gondor] Man); a Shadow item in the Shadow phase, 13_80 only (Staff of Saruman 13_81). Still a condition: Traveler's Homestead 13_42, or Pallando 13_37 on a companion who wins a later skirmish, offers it beside a second Shadow condition.
- Test, the two checks (requirement at trigger collection, `If` at resolution): the Q-0077 test lays the trigger's event again while the card is the minion and reads strength and vitality unchanged: a second heal (13_80), its own win as a Wizard in the fierce round (13_78). On 13_78 the `If` alone is told by two Wizards winning one skirmish (helpers, "Two Wizard minions in one skirmish"); on 13_80 heals resolve one at a time (Mithlond 18_136 too), so only with both out does it stack (strength 24, vitality 2). 13_79: no board has it a minion at the start of a maneuver phase: Notes.

**Reinforcing more than one token is one token per choice (F-0113, F-0620).** A `Repeat` round `ReinforceTokens`; `times:` on ReinforceTokens puts all on one card and is a finding.
- "Reinforce a [culture] token twice" is one `ReinforceTokens` with `times: 2` on the one card chosen; a `Repeat` there is the finding (Corsair Grappling Hook 18_62; the set 8 Raider errata). Test: nothing asked after the first choice, both tokens on that card.

**"Its skirmish" on a minion is the skirmish that minion is in (F-0114, F-0321).** Assigned and waiting, its text is not active. A finding: counting over `InSkirmish` with only `filter: self`, or a race/type filter lacking its own `InSkirmish` (Race Text 93_8: `filter: wraith` wrong, `wraith,InSkirmish` right).
- Test: a card of the kind assigned and waiting while another skirmish with something to count is fought.

**"By another card" is a card other than THAT card (Q-0079, Q-0395).** A [raider] minion hindering itself is answered by Corsair Helmsman 103_46.
- Open (Q-0395): "another <kind>" on an item its bearer's kind (Southron Bow 4_247, built `not(bearer)`): test as built.

**Where a "spot" stands decides effect or requirement (Q-0085, Q-0058, Q-0393; F-0022, F-0440, F-0492, F-0557, F-0599, F-0615; F-0119, F-0261, F-0711).** After "to" it is an effect: offered with nothing to spot (Q-0085); among the costs it is a finding (Perspective 18_25, F-0615). These are requirements (CR 4.2 "spot"), not offered with nothing to spot, `-` that board, a finding without `requires: canSpot` or with only a `choose` in the effect:
- In a cost sentence with no "to" ("Maneuver: Exert Boromir and spot an ally.", Horn of Boromir 3_42; Q-0058, F-0492; Aldor 4_262). An erratum moving it after "to" (53_42) does not carry over.
- Before "to" ("Exert Galdor and spot a minion to return that minion", Galdor 3_18; Vilya 3_27): a cost (F-0022, F-0440, F-0557).
- An event's opening "Spot a <kind>." that the next sentence acts on (Battle to the Last 11_4, F-0599): `-` board, not `F`.
- "Spot X and choose one: ..." = "spot X *to* choose one" (Ketura, Q-0393; Hearth and Hall 17_8).

**A bare cost sentence with no "to", then effect sentences (Q-0058; F-0562, F-0569, F-0585, F-0591).** The no-"to" sentence is the price, in `cost`, not asked as a question; `C-` is it unpayable (Uruk Spy 1_155, `Card_01_155_Tests.AnExhaustedSpyCannotRespond`; Wizard's Stash 103_7). The rest go in `effect`: as costs they still happen if the action is cancelled (No Defense 4_28, F-0562: War-warg 5_64 on a warg-rider in the skirmish cancels, `Card_04_028_Tests.CancelledByAWarWargTheTokenStaysRemovedAndTheCharacterKeepsHisPossessionBonus`).
- "Discard the top card of your draw deck." read by the next sentence (Vilya 9_23): `cost`, memorized there; `C-` empty deck (F-0569). Q-0148's `HasCardInDrawDeck` is for a REVEAL, which pays nothing.
- An event's opening discard read by the next sentence (Clever Hobbits 7_54): event `cost`, memorized; test The Mouth of Sauron 103_70 cancelling, conditions stay discarded (F-0585, `Card_07_054_Tests.CancelledByTheMouthOfSauronTheConditionsDiscardedForItStayDiscarded`).
- "Discard any number of cards from hand. The Free Peoples player then discards ..." (Hosts Still Unfought 7_151, Rope and Winch 7_309; F-0591): Shadow player's discard `cost`, Free Peoples player's in `effect`; test Gandalf, Wise Guide 19_8 cancelling (`Card_07_151_Tests.CancelledByGandalfTheCardsDiscardedForItStayDiscarded`, `Card_07_309_Tests.CancelledByGandalfTheCardsDiscardedForItStayDiscarded`).

**"Remove X ... to <do something to> a card costing X or less" (Q-0087, F-0133; F-0132).** X is capped only by what there is to remove; a cap by "the highest cost" is a finding (ForEachTwilightCost over HighestTwilight adds up ties).

**"The <other> player may make you <do X> to prevent this" (Q-0088).** "You" chooses what X is done to; the other way is a finding.

**"A <thing>" is one: a choice of one, not all that match (F-0136).**

**"Items" and "a possession or artifact" take artifacts as well as possessions (F-0045, F-0796).** "This minion may not bear items" (Writhing Tentacle 101_66) bans artifacts too: its `cantBear` filter said `possession` and goes by items. "Discard a possession or artifact borne by ..." (Azog 32_35) chooses `or(possession,artifact)`. A filter naming possessions alone where the text says items or artifacts is a finding, moot or not.

**"The second time / the first X ... each Shadow phase" needs a Shadow-phase requirement (F-0130, F-0390; F-0163; F-0156, F-0751).** Missing is a finding. On a cost modifier (Dimrill Dale 1_350) write `{ type: phase, phase: shadow }` beside the `perPhaseLimit` (as Great Chasm 2_118, Breeding Pit of Isengard 15_188).
- Test: the same play in the regroup phase (helpers.md, "A minion played outside a Shadow phase").
- "The first <card> played ... each turn" counts by the turn: `perTurnLimit` / `incrementPerTurnLimit` (Lake-Town 30_63, F-0751; Ford of Bruinen 1_338), and Deep of Helm 4_347's "played at Deep of Helm" adds `PlayedCardThisTurn` with `atCurrentSite` (F-0156). Counted by the phase (`perPhaseLimit`), a play in a later phase of the turn is discounted again. Test: a play of the same kind in a later phase of the same turn, at full cost.

**"May X, and must Y for each X"; "copies of that card"; "is considered a <race>" (Q-0091, Q-0092).** The first is no price: X, then Y as far as possible (Q-0091). "Copies" counts the card itself, by title (Q-0092). "Is considered a [culture] <race>" ADDS the race.

**"A minion" on a Skirmish event that reaches only minions in the skirmish (Q-0095, F-0527).** The TEXT is changed to "a skirmishing minion" (done on 103_84); elsewhere a finding of kind gametext naming Q-0095.
- A Decipher card is built as printed: no `InSkirmish`, a card in no skirmish may be chosen for nothing (F-0527: B, Ketura 2026-10-15; Vengeance 4_208). Narrowing it is a finding of kind gametext naming F-0527; every Decipher "Make a/an <kind> strength +N" skirmish event chooses so (Kill Them Now 4_156 is not this shape).

**"<Exert> a <kind> to <do X to> a character he is skirmishing" on a Skirmish event (Mordor's Strength 1_255).** The cost names the kind with no `InSkirmish`, as printed (not F-0527's shape); it wants a candidate in no skirmish (the Standard's "a minion" line).
- Test: an assigned Orc whose skirmish is still to come pays, nobody wounded; catches `inSkirmish` and `assignedToSkirmish(memory(...))` written for `inSkirmishAgainst(memory(...))`.

**"Make a <kind> strength +N (or +M if in a fierce skirmish)" conditions on the card chosen (F-0550, Sentinels of Númenor 2_37).** `Conditional` on `memoryMatches` with `inFierceSkirmish`, not `fierceSkirmish` (goes by phase; Unimpeded 12_182 is right).
- Test (owed): a companion in no skirmish chosen during another's fierce skirmish reads +N (Card_02_037_Tests).

**A possession's "while bearer is skirmishing ..." filters `bearer`, never `self`: a possession is never in a skirmish (F-0713).** Great Axe 15_80's modifier on `self,inSkirmishAgainst(hunter,companion)` never applies; `bearer,inSkirmishAgainst(hunter,companion)` does.

**"Wound X twice" written as an exertion is a definition fault (F-0148, Take it Down! 103_89).** A wound can kill and hit an exhausted character; an exertion cannot. Fixable at landing.

**`count:` is how many cards, `times:` is how often one card (F-0108).** "Exert a [gondor] companion twice" (We Must Go Warily 3_48) is `times: 2`; `type: exert, count: 2` exerts two different companions once each, so a lone unwounded [gondor] companion cannot pay it and two both take a wound.
- Test: one companion chosen, and exerted twice.

**"Make allies take wounds from archery fire instead of companions" (Q-0136, Q-0137, Q-0147, F-0243, F-0244, F-0275).** One modifier, `AlliesTakeArcheryFireWoundsInsteadOfCompanions` (Come Down 4_146, 54_146), wound by wound: to an ally while one can take it, else as usual. Not `AllyCanParticipateInArcheryFire` plus `CantTakeArcheryWounds` (adds archer allies to the total; loses wounds no ally takes).
- Build: no cost, so require `canSpot` with `ally,not(barredFromArcheryFire)` (Q-0034). Test: withheld with no ally and with only a barred one.
- Streets of Edoras 4_340 (`AllyMayNotParticipateInArcheryFireOrSkirmishes`) bars these wounds too (Q-0147, F-0275): an `Interaction` clause owed in 4_340's file, a line in each Come Down's Notes; `mechanics/skirmishes/AlliesTakeArcheryWoundsTests` meanwhile.

**"<X> instead" replaces the effect only when X can happen; otherwise the original effect occurs (F-0537, F-0610).** CR 4.2 "instead": "If the second effect cannot happen for any reason, then the original effect occurs." Extraordinary Resilience 1_287 with no burden to remove lets the heal happen (no `preventHeal` cost; an If on `canSpotBurdens`). Force of Uruk-hai 11_184 with the Free Peoples draw deck empty is withheld (`HasCardInDrawDeck`, `player: free people`), since the wound would be taken anyway.
- Test: the board where the replacement cannot happen, and the original effect read.

**"Participates in archery fire and skirmishes" against "may not participate" (Q-0150, F-0281, F-0283; F-0789).** Nothing to define: the engine lets no card allow an ally `AllyMayNotParticipateInArcheryFireOrSkirmishes` (4_340 only) bars, so `AllyCanParticipate...`, `EnableParticipation...` and `ignoreAllyLocation` assignment (Breaching Shot 102_21) skip him for archery total, wounds and any assignment; other effects (a strength bonus) still happen. No finding against the granting card.
- Test: Interaction clause owed in 4_340's file, a Notes line in the other card; `mechanics/skirmishes/AllyBarredFromTakingPartTests` shows it, and 53_42 and 102_21 each carry one `R` test.
- A no-cost ability whose whole effect is to have an ally fight and assign its minion to him (Dunlending Ravager 4_15, Dunlending Warrior 4_18) is withheld when it can assign to nobody: only a barred ally, all allies assigned, the minion assigned, or a non-fierce minion in the fierce round (F-0283: A). Requirement and choice both `ally,AssignableToSkirmishAgainstIgnoringAllyLocation(self)` (hjson-reference); see `mechanics/skirmishes/MinionThatAssignsItselfToAnAllyTests` and the Warrior's test in `AllyBarredFromTakingPartTests`. Assert withheld; park nothing.
- Its own file tests the filter in the requirement as well as the choice ("A phrase the definition writes twice"): the offer on a [rohan] Man ally alone and a Dwarf ally alone (Guma 4_277, Thrarin 1_27), the `-` of the minion assigned with another ally free, the choice minimum 1 (Card_04_015_Tests); not the mechanics classes' boards.

**"Cannot take archery wounds" is `cantTakeArcheryWounds`, not `cantTakeWounds` during the archery phase (F-0778).** Built as `cantTakeWounds` on allies in the archery phase, Goblin Bow 33_40 refused them every wound in that phase (Malice 3_79's on Bard 30_22); Haradrim Marksman 8_60 is the model.
- Test: an ally wounded by a card's text in the archery phase takes the wound.

**"Choose X ... (or you may play it ...)" plays the card chosen (F-0152, Ominous Sky 103_97).** Asking again which card is a finding, fixable at landing.

**"If a minion loses this skirmish to that <character>", a shuffle of none, and "free" (F-0197, Q-0113; F-0196, Q-0112; Q-0115).** ONE effect however many lose: `limitPerPhase: 1` on the `addTrigger` (1_29, 51_29); without it a finding. A shuffle FROM PLAY of "any number" with none chosen still shuffles; Mumakil Commander 18_71 requires a [men] mount of his spotted. Assert both; park nothing. A Decipher ruling on a WORD ("free") does not reach an erratum that dropped it.

**"While ..., the move limit is +1 (-1)" lasts the turn, "for this turn" or not (Q-0179: B; F-0333, F-0334, Q-0122, F-0608, F-0317, F-0389, F-0586, Q-0396, F-0518).** CR 4.2 "move limit": "is in effect for the whole turn, even if the conditions for the modification change"; Ketura: they "follow the basic rules around move limits, which is that they are permanent".
- Build: once-a-turn trigger adding the modifier `until: endOfTurn` (The Number Must Be Few 1_260, Derndingle 4_330, Radagast 9_26, Théoden 13_137, Race Text 91_8, 91_12: `ConstantlyCheck` with `PerTurnLimit` 1 and the condition; `IncrementPerTurnLimit`, then `AddModifier` ... `until: EndOfTurn`). A plain `ModifyMoveLimit` with `requires` is right only on 51_260, by its own second sentence (Q-0122); elsewhere a finding.
- Test: condition ended in regroup, limit unchanged; D test ending it in fellowship (Saved From the Fire 8_20), two moves, limit read in the Shadow phase after the second, then on the other player's turn (a -1 needs Durin's Secret 1_71 for a second move); the fellowship-phase change read again in regroup, the condition made by a play in that phase, not before `StartGame()` (which hides a missing `until`; Riding Like the Wind 11_155, F-0608); three turns with the condition holding. Use a board the floor of 1 cannot hide (pitfalls, "The move limit never reads below 1").
- A bought +1 ("Each time your fellowship moves, you may kill a companion ... to make the move limit +1", Race Text 93_3) is `until: endOfTurn`, not `until: end(regroup)` (F-0317). D test: kill on move 1, decline on move 2, read 3 in that Shadow phase, make move 3, read 2 next turn.
- The check is a required action ordered with others and rechecked on resolution, so the +1 is lost if its spotted card leaves first (Rivendell Waterfall 1_342, ranger killed by Blade Tip 1_209 at the start of fellowship). No finding (F-0389, dropped: "that's part of the reason *why* freeps has the last tiebreaking say in cases like this, so that it's their fault and not the rules"): test the order that gives +1, park nothing, add no second trigger.
- Self-discarding "The move limit for this turn is +1" (Secret Paths 7_69): built as Gift of Boats 1_46, required `played` trigger on `self` adding `until: endOfTurn`; a plain `modifier` loses it when Sleep, Caradhras 1_84 discards it (F-0586, Card_07_069_Tests.TheMoveLimitStaysRaisedForTheTurnAfterTheConditionIsDiscarded).
- Spotting X "at <this site>" counts X's place (Q-0396: A; F-0518): Horse-country 4_326 spots `culture(rohan),mount,atSite(self)`; Rider's Mount 4_287 on Guma 4_277 (home 4T) is not there, limit stays 2. 1_342 and 4_330 never meet it (no ranger ally printed; allies never unbound).

**"Make the Free Peoples player choose to move again" is one forced move; with no cost it wants `canMove` (Q-0018; F-0495; Q-0301: A; F-0754).** `addModifier` of `hasToMoveIfAble` with no `until`: it ends with the phase, and a later regroup phase asks "another move?" as usual. "This turn" (4_30, 5_118, 8_120, 13_108) does not stretch it.
- Test: a later regroup phase (limit 3 via ad hoc `MoveLimitModifier(null, 1)`) asks as usual (It Betrayed Isildur 101_47, Osgiliath Channel 8_120: TheFreePeoplesPlayerIsMadeToMoveAgainOnceAndIsAskedAsUsualInALaterRegroupPhase; Hornburg Wall 5_118: TheFellowshipIsMadeToMoveAgainOnceAndIsAskedAsUsualInALaterRegroupPhase).
- No-cost (8_120): requires `{ type: canMove }`, test not offered at the limit; with a cost it is offered there by rule.

**"<Do> N ... If you do, ..." wants all N (Q-0169, F-0330).** What can be done is done (CR 4.2 "effect"), but below N the "If you do" part does not follow. A New Light 67_15 checks `ForEachInMemory` `IsGreaterThan` 1; "more than 0" copied from a one-card text (17_15) is the fault.
- Test: exactly N-1 candidates; first part done, second not.

**"<Do X>. You may repeat this for each <other ...>": the first X is required, and only the repeats are asked (F-0658, F-0671).** Trap Is Sprung 13_57 and Dark Fell About Him 13_178: the first heal a cost, then `addBurdens`, then the repeat counted on the wounds left. Built as optional repetitions throughout, the first heal-and-burden can be declined and one repetition too many is offered.
- Test: with one wound nothing is asked; with three, two repeats are offered.

**`cardsInHandMoreThan` is strictly more than: "2 or more cards in hand" is `count: 1`, "a card left in hand" `count: 0` (F-0409, F-0602).** Oath Sworn 11_91 with `count: 2` was not offered with exactly 2 cards in hand; Loyalty Unshaken 7_10 takes `count: 0`.
- Test: the hand at exactly the printed number.

**"Frodo is corrupted" on a card that names Frodo (F-0356, Held 6_109; Q-0213, Q-0103, Q-0227, Q-0237).** `corruptRingBearer` takes no filter, so the trigger has `requires: canSpot` with `ring-bearer,title(Frodo)` (Ketura, Q-0213 row L).
- Test: on Frodo the Ring-bearer, the move to site 9 ends the game (game log); on Frodo, Frenzied Fighter 13_149 beside `GimliRB`, nobody corrupted, Shadow phase at site 9 follows.
- A hindered Frodo cannot be spotted, so is not corrupted (Q-0103); no test, and Q-0227 owes no R clause (hinder; no Decipher card tested under it, Q-0237). Named on the ledger's Sources line.

**"While a card titled X is removed from the game" names no owner (F-0375, Gollum, Her Sneak 13_50).** `hasCardInRemoved` checks one pile, `player` defaulting to `you` (a modifier's owner); its all-players code is unreachable. Build an `or` of it as is and with `player: free people`. The next sentence's "your discard pile" is the owner's.
- Test: the Free Peoples player's own Déagol 13_46 in his removed pile (`MoveCardToZone(card, Zone.REMOVED)`); the Shadow player's Gollum reads strength 7 and fierce.

**"Loses <keyword> and cannot gain <keyword>" until a time is one `RemoveKeyword` with that `until` (Q-0011, Harrowdale 11_243; Q-0282, F-0487).** Removal until a time also keeps the keyword out from other cards and the card's own text, so a missing "cannot gain" is no finding; the ledger still cuts at "and".
- Test the gain AFTER the loss: Easterling Axeman 4_224 given 2 burdens after the skirmish reads strength 9, not fierce, not offered in the fierce assignment, fierce at the first regroup decision (`Card_11_243_Tests`, last test).
- Into the Caves 17_100 CHOOSES the keywords and bars gaining any (Q-0282: A): `chooseAKeyword` with `fromCard: memory(chosenMinion)` and `noneOption: None` in a `doWhile` (until None or none left; nothing asked if none), `removeKeyword` with `keywordFromMemory`, then `cantGainKeywords` (no new keywords and no more of one: damage +1 stays +1) until regroup. Each use loses only its named keywords (F-0487).

**A printed "until <a time>" must be in the definition; with none the engine ends the effect early (F-0076, F-0511).** Royal Guard 102_54's valiant "until the regroup phase" is `until start(regroup)` (without it, it ends with the skirmish); Stewards' Legacy 13_75's muster "until the end of the regroup phase" is `until: end(regroup)` (without it, it lapses with the phase Denethor exerted in).

**"While you can spot a <keyword> ..., this <card> is (gains) <that keyword>" with no "another" keeps it once gained (Q-0276: A; Q-0290: A; Q-0291).** Sentry Uruk 15_170 and Ravaging Wild Man 15_91 (the only two) write `spotsItself: true` on each `addKeyword`: it then spots itself (`spotsItself`) and keeps the keyword after the other card goes; alone from the start it has none, and once removed or its text stops it is not regained alone (Q-0291).
- Test: the other card leaving play, then the fierce round.

**"Spot a race" chooses a character; "name a race" chooses a name (Relentless Warg 17_89; Úlairë Nertëa 19_38; F-0643, Q-0385).** Spot: `chooseActiveCards` of `character,hasRace` then `storeRaceFromCard` (every raced card is a character; read the minimum 1). Name: `chooseARace` then `storeRace`. Both store nothing when cheated in, so play the card (pitfalls, "A card that names something as it is played").
- "Choose a Shadow player who must spot a race" (Argument Ready to Hand 12_1; F-0643: B) and "Spot a Shadow race" (Let Fly 13_19; Q-0385: A) are `choose(minion,hasRace)` and right: test a Man companion not offered.
- A printed race requirement ("To play, spot a Wizard") is a plain spot.
- "Make the Free Peoples player name 3 cultures ..." (Gate Picket 6_101): his `chooseActiveCards` of 0 to 3 companions or allies, then `cultureFromMemory`; outcomes equal a naming's, test as built.

**"(Or <more> if you can spot that discarded <card>)" is spotted by title (The Witch-king, Dark Lord 19_40).** A discarded card cannot be spotted (CR 4.2 "spot"); one title is one thing (CR 4.2 "unique"): `memorize` on the cost, `canSpot nameFromMemory(<memory>)` in a `Conditional`.
- Test (Card_19_040_Tests): another-title Nazgûl gives the lesser (Black Rider 12_161, discarded Úlairë Nelya 1_233); a second non-unique copy the greater (two Black Riders); own title discarded spots the reader (The Witch-king 1_237).

**"Spot X and a Y" is two cards; "Spot X to <do something to> a Y" is not (Q-0398: B, 2026-11-04; Q-0400: A; F-0716).** One Last Surprise 15_33 writes the second `canSpot` as `unbound,companion,not(name(Gandalf)),inSkirmishAgainst(minion)`; test IsNotPlayableInGandalfsOwnSkirmishSinceGandalfIsNotTheUnboundCompanion. ## Limits

**Limits on uses versus limits on what the effect gives (Q-0041, Q-0082, Q-0093; F-0199, F-0792).** A limit on USES (limitPerPhase / limitPerTurn / limitPerSite: "limit once per turn", "limit N times") withholds the ability at its limit, cost or no cost. A limit on WHAT THE EFFECT GIVES (effectLimitPerPhase / effectLimitPerTurn: "(limit +3)", "add a threat (limit 1)", "(limit 2 per phase)" of things made) leaves a costed ability on offer: cost paid, nothing done, flagged `limit` with FreepsActionNoEffect / ShadowActionNoEffect. Full text: hjson-reference "Limits", test-generation's L check.
- Test (uses): not offered, with its cost payable and something left for the effect to act on. Test (effect field): the card writes the R clause for the flag.
- A cap inside the effect (CardPhaseLimit or CardAffectedLimitPerPhase value, limitPerCardThisPhase, or an `If` on `perPhaseLimit` whose `true` branch does `incrementPerPhaseLimit`, as Úlairë Attëa 1_229) also stays on offer. The engine flags `limit` only for a cap on strength, keyword amount or cards drawn with nothing else happening (mechanics/actions/NoEffectFlagTests; draws only 33_48, 33_49); other caps read "false" (Sting 51_313). The card asserts no flag and no R clause (the mechanic's; test-generation, "Rulings, interactions and formats"); test the cap under L.
- A field that mismatches its text's kind of limit is a finding, fixable at landing. Park nothing on Q-0093.
- "Limit once" (a limit on uses) written as an `If` on `perPhaseLimit` inside the effect is a finding: the ability stays on offer after its use, its cost paid for nothing (The Arkenstone 31_7, F-0792), and a trigger raised twice in one skirmish is put to the player to order, the second doing nothing (Dwarven Axe 1_9, F-0199). **What a `packet.py --find` pattern is tried against.** Each card is up to three lines, as printed, joined by newlines, matched case-insensitively: `.` and `.*` stay in one line, `\n` steps to the next.
- The id line: id, name, culture, type, race or item class, an event's phase, `unique`, the numbers, an ally's home, a signet; it ENDS with the last of these a card has (`site 5` on a minion, `home 3F` on an ally, `signet Gandalf` on a companion), so `vitality 2$` finds no minion: write `vitality 2, site`. Every event's phase is on it after the type, from `timewords:` (`[gondor] event, Skirmish; twilight 0`, `event, Maneuver or Skirmish`), so "a Shadow Skirmish event" is `'\[sauron\] event,.*Skirmish'`; from the King block (set 7 on) the phase word is not in the text line (End of the Game 10_30 is `Make an exhausted ...` where Axe Strike 1_3 is `Skirmish: Make ...`).
- `keywords: ...`: the card's own printed keywords only; a keyword its text gives it under a condition, or gives another card, is not there (Gollum, Her Sneak 13_50 and Royal Guard 102_54 have no `keywords:` line), so a card that only GAINS a keyword is found by its game text. `'keywords:.*Ranger'` is the keyword, never the word in a text (Uruk Scout 2_47); a culture with a keyword is `'\[shire\].*\n.*keywords:.*Tale'`.
- The game text has no markup (`Regroup:</b>` matches nothing: write `Regroup:`).
- `^` is the start of the id line; `$` is the end of the card's LAST line only: `'minion.*site 3$'` finds 7 cards (no keywords, no text), `'minion.*site 3(\n|$)'` finds 147; end an id-line pattern with `(\n|$)`. Without `^.*` id-line words also match game text: `'\[men\] minion, (?!Man)'` lists Force of Harad 11_83 and Láthspell 11_87, `'^.*\[men\] minion, (?!Man)'` only Saruman 17_48 and Ted Sandyman 18_75 (a lookahead never steps over a newline). A card whose whole text is one sentence: `'keywords: Damage\+1\nDamage \+1\.$'` lists cards whose text is "Damage +1." alone (Uruk Savage 1_151).
- Every card of one title: `--title Sting` (whole title, compared as the game does: no accents, capitals or spaces; with `--find`, the matching cards of that title), or `'^\S+  Sting(,|  )'` (after id and two spaces the title runs to a comma if there is a subtitle, else two spaces), so `Sting$` and `^Sting` find nothing and bare `Sting` finds Sting of Shelob too. A title with its own comma prints the same way: Boromir, My Brother 4_111 (`title: Boromir, My Brother`, no subtitle) is listed by `'^\S+  Boromir(,|  )'` though `name(Boromir)` does not match it, and NOT by `--title Boromir` (which goes by `title:` as the filter does); a good invalid candidate for a text naming the title (Card_13_060_Tests).
- A pattern with a tag in it is refused; a search finding nothing says so when its pattern runs `.*` across lines or opens with `^` and a letter.
- `--skip` leaves out the ids given AND every other version of each, of `--find` and `--defs` (51_221 beside batch card 1_221); `--defs` also keeps shut a card whose text is a batch card's with a word or two changed (Uruviel 1_67 beside Silinde 1_60, the same `copyCard`): choose another helper, or read it in stage 2 without `--skip`. `lookup.py <terms> --skip <ids>` does the like for the reference files: a shapes or pitfalls entry naming a batch card, and such a finding's row, is named by heading and not printed; the rulings log and a question's entry are printed whatever they name.

**`cardtest.py run` output and scope.** It ends with a blank line: the result line is the one before (`| tail -2`). `OK (N tests)` counts active tests only; a parked (`@Ignore`) test is an `I` among the dots. `run` takes a test file of the logic or common module as well as the server's (`cardtest.py run gemp-lotr/gemp-lotr-logic/src/test/java/<...>Tests.java --out <name>`, alone or beside card ids; it reads the package from the source, as `probe` does). - An original that is not unique starting from the tests of a unique erratum (Focus 15_15 from 65_15): the copied tests have no second copy, and lint goes quiet once one scenario names two aliases; lay the second copy wherever the original's text could read it (each copy's own offer, only the copy used discarded).
- `ruled`: a test is parked on a question; the warning names the rulings-log entries naming the card or that question. An entry on the card's sheet row or revision answers nothing; one naming the question has answered it.

**lint's `not-asked`, `not-in-hand` and `conditional` warnings (G-0030).** Each is a reading or answer that may show nothing.
- `not-asked`: an offer read as absent with nothing at that moment showing the player being asked: put his `Awaiting...PhaseActions()` or his decision beside it, or move the reading to where he is asked (for the `...NotAvailable` twin: a card read as offered in the same choice, or the choice's minimum).
- A response EVENT read as not offered to a player asked nothing at that moment (the Shadow player's event after a skirmish, when the next thing asked is the Free Peoples player's "Choose next skirmish to resolve"): read `assertFalse(scn.ShadowAnyDecisionsAvailable())` beside the other player's pending decision, with another test showing he IS asked there when the event can be played (Card_01_247_Tests).
- A Response ABILITY likewise: `FreepsActionAvailable(card)` answers false to a player not being asked (pitfalls, G-0030), so read `assertFalse(scn.FreepsAnyDecisionsAvailable())` beside the other player's `Awaiting...PhaseActions()`, with a control in the same phase where the ability IS offered (Card_19_008_Tests.IsNotOfferedWhenACardThatIsNoEventIsPlayed).
- `not-in-hand`: a play read as refused for a card no cheat took out of its draw deck. `conditional`: an answer under an `if` or `while` on a reading of the table: assert what is asked and answer it.
- A warning wrong on its board is let off on its line with `// lint-ok: <why>`, which the reviewer reads. Errors (refused): a `field` in a test class, `@Ignore` sharing a line with `@Test`, an empty `FreepsChoose("")`, a Race to Mount Doom stats test without its `Intensity:` line or `getIntensity()`.

**lint's `copies` and `if-is` warnings (Q-0239).** `copies`: a test cheats into play, for one player, more cards of one title than its dots allow (Q-0239; the Standard, "no more copies of a title in play than its dots allow"); read under `--form` too, so mechanics classes and lone tests draw it; a guard that must lay such a board says why on the line (`// lint-ok: ...`).
- Lint reads a `Get...Scenario()` that hands on another's table (`return GetScenario(format, ringBearer);`) through to its builder, Ring-bearer passed down. It counts by title whatever the subtitle, each player apart, the scenario's Ring-bearer included (a second Frodo beside `FOTRFrodo`), following a card by variable or alias: into play by `Move...ToTable`, `Move...SupportArea`, `AttachCardsTo`, `MoveCardToZone` to a zone in play and a play; out by the removing cheats and the test's own `assertInZone`/`assertInDiscard`/`assertInHand` elsewhere. A play the game allowed counts as room. What the game removes unread is not seen: where an earlier copy was killed or discarded by the game, read it in its pile before the next is cheated in, or end the line with `// lint-ok: <why>`. A file naming a card with `OverrideUniqueness` (Race Text 94_16) is not checked. The cure is another printed card of the kind, not a second copy.
- `if-is`: a clause other than a Trigger or Response has a sentence "If <the thing named> is/are ..." and lists no `F` (2_79: `ringBearer,exhausted` written `companion,exhausted` passed until an exhausted Aragorn stood beside Frodo). Add the test with the nearest card that IS in that state while the one named is not, claiming `F`, or a `no F: <why>` line where none can stand. "If you ...", "If there ..." and an "If" with a verb before "is" are not taken for it.
- The named card standing idle may not be the nearest: on Shield of Boromir 52_38 ("If bearer is Boromir") "Boromir is in the skirmish" passed until Boromir fought while Aragorn bore the Shield; try the mistake with mutate.py before claiming `F` on an existing test.
- "If the fellowship is at <a kind of site>": the nearest is the Shadow player's own fellowship left at such a site: site at 2, `SkipToSite(2)`, lay the board, and the first player's move takes his fellowship to site 3 with the other marker still at 2 (Whistling Death 11_104). "In region N" likewise: the other marker left at the region's last site while the fellowship moves into the next (Well-traveled 11_66, "in region 1": `SkipToSite(3)`, then a move to 4).

**lint's `title-kinds` warning, and a `no C-` it passes over (Q-0306; F-0023).** `title-kinds`: the definition names a title (`name(Gollum)`, `title(Merry)`) carried by cards of more than one culture or type, and the cards of that title the file names leave out a type or are all one culture. Where the text has no culture or type word, give each type and a second culture its own test, not every culture (the Standard's F, Q-0306; never two cards of one unique title on a board), or say in Notes why the text shuts the others out; the warning then stands for the reviewer.
- Every clause naming the title (a play's "bearer must be", an ability's "if bearer is", a cost's "exert <title>") owes each kind its own test (Sting 11_173, C2); lint reads the cards laid by the tests claiming each clause, and the whole file only where no clause's text names the title. It cannot tell which card PAYS a cost (Follow Sméagol 5_23): a second culture laid as a bystander passes it.
- Hobbit sets are not counted, nor a card naming its own title, even in a Hobbit card's file: a Hobbit card naming Elrond with only HDG Elrond ally 30_21 laid is asked for an ally Elrond, so lay Decipher Elrond, Lord of Rivendell 1_40 on the Hobbit table beside it (Lore of Imladris 30_13).
- The card's own bearer line shuts out the title's other culture ("Bearer must be a [gondor] Man" with "if bearer is not Aragorn": the [rohan] Aragorn 17_93 can never bear it, Not Bound to His Fate 19_14) or other TYPE ("Bearer must be an [elven] companion" with "if bearer is Arwen": ally Arwen, Lady of Rivendell 102_6; Arwen's Bow 18_4, Arwen's Dagger 18_5): no board owed, warning stays, answered in Notes.
- For "bearer must be <title>" a follower of the title is never VALID, though the warning calls it one: followers bear no cards (CR 4.2 "follower"; F-0023). Lay him as an invalid candidate (in the support area, and borne as his Aid puts him) and the warning clears (Radagast's Staff 0_57 with Radagast, Tender of Beasts 13_38). For a cost "exert <title>": a follower cannot exert; lay him as the only card of the title and read the ability not offered (Saruman, Servant of Sauron 17_38 under A Fell Voice on the Air 3_52).
- Under a bar on being ASSIGNED ("Saruman and Gríma cannot be assigned to skirmishes") a follower shows nothing either way (followers are no characters): no board, warning stays, answered in Notes (Throne of the Golden Hall 17_98).
- A cost "exert <title> twice" written `select: bearer` on a card only that title may bear is still owed for each culture of the title, since each can be the bearer who pays (Pippin's Dagger 9_21 with the [gondor] Pippin 103_31, TheGondorPippinPaysTheExertionsToo).
- A `no C-:` line under a clause whose only cost is the card discarding itself is not asked for by the Standard: its `waived` warning says to take it out.
- A Ring-bearer given in the scenario's constructor ("9_49" for Bilbo, Bearer of Things Burgled) counts as laid only by a test that calls `GetRingBearer()` (Herbs and Stewed Rabbit 15_44).

**lint's `race-culture` warning fires only on a definition at fault (Q-0213: B; F-0019, F-0291; F-0052).** It asks for no board. It fires only where a definition goes by a race (`elf`, `dwarf`, `hobbit`, `wizard`, `wraith`) while its text names that race's culture and never the race, or goes by `culture(...)` where the text names the race and never the culture. Then file the finding (F-0019, F-0291); lay no board and give no card a culture or a race. Silent where text and definition agree.

**lint's `cardinfo` warning: a `data` finding, never a test (Q-0145; Q-0158: 1A; F-0279).** `lint.py` raises it when a definition's cardInfo block departs from the rest of the definition or from that block on other cards of its kind (`cardinfo.py <id>` shows the same, `cardinfo.py --survey` each kind's fields). No test reads cardInfo: put the line right and report a `data` finding. On a PC erratum `//id:` is the erratum's own id and revision (`54_192.1`), never the original's (`4_192.1`): "`id` is for the card's unique identifier, and parent is supposed to point to the original" (Q-0158: 1A). An image name's revision number is not compared with `revision:`.

**lint's `census` warning: a word of the text nothing in the definition holds (Q-0220: B; F-0430 to F-0443).** `census.py <id>` prints the same (`--all` for every definition). Three kinds:
- `phrase`: "[culture] <kind>" with no one filter having both ("heal a [rohan] ally" built as any ally, Supplies of the Mark 4_290; "another [men] Man" as any other [men] card, Furious Hillman 14_10).
- `kind`: a kind the text names (companion, ally, minion, a race, exhausted, wounded, a possession ...) that no filter has ("and neither is exhausted", A Promise 12_128; an ally where the text says companion, Houses of Lamentation 10_61).
- `done`: a thing done or read (exert, roaming, killed, a spot before a "to" ...) with nothing of its family in the effects (Pressing On 7_325 had no exertion, Vilya 3_27 no Shadow condition to spot).
- A match is a place to read, not a fault. Where the definition is at fault, file the finding (`hjson-logic`) and show it by a test. Where something else carries the word (a trigger only such a card can set off, a game rule, an engine effect), say what in Notes and the warning stands: Elessar's Edict 8_33 has no "prevent" because its discard is the prevention; Goblin Swarms 1_183 has no discard or wound removal for "discard cards and wounds on that Orc" because `StackCardsFromPlay` takes him out of play, which does both (assert the borne card in the discard pile and the wounds gone).
- A flag modifier with no filter is the usual case: Chamber of Mazarbul 11_233's "transfers of Free Peoples artifacts and possessions" are `transferForFree`, kinds from the engine rule (`TransferItemRule` offers only active Free Peoples possessions and artifacts its player owns).
- "Spot a <kind> and <do X to> him or her" with no "to" between is one choice: the spot is the effect's or cost's own `choose(...)` of the card acted on (Make Haste 10_109, `returnToHand` of `choose(culture(shire),companion)`).
- Silent where text and definition agree, on a definition with no `gametext:` (the Hobbit sets), and on a Race to Mount Doom modifier. 22 definitions match as it stands (one worked: Úlairë Enquëa 103_74, "companion or ally" built as a Free Peoples character).

**lint's `plain-play` warning asks for a card's play to be tested where its own text bears on it (Q-0279: C, B).** A card played from hand that stays in play, with no live test playing it from hand (`FreepsPlayCard` or `ShadowPlayCard` of it), must be one whose play `gen_play.py` calls plain (`gen_play.Card.bears_on_play`: a bearer, a requirement or extra cost, a cost or discount of its own text, toil, a trigger its own play may set off, a place its type does not give it). Otherwise lint names what bears on the play and asks for the test that plays it, which is that clause's test. The generator's reasons about its own table (a Ring-bearer of the card's title, the site under the play) and a sheet to settle are not counted. Silent on the landed cards.
- A text about ANOTHER card's play (Mantle of the White Wizard 103_3's "played" trigger) is named too: such a card keeps a test that plays it, claiming a `P1 Play [+]` clause (the seven of `orchestrator/engine-pass-28/tools-files/plainplay-keep.tsv` other than Uruk Trooper 4_199 and Uruk Veteran 4_201, whose test is the nearest miss of their own discount and claims its `-`; that file's column "strip-list verdict" is the old verdict: every row keeps its test). Any other card named for such a text has no `P1`: its own play from hand is laid as a `-` of that trigger where the card is a near miss of its filter (Sharku 5_58, "each time you play an [isengard] mount": his play is [isengard] and no mount, and notices `culture(isengard)` in place of `culture(isengard),mount`).
- A "reveal this card from hand" trigger on another card's play (Corsair Champion 13_84, "each time a Free Peoples possession is played") is cleared the same way: a second copy played from hand as a near miss (a Shadow minion, no possession).
- A Decipher original of a listed card (The Witch-king 2_85 beside 52_85) has no `P1`; lint prints no `plain-play` for any other version of a card the list keeps under the verdict "strip" (Scouring of the Shire 18_112 beside 68_112); its Notes may still say why.
- A card whose whole text is keywords, one of them Toil N (The Witch-king 11_226): the `K` clause is claimed by the stats test and by one test playing it by toil (the exertions' minimum and maximum, a character of another culture not offered, the pool after), as a follower's Aid is.
- "Roaming" bears on the card's own play only on a minion (Pippin 7_324's "wound a roaming minion twice" and Faramir 4_116's skirmish text are not named). A minion whose "roaming" picks a modifier's or ability's targets (Advance Captain 10_75, Advance Marauder 10_76, Advance Regular 10_77) or a branch of its own effect (Mordor Fiend 10_91, "add (3) (or (5) if this minion is roaming)") is named: the clause's test plays it from hand at a site below its own, so it roams and is read among its own targets or on its branch. "Arrange by cheating" does not stand against it.
- A twilight cost the text plainly gives another card ("play an [uruk-hai] hunter at twilight cost -2", "that minion's twilight cost", "with a twilight cost of X", Grond 58_103) is not counted; one lint cannot tell ("play this minion. Its twilight cost is -1" from a stack) still is.
- An own discount holding only in a phase where only another card's effect can play it (6_3, 6_5: "during a skirmish phase", played by Hill Clan 6_6) is not cleared by that test (lint counts only `FreepsPlayCard`/`ShadowPlayCard`): the nearest miss, its play at full cost in the Shadow phase, clears it.

**lint's `stale`, `race-side`, `pile-cost`, `rule-of-4`, `self-bar`, `item-class` and `bearer-min` warnings (Q-0058; Q-0062; Q-0262; F-0510).** Each names a place to read; a warning wrong on its board is answered on its line (`// lint-ok: <why>`) or by the ledger.
- `stale`: an offer read as absent after a cheat with no answer or `...RefreshDecision()` between (pitfalls, "A board changed under a pending decision"); `SetHand`, `SetDrawDeck`, `MoveCardsToDiscard` and twins refresh as they finish. It also names a card's action taken from, or read as offered at, that decision after a cheat that moved cards (`Move...To...`, `AttachCardsTo`, `StackCardsOn`): call `FreepsRefreshDecision()` / `ShadowRefreshDecision()` after the cheat (a skipper's draws make the offer chance: Card_01_175_Tests, Card_04_346_Tests).
- `race-side`: a PlayOn clause, or one listing `F`, names a race both sides have (Man, Hobbit, Wizard, Wraith) with no side word, and the Notes name neither Q-0058 nor the opponent: lay the opponent's card of that race as a candidate, or say why no board holds one. Only the literal words `Q-0058`, `opponent`, `other player`, `either side` or `both sides` clear it: "No Hobbit minion can be in play in a fellowship phase at site 1" (1_322, 2_115) does not; "The opponent's Hobbit (Q-0058) cannot be laid: ..." does (1_319). On "each time you play a <race>" the candidate is that card played by the other player while this card is active (F-0510's entry). Not printed for "skirmishing a <race>" (or "a Man or ...") on a Free Peoples card: no card of the other side is the one skirmished (Sword of Gondor 4_134, Faramir's Bow 4_118).
- `pile-cost`: a special ability playing another card "from your discard pile" lists no `C-` (Q-0062: the play is a cost).
- `rule-of-4`: a Free Peoples optional trigger or Fellowship ability whose whole effect is a draw, with no word of the Rule of 4 or Q-0262 in the ledger (the `-` board where it is spent). A draw on "each time you reconcile" owes no such board: no Free Peoples card reconciles in a fellowship phase (`packet.py --find reconcile`); a Notes line says so (13_71).
- `self-bar`: a Restriction on the card itself ("This minion may not ...") listing no `F` (the Standard's Restriction row, 19_18). `item-class`: two items of one class cheated onto one bearer. `bearer-min`: a PlayOn with no "may" or "up to" whose bearer choice's minimum the file reads nowhere (the Standard's line on `FreepsGetChoiceMin()`).
- Errors: a possession or artifact that prints no item class has its stats test read `assertNull(card.getBlueprint().getPossessionClasses())` (the Standard, "The stats test"); `ledger-text` names the first sentence of the game text the C clauses do not hold, or the non-C clause that holds text.

**lint's `read-before`, `helper-pool` and `mounted` warnings (Q-0307: A; Q-0279: E2; F-0196).**
- `read-before`: a state a cheat laid (wounds or tokens on a card, burdens, threats, where a card lies after `AttachCardsTo`, `MoveCardsTo...` or `StackCardsOn`) is first read only after the test has played, answered or passed. A `FreepsPass`, a `SkipToPhase` or `StartGame` is the game moving on, so a state cheated before a skip is read before the skip (the Standard's "Read before and after"; Q-0307: A). lint only checks the reading precedes the ACTION; put it before the skip yourself (Card_11_011_Tests).
- In a lone test kept on a Decipher card, adding the reading as its own line is no rewrite (as an alias renamed is none; an engine-pass lone test draws it too: Card_18_071_Tests, F-0196); nor is removing a reading Q-0279: E2 forbids (the pool after the event's own play: Card_01_044_Tests).
- A card put in hand and then played needs no reading. One cheated into hand mid-test and then stacked, discarded or used by the action is read in hand after `FreepsRefreshDecision()` and before the action (Card_01_073_Tests); one put in hand by `MoveCardsToHand` before `StartGame()` and first read after a `FreepsPass()` (as "not playable") is read in hand before the pass too; one the action removes at once (an item discarded as soon as attached) is let off on its line.
- Zone readings that count: a card moved to the top or bottom of a draw deck only by `assertInZone(Zone.DECK, card)` (`assertSame(card, scn.GetShadowTopOfDeck())` or `assertEquals(card, scn.GetFromTopOfFreepsDeck(n))` alone still warn: write both; Ring of Isengard 4_358). A card on a bearer only by `assertInZone(Zone.ATTACHED, card)` or `assertAttachedTo(card, bearer)` (lint's ZONE_READ; the rig has no `assertStackedOn`, though lint would count one): `assertSame(bearer, card.getAttachedTo())`, `assertEquals(bearer, card.getAttachedTo())` or `assertTrue(scn.GetAttachedCards(bearer).contains(card))` alone warn, even right around `StartGame()` (Dead Faces 16_4, Ancient Blade 7_15). A stacked card only by `assertInZone(Zone.STACKED, card)`, not `assertTrue(scn.GetStackedCards(x).contains(card))` (or `.containsAll`).
- `helper-pool`: the pool read on the line straight after a helper's play, on a card whose text says nothing of a cost, the pool, roaming or a twilight number (Q-0279: E2; 68_12). Where the card's text changes that cost another way (a site number -1 taking off a helper's roaming penalty: Úlairë Nertëa 8_80), keep the reading and end its line with `// lint-ok: <why>`; a plain trailing comment does not silence it.
- Both are left off cards approved or ready in status.tsv (some 835 and 58 landed files would draw them); `lint.py <ids> --landed-too` shows them. A test copied from a landed file (an erratum's, a twin's) can draw `read-before` on its new home: read the cheated state again straight after `StartGame()` (Card_01_005_ErrataTests has it in six tests). The pool laid by `SetTwilight` is not looked at by `read-before`: read it before the play anyway (Card_01_077_Tests).
- `mounted`: the text says "mounted" (bearing a mount) and the file lays no possession or artifact that is no mount, the nearest invalid candidate (White Rocks 4_346: `hasAttached(possession)` passed every author test). On a card that MAKES itself mounted (103_41, Bladetusk Matriarch 103_42, Bladetusk Rearguard 103_43) it is answered in Notes. Not printed on a possession whose OWN text says "if bearer is mounted" or "while bearer is mounted" (Rider's Spear 4_288; 11_153, 13_135, 15_140, 17_90, 18_95, 103_91): the card itself is the non-mount possession and the bearer carrying only it is the nearest miss. Not so where the card DISCARDS ITSELF as the cost and the check reads the bearer remembered (Sturdy Shield 15_141, `memorizeActive` of `bearer` then `discard` self): lay a second non-mount possession on the unmounted bearer (Heavy Chain 4_278, Card_15_141_Tests), or `hasAttached(possession)` passes. An ARTIFACT with such a text still draws it (an artifact is no possession).

**lint's `side-doers` warning: a bare side word's spread of doers (F-0551).** A Restriction clause listing `F` that names its doers by a bare side word ("Shadow cards may not discard ...", "cannot be removed by Free Peoples cards") draws it where the file lays no card of that side of one of the Standard's three kinds: a character (companion, ally, minion), an item (possession, artifact) and a card that is neither (10_47; Frodo 4_301 without an item let `side(shadow),not(possession)` pass). Judged on the cards the file names by id, the card itself left out. Race Text 94_20 lays Sting 1_313 on Frodo (its "Exert Frodo" ability withheld under the modifier, offered and paid with none); Ulaire Enquea 60_68 has the Notes line. Where no printed card of the kind can do the act, a Notes line says so in the words "bare side word" (as 4_301's), which lint reads.
- Not drawn where the text names the ALLOWED side ("may be exerted only by Shadow cards") and the definition bars the other by a bare side word; the barred side's spread is still owed, with a site among the barred (Throw Yourself in Next Time 2_71: Sorry About Everything 1_312, Sam 1_311's own ability, Sting 1_313, Ettenmoors 1_331; F-0551).

**No Play clause, and no test that only plays a card (Q-0279: B; Q-0220: I).** `gen_play.py` no longer writes `P1`'s test (`PlayedFromHandFor...`; `--write` is refused); it is kept for its reading of whether a play is plain, which lint's `plain-play` uses. A plain play is shown once by `mechanics/costs/TwilightPoolTests` and `WhereACardIsPlayedTests`; a card's play is tested where its own text bears on it, by that clause's test. "Plays to your support area." clauses are `Keyword [K]`, claimed by the stats test. A ledger's Notes may still say "The play: ..." of what a remaining test reads as it plays the card.
- A landed erratum's test a Decipher card's file starts from may still read the pool or a printed bonus across a plain play (as Card_15_062_ErrataTests' bearer test once did), or after a helper played only to meet a requirement (54_80 and 54_81 after Legolas, 54_80 after Ordulus's own play too): drop those readings in the copy.
