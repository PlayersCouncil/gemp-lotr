# Shapes already settled

What the campaign has learned about particular shapes of card text: what a ruling means for a definition and a test, what the engine now does, and what to read on the board. Each entry was first written in the orchestrator's notes to authors and moved here so that every phase has it. A definition that departs from an entry here is a finding (fixable at landing unless the entry says otherwise); do not raise these shapes as questions again. The words of the rulings themselves are in `rulings-log.md`; `rulings-index.md` lists both files' entries.

## How card text is read

**Any X of exertions is at least 1 (Q-0076).** Any X of exertions is at least 1: "exert X <cards>" as well as "exert X times" (Q-0076). X of zero by spotting, or of anything else, is still allowed.

**A card that makes itself a minion (Q-0077, Q-0078).** A card that makes itself a minion from the support area offers that ability from the support area only (Q-0077, the Bladetusks); its definition's requirement is right. A sentence after the ability that calls the card "she"/"it" as the minion is part of the ability (Q-0078).

**Reinforcing more than one token (F-0113).** Reinforcing more than one token is one token and one choice of card at a time (F-0113: `Repeat` round `ReinforceTokens`; `times:` on ReinforceTokens puts them all on one card and is a finding unless the text says "reinforce a token twice").

**"Its skirmish" on a minion (F-0114).** "Its skirmish" on a minion is the skirmish that minion is in; assigned and waiting, its text is not active (F-0114). A modifier counting over `InSkirmish` with only `filter: self` is a finding.

**"By another card" (Q-0079).** "By another card" in a card's text is a card other than THAT card (Q-0079, Corsair Helmsman 103_46): a [raider] minion hindering itself when played IS answered by a Helmsman.

**A spot that stands after "to" is an effect (Q-0085).** A spot that stands after "to" is an effect: the ability is offered though nothing can be spotted (Q-0085). A definition that has such a spot among its costs is a finding of its own.

**"Remove X ... to <do something to> a card costing X or less" (Q-0087, F-0133).** "Remove X ... to <do something to> a card costing X or less" has no cap on X but what there is to remove (Q-0087). A cap by "the highest cost" is a finding; ForEachTwilightCost over HighestTwilight adds up ties (F-0133).

**"The <other> player may make you <do X> to prevent this" (Q-0088).** "The <other> player may make you <do X> to prevent this": "you" chooses what X is done to (Q-0088). Built the other way is a finding.

**"A <thing>" is one (F-0136).** "A <thing>" is one: a choice of one, not all that match (F-0136).

**"The second time ... each Shadow phase" (F-0130).** "The second time ... each Shadow phase" needs a Shadow-phase requirement (F-0130): a finding where missing.

**"May X, and must Y for each X"; "copies of that card"; "is considered a <race>" (Q-0091, Q-0092).** "May X, and must Y for each X" is not a price: X is done, then Y as far as it can be (Q-0091). "Copies of that card" counts the card itself and goes by title (Q-0092). "Is considered a [culture] <race>" ADDS the race.

**"A minion" on a Skirmish event that reaches only minions in the skirmish (Q-0095).** "A minion" on a Skirmish event that reaches only minions in the skirmish: the TEXT is changed to "a skirmishing minion" (Q-0095, done on 103_84). On another card of the shape, say so as a finding of kind gametext, naming Q-0095.

**"Wound X twice" written as an exertion (F-0148).** RULED 2026-10-07 (F-0148, Take it Down! 103_89): "wound X twice" written as an exertion in a definition is a definition fault (a wound can kill and can fall on an exhausted character, an exertion cannot): fixable at landing.

**"Choose X ... (or you may play it ...)" plays the card chosen (F-0152).** RULED 2026-10-07 (F-0152, Ominous Sky 103_97, fixed): "choose X ... (or you may play it ...)" plays the card chosen; a definition that asks a second time which card is a finding, fixable at landing.

**Ruled 2026-10-08 and being built by engine pass 15 (F-0197, F-0196, Q-0115).** RULED 2026-10-08, being built (engine pass 15), park on the number: F-0197 (Q-0113): "If a minion loses this skirmish to that <character>, <effect>" is ONE effect however many minions lose (read as "at the end of this skirmish, if at least 1 minion lost ..."); the game still makes it once per minion. F-0196 (Q-0112): a shuffle FROM PLAY of "any number" with none chosen will shuffle the deck all the same (the game does nothing today); Mumakil Commander 18_71 will require a mount of his to be spotted. Q-0115: a Decipher ruling about a WORD ("free") does not reach an erratum that dropped the word.

## What is offered and what is asked

**The no-effect flag: which cards assert it.** The no-effect flag exists (gemp-testing, "The no-effect flag"; test-generation, "Rulings, interactions and formats"): `FreepsActionNoEffect(card)` / `ShadowActionNoEffect(card)` (and with words of the action's text) answer "false", "effect", "limit" or "skirmish". Do NOT assert it on every card: only where the definition declares `noEffectUnless`, or the rulings log names the card for it. A no-cost ability or optional trigger that is offered though it would do nothing is still a finding (Q-0034), flag or no flag.

**A choice whose every branch has nothing to act on (F-0116).** A choice whose every branch has nothing to act on is not put to the player (F-0116): a pointless prompt is a finding.

**A parenthesis with its own price (Q-0089).** A parenthesis with its own price the player elects to pay ("(or X if you also exert ...)") is the player's choice (Q-0089). Do not ask.

**One thing left to do is done without a second question (Q-0090).** Where taking an offer leaves only one thing to do, it is done without a second question (Q-0090). A second yes/no there is a finding.

**A number that can only be one number is not asked for (F-0147).** A number that can only be one number is not asked for (F-0147, in this clone): where X runs from 1 to 1, or "how many" has one answer, the game takes it. Do not answer such a question in a test (the answer would go to the NEXT decision); to read the bounds of an X, arrange a board where two numbers can be named.

**"You may make <the other player> do X" (Q-0106).** Q-0106 (ruled): "you may make the <other player> do X" is asked of "you" as a yes/no, only when X can be done, and the other player still makes the choices the text gives him. Where the game never asks, that is an error: fix or park it as a finding, not a question.

**Known shapes from the Dwarven errata (F-0181, F-0194, F-0182).** KNOWN SHAPES from the Dwarven errata, each still a finding on its card (show the correction; it is fixed at landing): (1) "the first time <X happens> each turn" built as PerTurnLimit + IncrementPerTurnLimit is counted only when the offer is TAKEN, so a declined offer comes again; the requirement `playedCardThisTurn` (filter, max 1) is the right word (F-0181). (2) A `preventable` after a choice that can come up empty was asked "to prevent NONE"; since engine pass 14 the engine itself does not ask (F-0194), so this is no longer a finding. (3) An optional trigger with no cost whose whole effect would do nothing is to be withheld by a requirement (Q-0034; F-0182).

**"You may <do X to a card>" on a required trigger wants its choice 0 to 1 (Q-0010, F-0191).** "You may <do X to a card of a kind>" on a REQUIRED trigger wants its choice 0 to 1 (Q-0010); a choice of exactly 1 there cannot be declined and is a finding fixed at landing (F-0191). Read the choice's minimum in a test.

**A prevention is asked only when the effect will happen (F-0194, Q-0114).** BUILT in this clone (engine pass 14, F-0194, Q-0114): "<effect>. <A player> may <pay> to prevent this" is put to that player only when the effect is going to happen. Not asked: a draw from an empty deck or past the Rule of 4, nobody chosen for the effect or the one chosen out of reach, burdens that cannot be added, threats at their limit, no site to control, a skirmish that will not be cancelled. Still asked: an effect that happens in part (draw 3 with 1 card left), and an amount that comes to zero (the engine cannot see that: there an `If` in the definition is still wanted, and its absence is a finding). Where the card can reach a board on which the effect cannot happen, one test reads `assertFalse(scn.ShadowDecisionAvailable("prevent"))` and the cost unpaid (ShadowAnyDecisionsAvailable() is true anyway in phases both players act in). Do not ask for a preventable to be wrapped in If/HasCardInMemory any more: the ones so written are harmless. mechanics/PreventionIsAskedOnlyWhenItsEffectWillHappenTests has boards.

**An "or" between two cost-to-effect halves (Q-0034).** An "or" between two cost-to-effect halves ("exert a Dwarf to heal an Elf, or exert an Elf to heal a Dwarf") is not CR 4.2's "choose one of two actions you are fully capable of performing": either half may be used though it will do nothing while the other could be carried out in full (Q-0034). Assert that; not a question.

## Limits

**Limits on uses and limits on what the effect gives, as built.** LIMITS, as built in this clone since 2026-10-07 (engine pass 8; Q-0041, Q-0082, Q-0093; hjson-reference "Limits" and test-generation's L check have the full text). A limit on USES ("limit once per turn", "limit once per phase", "limit N times": fields limitPerPhase / limitPerTurn / limitPerSite) WITHHOLDS the ability at its limit, cost or no cost: the test reads it not offered, with its cost still payable and something on the board the effect could still act on. A limit on WHAT THE EFFECT GIVES ("(limit +3)", "add a threat (limit 1)", "(limit 2 per phase)" where it counts things made: fields effectLimitPerPhase / effectLimitPerTurn, or a CardPhaseLimit value) leaves an ability with a cost on offer at the limit, cost paid, nothing done, flagged `limit` with FreepsActionNoEffect / ShadowActionNoEffect, and that card gets the R clause for the flag. A definition whose field does not match its text's kind of limit is a finding, fixable at landing. Park nothing on Q-0093.

**"Limit once per site" (Q-0084, F-0146, F-0145).** "Limit once per site" is once for each site in a turn (Q-0084) and is the engine's since 2026-10-07: `limitPerSite: 1` on the ability or trigger (or PerSiteLimit / IncrementPerSiteLimit), counted for the site the fellowship is at, cleared when it moves and at the end of the turn; an exertion on ARRIVING belongs to the site arrived at (F-0146). It is a limit on uses: withheld at the limit. A required trigger with a limit on uses is carried out no more often than its limit even when one event raises it twice at once (two companions exerting together draw a Cirith Ungol Watcher one card, F-0145). 103_92 and 103_103 are built so. A definition still built from per-phase limits and MoveCountMinimum is a finding.

**"(limit +N)" is the card's for the whole phase (F-0135, F-0125).** "(limit +N)" is the card's for the whole phase: cumulative over uses (F-0135, a per-use cap is a finding) and counted per source card, not per target (F-0125, FIXED in this clone).

## Costs, and playing cards from piles and hands

**The twilight cost of a card in play (F-0109, built).** F-0109 is FIXED: a card in play costs the number printed on it, whatever another card or its own text says, and a transfer costs the printed number only. `GetTwilightCost(card)` on a card in play now answers the printed number; to show a cost modifier in force read a card in HAND (what it would cost to play) or play one and read the pool. A site's Shadow number is still read as modified. Do not park on F-0109 any more.

**LeastDiscountToPlay bounds X in "remove X ... to play ... from your discard pile" (F-0067).** `LeastDiscountToPlay` (hjson-reference, values) bounds X in "remove X ... to play ... from your discard pile; its twilight cost is -X" (F-0067). It covers the discard pile only: Endless Night 103_96 (hand or discard) still starts X at 0; on that card park the lower-bound test on F-0067 and say so, do not report it again.

**Costs, and effects, in the order printed (Q-0080).** Costs, and effects, are carried out in the order printed (Q-0080, answered). A cost that puts out of reach what a later cost of the same action needs makes the action unpayable and it is not offered; the engine checks each cost against the board as it stands, so a definition without a requirement for this is a finding (the like case: F-0123, Raiding Schooner 103_55, fixed by a requirement).

**A RemoveTwilight cost beside a PlayCardFromDiscard needs removedTwilight (F-0046, F-0141).** A RemoveTwilight cost beside a PlayCardFromDiscard (or from another pile) needs `removedTwilight: N` on the play, so that the play is counted with what the cost removes (F-0046; F-0141 fixed Ulaire Otsea 103_78 this way). Without it the ability is offered when the pool pays the cost but not the card as well: a finding (fixable at landing; say so and park the test). Ominous Sky 103_97 is written this way.

**"Play any number of / up to N ... from your discard pile" is withheld when nothing can be played (Q-0086).** "Play any number of / up to N ... from your discard pile" is WITHHELD when nothing there can be played (Q-0086; IN THIS CLONE since 2026-10-07: the definition needs a HasCardInDiscard requirement, with PlayableOn(<filter>) where the cards are played onto named bearers; a definition without it is a finding fixable at landing).

**"Hinder/remove X ... to play X cards from your discard pile": X is capped (Q-0094).** "Hinder/remove X ... to play X cards from your discard pile": X is capped at the cards that can be played (Q-0094); a definition that lets X run past them is a finding (the engine has the filter PlayableOn(<filter>) for "can be played on").

**An action's costs are judged in order, with its own exertions counted (F-0184).** BUILT in this clone (engine pass 13, F-0184): an action's costs are judged IN ORDER, with the exertions and wounds its earlier costs are certain to place counted (a cost of `self`, `bearer`, a memory the trigger filled, or a choice with exactly as many candidates as it needs), for each later cost and for the card the action then plays from a pile, hand or stack. "Exert X twice to play ... from your discard pile" is not offered when X's exertions would leave the only candidate unplayable; "exert A and B" is not offered when one character with one exertion left is the only A and the only B. Where the player REALLY chooses who exerts, the action is offered on the board as it stands and may come to nothing: assert that, it is not a finding. mechanics/CostsAreJudgedInTheOrderPrintedTests has boards.

**Response events are not played by "play an event from ..." (F-0186).** BUILT in this clone (engine pass 13, F-0186): "play a(n) ... event from your discard pile / stacked here / from hand / from your draw deck" neither offers a RESPONSE event nor counts one towards being usable, unless it also has the current phase's timeword (Eye of Barad-dur 5_96). For "a [culture] event" a response event of that culture is the invalid candidate to lay beside the phase events; a phase word in the definition's filter beside it can then not be told from its absence by any board (say so in Notes, it is no gap), and a `not(timeword(response))` in a filter now does nothing (no finding). mechanics/ResponseEventsAreNotPlayedByEffectsTests has boards.

**A card hindered as its own cost still carries its ability out (F-0151).** FIXED in this clone since 2026-10-07 (F-0151): a card that has hindered itself as a cost, or been hindered in answer to its ability, still carries the ability out and is still bound by every "cannot" (burdens cannot be added while the Ring-bearer is hindered, and the like). **Q-0098 as built: the words in a definition.** Q-0098 as built (engine passes 8 and 10): `override: hindered` on a Discard or ReturnToHand, `hindered: true` on ChooseActiveCards / Memorize / CanSpot / ForEachYouCanSpot, and the same word on the requirement that gates the offer. **"Support" and support(<whose>) (Q-0099, Q-0101).** Q-0099 is BUILT in this clone (engine pass 9): the filter `support` is a "support card": a card lying in a support area or borne, at any depth, by a card that is (what an ally or a controlled site bears; not what a companion or minion bears). **A hindered Ring-bearer cannot be discarded or returned to hand (F-0174).** FIXED in this clone (F-0174): a hindered Ring-bearer cannot be discarded or returned to hand by anything; a definition needs no not(ring bearer) for that.

**Ruled on the night of 2026-10-07, and built by engine pass 12 (Q-0103 to Q-0108, F-0175 to F-0179).** RULED 2026-10-07, night (the rulings log has the words; do not raise any of these again): a card discarded WITH its dying bearer is "discarded" for a card that then plays it from the discard pile (Q-0105; a response that would PREVENT such a discard is still not offered, Q-0023); an optional look through an EMPTY draw deck is still offered (Q-0104); burdens are not "on Frodo" while Frodo is hindered (Q-0103); a card borne by a HINDERED companion is not "borne by a companion" (Q-0108); a guard that withholds a costed optional trigger may stay on a card that would otherwise ask at every phase (Q-0107: ask about another, do not convert it). BUILT in this clone (engine pass 12), assert them: The One Ring is never discarded, hindered or not (F-0175: a discard neither offers nor takes it); a hinder never offers a Map or a meta-site (F-0176); "shuffle ... from your discard pile into your draw deck" shuffles the deck once the effect is carried out, with nothing chosen or nothing there (F-0178: taking such an offer reads one more shuffle and the count unchanged; "the offer is withheld with nothing in the discard pile" is WRONG for these; a shuffle FROM PLAY of nothing is F-0196, below); "bearing N or more cards" / "for each card borne" count hindered cards (F-0179: `hasAttachedCount(N,any,override(hindered))`, `hindered: true` on forEachHasAttached; a count that names a type or culture does not see hindered cards).

**Whose support area: support(you), support(opponent) and the rest (F-0177, Q-0101).** BUILT (engine pass 12, F-0177, Q-0101): "in / from <a player's> support area" is written `support(you)`, `support(opponent)`, `support(free people)`, `support(shadow)`, `support(owner)`. ## Reveals, draw decks and shuffles

**A stacked card shuffled in goes to its own owner's deck (F-0172).** FIXED in this clone (F-0172, engine pass 11): a card stacked on a card that is shuffled in with `includeStacked` goes into its OWN owner's draw deck, and every deck that receives a card is shuffled; a hindered card shuffled into a deck does not stay hindered.

**revealHand of an empty hand.** `revealHand` of an EMPTY hand still gives the card's player a "Hand of <player>" decision listing no cards, which must be dismissed (FreepsDismissRevealedCards() answers it; the revealed count is 0); a RANDOM reveal from an empty hand shows nothing. Assert what the game does; not a finding.

**FreepsGetCardChoiceCount() while cards are only shown.** While cards are merely being SHOWN (a reveal), FreepsGetCardChoiceCount() counts them though none can be chosen: read a showing with FreepsGetTotalRevealedCardCount() and FreepsHasCardChoiceAvailable(card, false), and FreepsGetCardChoiceCount() only at a real choice.

## Boards, readings and tools

**FreepsGetCardChoiceCount() at a choice of phase actions.** The rig check `FreepsGetCardChoiceCount()` is NOT 0 at an ordinary phase-action decision (it counts the cards offering actions); to assert no card choice is pending use `AwaitingFreepsSkirmishPhaseActions()` and its kin.

**Check D on "until the end of the turn" for a card the end of the turn removes.** Check D on "until the end of the turn" for a card the end of the turn itself removes (an artifact made a minion is discarded by the regroup discard of minions before the turn ends: Bladetusks 103_41, 103_42, 103_43): claim D by the before side (still so at the move decision and after a second move) plus the discard, and say so in the ledger's Notes. Do not park or invent an after side.

**A filter that feeds a count.** Where a filter feeds a COUNT ("for each X stacked here", "for each X you can spot"), lay the board so the count under the right filter differs from the count under each neighbouring filter (the word dropped, the other clause's word, the card's culture): equal numbers of each kind hide a wrong filter.

**A correction of several lines for mutate.py.** A correction of several lines (a block wrapped in an If) cannot be typed on mutate.py's command line: build the old and new text in a few lines of python and call mutate.py from it. /home/claude/work/analysis/multiline-correction-example.py is one that works; copy and adapt it.

**A site can be controlled only once every marker has passed it.** A site can be controlled only once every marker has passed it: on player 1's first turn ShadowTakeControlOfSite() fails; SkipToSite(3) or later first.

**A required start-of-turn trigger with a 0-to-N choice.** A required start-of-turn trigger with a 0-to-N choice ("heal up to 3 allies") leaves its card choice pending when StartGame() returns, and again when SkipToOtherPlayersTurn() arrives at that player's next turn: answer it there. With nothing to choose it asks nothing.
