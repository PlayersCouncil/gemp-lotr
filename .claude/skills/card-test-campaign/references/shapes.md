# Shapes already settled

What the campaign has learned about particular shapes of card text: what a ruling means for a definition and a test, what the engine now does, and what to read on the board. Each entry was first written in the orchestrator's notes to authors and moved here so that every phase has it. A definition that departs from an entry here is a finding (fixable at landing unless the entry says otherwise); do not raise these shapes as questions again. The words of the rulings themselves are in `rulings-log.md`; `rulings-index.md` lists both files' entries.

## How card text is read

**Any X of exertions is at least 1 (Q-0076).** Any X of exertions is at least 1: "exert X <cards>" as well as "exert X times" (Q-0076). X of zero by spotting, or of anything else, is still allowed. Where one X is both spotted and exerted ("spot X burdens to exert X companions", They Will Find the Ring 2_81, F-0553), the exertion's floor wins: the choice of cards is `count: {type: range, from: 1, to: {type: forEachBurden}}` (with no burden its maximum is 0 and nobody exerts), not F-0552's number prompt from 0, and a test reads the minimum 1 and chooses fewer than the burdens.

**A card that makes itself a minion (Q-0077, Q-0078).** A card that makes itself a minion from the support area offers that ability from the support area only (Q-0077, the Bladetusks); its definition's requirement is right. A sentence after the ability that calls the card "she"/"it" as the minion is part of the ability (Q-0078). Where the card answers a TRIGGER ("each time a Wizard heals"), the test of this is one in which the trigger's event happens while the card is already the minion, read as strength and vitality unchanged: see the next entry.

**"This condition becomes a <keyword> <race> minion until the start of the regroup phase that has N strength and M vitality, and cannot take wounds or bear other cards. This card is still a condition." (the Deceived conditions 13_78, 13_79, 13_80; A Shadow Rises 11_216 has it with no wound bar).** The ledger cuts at "and cannot take wounds" and "or bear other cards" (two `Restriction` clauses) and at "This card is still a condition." (`Effect`). It is built as `turnIntoMinion`, `disableWounds` and an added `cantBear` modifier, each with its OWN `until: regroup`, under a requirement and an `If` that both ask for the card in the support area. Read the minion in `Zone.SHADOW_CHARACTERS` and back in `SUPPORT` at the first regroup decision. The wound refused: a skirmish it loses (Ettenmoors gives a companion the strength; with 1 vitality a wound would have killed it) and the choice of Defend It and Hope 4_115, which leaves it out. "Bear other cards": a [gondor] fortification's transfer, which leaves it out (First Level 7_92 in the maneuver phase; Third Level 7_125 in a skirmish, with other minions on the same [gondor] Man); a Shadow item goes on in the Shadow phase, when only 13_80 can be a minion (Staff of Saruman 13_81). "Still a condition": Traveler's Homestead 13_42, or Pallando 13_37 on a companion who wins a later skirmish, offers it beside a second Shadow condition in the support area. Read each of the three time limits in a LATER phase than the one the card was made a minion in: on 13_79 the bar on bearing was read only in the maneuver phase that laid it, and its `until` made `end(maneuver)` passed every test until the Third Level test was added (dec-b13_78, reviewer). The two support-area checks: the requirement is asked as the trigger is collected and the `If` as it is carried out, so on 13_78 the `If` alone is told by two Wizards who win one skirmish (both triggers are collected while the card is still in the support area; helpers, "Two Wizard minions in one skirmish"), while on 13_80 neither alone differs in play (heals are carried out one card at a time, Mithlond 18_136 included) and only with both out does the minion stack (strength 24, vitality 2 at a second heal). So the test of Q-0077 lays the event again while the card is the minion and reads it unchanged: a second heal (13_80), the card's own win as a Wizard in the fierce round (13_78). On 13_79 no board has it a minion at the start of a maneuver phase: Notes. The definitions also discard whatever the card bears as it becomes a minion (their comment cites a Decipher ruling that is in neither rules document); no printed card goes on a condition in a support area, so it is a line in Notes and no test.

**Reinforcing more than one token (F-0113).** Reinforcing more than one token is one token and one choice of card at a time (F-0113: `Repeat` round `ReinforceTokens`; `times:` on ReinforceTokens puts them all on one card and is a finding unless the text says "reinforce a token twice").

**"Its skirmish" on a minion (F-0114).** "Its skirmish" on a minion is the skirmish that minion is in; assigned and waiting, its text is not active (F-0114). A modifier counting over `InSkirmish` with only `filter: self` is a finding. So is one with a race or type filter and no `InSkirmish` of its own, wherever the text says "its skirmish" (F-0321, Race Text 93_8: `filter: wraith` gave every Wraith on the table the wounds of the skirmish being fought; now `wraith,InSkirmish`). The test is a card of the kind assigned and waiting while another skirmish with something to count is fought.

**"By another card" (Q-0079).** "By another card" in a card's text is a card other than THAT card (Q-0079, Corsair Helmsman 103_46): a [raider] minion hindering itself when played IS answered by a Helmsman.

**A spot that stands after "to" is an effect (Q-0085).** A spot that stands after "to" is an effect: the ability is offered though nothing can be spotted (Q-0085). A definition that has such a spot among its costs is a finding of its own. A spot INSIDE a sentence of costs with no "to" ("Maneuver: Exert Boromir and spot an ally.", Horn of Boromir 3_42; Q-0058) is a requirement (CR 4.2 "spot"): the ability is not offered with nothing to spot, the `-` is that board, and a definition without `requires: canSpot` is a finding (F-0492; Aldor 4_262 is built so). Do not carry over the reading of an erratum that moved the spot after "to" (53_42). A spot BEFORE "to" in an X-to-Y sentence ("Exert Galdor and spot a minion to return that minion", Galdor 3_18; "Exert Elrond twice and spot a Shadow condition to return that condition", Vilya 3_27) is a requirement the same way (F-0022: what stands before "to" is a cost): not offered with nothing to spot, and a definition that only chooses the card inside the effect is a finding (F-0440, F-0557).

**A bare cost sentence with no "to", then the effect sentences (Q-0058).** "Response: If the Ring-bearer puts on The One Ring, exert this minion. Discard ... Add a burden ..." (Uruk Spy 1_155), like "Exert bearer and discard this" (Wizard's Stash 103_7): the sentence with no "to" is the price of the sentences after it, paid in `cost`, and its `C-` is that cost unpayable (an exhausted Spy is not offered: `Card_01_155_Tests.AnExhaustedSpyCannotRespond`). It is not asked as a question (dec-b1_155). The sentences after it go in `effect`, not among the costs: written as costs they still play as the text reads until the action is cancelled, when they are carried out all the same. No Defense 4_28 was so (F-0562): the board that tells is War-warg 5_64 on a warg-rider in the same skirmish, cancelling the use (`Card_04_028_Tests.CancelledByAWarWargTheTokenStaysRemovedAndTheCharacterKeepsHisPossessionBonus`). "Discard the top card of your draw deck." with the next sentence reading the card discarded (Vilya 9_23: "X is the twilight cost of the discarded card") is this shape, not Q-0148's: a discard is a price, so it goes in `cost` (memorized there) and its `C-` is the draw deck empty (F-0569). Q-0148's `HasCardInDrawDeck` is for a REVEAL, which pays nothing.

**"Remove X ... to <do something to> a card costing X or less" (Q-0087, F-0133).** "Remove X ... to <do something to> a card costing X or less" has no cap on X but what there is to remove (Q-0087). A cap by "the highest cost" is a finding; ForEachTwilightCost over HighestTwilight adds up ties (F-0133).

**"The <other> player may make you <do X> to prevent this" (Q-0088).** "The <other> player may make you <do X> to prevent this": "you" chooses what X is done to (Q-0088). Built the other way is a finding.

**"A <thing>" is one (F-0136).** "A <thing>" is one: a choice of one, not all that match (F-0136).

**"The second time ... each Shadow phase" (F-0130).** "The second time ... each Shadow phase" needs a Shadow-phase requirement (F-0130): a finding where missing. So does "the first X played each Shadow phase" on a cost modifier (F-0390, Dimrill Dale 1_350: `{ type: phase, phase: shadow }` beside the `perPhaseLimit`, as Great Chasm 2_118 and Breeding Pit of Isengard 15_188 have it); the test is the same play in the regroup phase (helpers.md, "A minion played outside a Shadow phase").

**"May X, and must Y for each X"; "copies of that card"; "is considered a <race>" (Q-0091, Q-0092).** "May X, and must Y for each X" is not a price: X is done, then Y as far as it can be (Q-0091). "Copies of that card" counts the card itself and goes by title (Q-0092). "Is considered a [culture] <race>" ADDS the race.

**"A minion" on a Skirmish event that reaches only minions in the skirmish (Q-0095).** "A minion" on a Skirmish event that reaches only minions in the skirmish: the TEXT is changed to "a skirmishing minion" (Q-0095, done on 103_84). On another card of the shape, say so as a finding of kind gametext, naming Q-0095. On a DECIPHER card, whose print cannot change, the card is built as printed: no `InSkirmish` in the choice, and a card in no skirmish may be chosen for nothing (F-0527: B, Ketura 2026-10-15; Vengeance 4_208, engine pass 49). A Decipher definition that narrows such a choice is a finding of kind gametext naming F-0527; every Decipher skirmish event of "Make a/an <kind> strength +N" now chooses so (Kill Them Now 4_156 is no card of the shape).

**"<Exert> a <kind> to <do X to> a character he is skirmishing" on a Skirmish event (Mordor's Strength 1_255).** The cost names the kind with no `InSkirmish`, as printed, so it is no card of the F-0527 shape. Have an Orc whose skirmish has NOT begun (assigned, his skirmish still to come) pay the cost and read nobody wounded: that one board notices both `inSkirmish` and `assignedToSkirmish(memory(...))` written for `inSkirmishAgainst(memory(...))` (mutate.py, dec-b1_255). The cost also wants a candidate assigned to no skirmish (the Standard's "a minion" line).

**"Make a <kind> strength +N (or +M if in a fierce skirmish)" (F-0550, Sentinels of Númenor 2_37).** The condition is on the card chosen: its `Conditional` reads `memoryMatches` of the chosen card with `inFierceSkirmish`, not `fierceSkirmish`, which goes by the phase and gives +M to a card in no skirmish chosen during another's fierce skirmish (fixed at the landing of dec-b2_35; Unimpeded 12_182 is built so). That board is owed: a companion in no skirmish, chosen while another fights the fierce skirmish, reads +N (Card_02_037_Tests).

**"Wound X twice" written as an exertion (F-0148).** RULED 2026-10-07 (F-0148, Take it Down! 103_89): "wound X twice" written as an exertion in a definition is a definition fault (a wound can kill and can fall on an exhausted character, an exertion cannot): fixable at landing.

**"Make allies take wounds from archery fire instead of companions" (Q-0136, Q-0137, Q-0147, F-0243, F-0244, F-0275).** Built with the one modifier `AlliesTakeArcheryFireWoundsInsteadOfCompanions` (Come Down 4_146 and 54_146), which the engine reads wound by wound: an ally while one can take it, otherwise as usual. Not with `AllyCanParticipateInArcheryFire` beside `CantTakeArcheryWounds` on companions: the first also counts an archer ally for the fellowship archery total, which "take wounds" does not say, and the second loses the wounds no ally can take. The ability has no cost, so its definition requires an ally it can send a wound to (`canSpot` with `ally,not(barredFromArcheryFire)`; Q-0034), and tests show it withheld with no ally in play and with only a barred one. An ally who "may not participate in archery fire" (Streets of Edoras 4_340, `AllyMayNotParticipateInArcheryFireOrSkirmishes`) takes none of these wounds: a bar on participating bars taking archery wounds as well as adding to the total (Q-0147, F-0275). The test of that pair of cards is an `Interaction` clause owed in 4_340's file when that card is done (Streets is the card named first), with a line in each Come Down's Notes; `mechanics/skirmishes/AlliesTakeArcheryWoundsTests` shows it meanwhile.

**"Participates in archery fire and skirmishes" against "may not participate" (Q-0150, F-0281).** Nothing for a definition to say: the engine answers that no card allows an ally another card bars (`AllyMayNotParticipateInArcheryFireOrSkirmishes`, Streets of Edoras 4_340 only), so `AllyCanParticipate...`, `EnableParticipation...` and an assignment with `ignoreAllyLocation` (Breaching Shot 102_21) all pass a barred ally over, for the archery total, archery wounds and assignment by either player or by an effect; the rest of the granting card (a strength bonus) still happens. Not a finding against the granting card. The test of a granting card with the Streets is the Interaction clause owed in 4_340's file (Streets is the card named first), with a line in the other card's Notes; `mechanics/skirmishes/AllyBarredFromTakingPartTests` shows the rule, and 53_42 and 102_21 each carry one test of it as an `R` clause, written by engine pass 18. A no-cost ability whose whole effect is to have an ally fight and assign its minion to him (Dunlending Ravager 4_15, Dunlending Warrior 4_18) is withheld when it could assign the minion to nobody: only a barred ally in play, every ally already assigned, the minion itself already assigned, or a minion that is not fierce in the fierce round (F-0283: A; built by engine pass 21). Its requirement and its choice are both `ally,AssignableToSkirmishAgainstIgnoringAllyLocation(self)` (hjson-reference), so only allies it could be assigned to are offered; `mechanics/skirmishes/MinionThatAssignsItselfToAnAllyTests` and the Warrior's test in `AllyBarredFromTakingPartTests` show it. Assert it withheld on those boards and park nothing. The card's own file owes the filter's spread to the requirement as well as the choice ("A phrase the definition writes twice"): the second copy's offer resting on a [rohan] Man ally alone and on a Dwarf ally alone (Guma 4_277, Thrarin 1_27; the first copy takes the other), the `-` of the minion already assigned with another ally free, and the choice's minimum of 1 read (Card_04_015_Tests, dec-b4_15); the mechanics classes' other boards are not laid again.

**"Choose X ... (or you may play it ...)" plays the card chosen (F-0152).** RULED 2026-10-07 (F-0152, Ominous Sky 103_97, fixed): "choose X ... (or you may play it ...)" plays the card chosen; a definition that asks a second time which card is a finding, fixable at landing.

**Ruled 2026-10-08 and built by engine pass 15 (F-0197, F-0196, Q-0115).** F-0197 (Q-0113): "If a minion loses this skirmish to that <character>, <effect>" is ONE effect however many minions lose (read as "at the end of this skirmish, if at least 1 minion lost ..."); built with `limitPerPhase: 1` on the `addTrigger` (1_29, 51_29), and a definition of that shape without it is a finding. F-0196 (Q-0112): a shuffle FROM PLAY of "any number" with none chosen shuffles the deck all the same; Mumakil Commander 18_71 requires a [men] mount of his to be spotted. Assert both; park nothing on them. Q-0115: a Decipher ruling about a WORD ("free") does not reach an erratum that dropped the word.

**"While ..., the move limit is +1 (-1)" with no "for this turn" lasts the turn (Q-0179: B; F-0333, F-0334).** CR 4.2 "move limit": a change to the move limit "is in effect for the whole turn, even if the conditions for the modification change". Decipher's cards are built so whether they say "for this turn" or not, as a once-a-turn trigger that adds the modifier `until: endOfTurn` (The Number Must Be Few 1_260, Derndingle 4_330, Radagast 9_26, Théoden 13_137 "While you can spot Théodred, the move limit is +1"). Race Text 91_8 and 91_12 were a plain `ModifyMoveLimit` with a `requires`, which turns off and on with its condition; Ketura ruled that they "follow the basic rules around move limits, which is that they are permanent", and both are now 1_260's trigger (`ConstantlyCheck` with `PerTurnLimit` 1 and the condition; `IncrementPerTurnLimit`, then `AddModifier` ... `until: EndOfTurn`). The one plain `ModifyMoveLimit` with a `requires` left is 51_260, by its own second sentence (Q-0122); another that turns up with no such sentence is a finding, not a question. Its tests: the condition ended in the regroup phase and the limit read unchanged; a D test in which the condition ends in the fellowship phase (Saved From the Fire 8_20), the fellowship moves twice and the limit is read in the Shadow phase after the second move, then on the other player's turn (a -1 needs Durin's Secret 1_71 first, to have a second move at all); the change given in the fellowship phase read again in the regroup phase (with no `until` it ends with the phase it was given in); and the limit read over three turns with the condition holding throughout, since "once a turn" is the build's and not the text's. The amount itself is shown on a board the floor of 1 cannot hide (pitfalls, "The move limit never reads below 1"). A +1 that a trigger buys ("Each time your fellowship moves, you may kill a companion ... to make the move limit +1", Race Text 93_3) is the turn's as well and writes `until: endOfTurn`: `until: end(regroup)` was gone once the second move was made, and no kill bought a third (F-0317, fixed). Its D test kills on the first move, declines on the second, reads 3 in the Shadow phase after it, makes the third move, and reads 2 on the next turn. The standing check is a required action that is ordered beside others of its moment and run against its condition again as it is carried out, so a +1 is lost when the card it spots leaves play ahead of it (Rivendell Waterfall 1_342 with a ranger whom Blade Tip 1_209 kills at the start of the fellowship phase, his death taken first). That is no finding, on this card or on another of the build (F-0389, dropped: what follows from the order in which a player takes his own required triggers is his own doing, "that's part of the reason *why* freeps has the last tiebreaking say in cases like this, so that it's their fault and not the rules"): the test takes the order that gives the +1, no test is parked for the other order, and no second trigger is written into the definition to get round it.

**"Make the Free Peoples player choose to move again" is one forced move, and with no cost wants `canMove` (Q-0018; F-0495; Q-0301: A, "this turn" too).** Built as an `addModifier` of `hasToMoveIfAble` with no `until`: it ends with the phase, so the next move is made unasked and a later regroup phase of the turn (a move limit of 3, by an ad hoc `MoveLimitModifier(null, 1)`) asks "another move?" as usual (It Betrayed Isildur 101_47, Osgiliath Channel 8_120: TheFreePeoplesPlayerIsMadeToMoveAgainOnceAndIsAskedAsUsualInALaterRegroupPhase). "This turn" (4_30, 5_118, 8_120, 13_108) does not stretch it over the turn: one forced move, as built (Q-0301: A, 2026-10-13; Hornburg Wall 5_118: TheFellowshipIsMadeToMoveAgainOnceAndIsAskedAsUsualInALaterRegroupPhase). The engine cannot tell that the modifier does nothing at the move limit, so a version with no cost (8_120) requires `{ type: canMove }` and owes a test not offered at the limit; one with a cost is offered there by rule.

**"<Do> N ... If you do, ..." wants all N (Q-0169, F-0330).** "If you do" asks for what the sentence before it says: where that sentence takes 2 cards, as much as can be done is still done with one (CR 4.2 "effect"), and the "If you do" part does not follow. A New Light 67_15 ("choose 2 minions, then return those minions ... If you do, reveal that hand ...") checks `ForEachInMemory` `IsGreaterThan` 1; a check of "more than 0" copied from a card that takes one (17_15) is the fault. Its test lays exactly N-1 candidates and reads the first part done and the second not.

**"Frodo is corrupted" on a card that names Frodo (F-0356, Held 6_109).** `corruptRingBearer` takes no filter: it corrupts the Ring-bearer whoever he is. Where the text names Frodo the trigger carries `requires: canSpot` with `ring-bearer,title(Frodo)`, the card that is both (Ketura, Q-0213 row L). Two lone tests: borne by Frodo the Ring-bearer the move to site 9 ends the game (read from the game log); borne by Frodo, Frenzied Fighter 13_149 beside `GimliRB` it corrupts nobody and the Shadow phase at site 9 follows. A hindered Frodo cannot be spotted, so he is not corrupted while hindered (as "on Frodo", Q-0103); no test reads that.

**"While a card titled X is removed from the game" names no owner (F-0375, Gollum, Her Sneak 13_50).** `hasCardInRemoved` looks in one player's removed pile and defaults `player` to `you` (for a modifier, the card's owner); its code for every player's pile is never reached. A text with no "your" writes an `or` of the requirement as it stands and the same with `player: free people`, which is both players of a two-player game while the card is a minion. The test: the Free Peoples player's own Déagol 13_46 laid in his removed pile (`MoveCardToZone(card, Zone.REMOVED)`; in a mirror match his Gollum put it there), and the Shadow player's Gollum reads strength 7 and fierce. The sentence after it does say "in your discard pile", and is the owner's alone.

**"Loses <keyword> and cannot gain <keyword>" until a time is one `RemoveKeyword` with that `until` (Q-0011, Harrowdale 11_243).** A keyword removed until a time is also kept out until then, whether another card would give it or the card's own standing text would: Easterling Axeman 4_224 ("While you can spot 2 burdens, this minion is strength +3 and fierce") with 2 burdens put on after the skirmish that took fierce from him reads strength 9 and not fierce, is not offered in the fierce assignment, and is fierce at the first regroup decision. So a definition with no separate "cannot gain" is no finding; the ledger still cuts the sentence at its "and", and the second clause's test is a gain that comes AFTER the loss (`Card_11_243_Tests`, the last test). Into the Caves 17_100 ("lose all game text keywords of your choice and unable to gain game text keywords") is the one card where the keyword is CHOSEN, and its bar on gaining reaches every keyword (Q-0282: A, built by engine pass 30): `chooseAKeyword` with `fromCard: memory(chosenMinion)` and `noneOption: None` in a `doWhile` (the minion's own game text keywords and None, asked again until None or nothing is left; nothing asked of a minion with none), `removeKeyword` with `keywordFromMemory`, then `cantGainKeywords`, which keeps it to the game text keywords it then has and no more of one (a damage +1 made damage +1 again stays +1) until the regroup phase. Each use loses only the keywords named in it (F-0487, mended by engine pass 28).

**"While you can spot a <keyword> ..., this <card> is (gains) <that keyword>" with no "another" keeps the keyword once gained (Q-0276: A; built by engine pass 30).** Sentry Uruk 15_170 writes `spotsItself: true` on each `addKeyword`: once it has the keyword it is itself what the sentence spots, so it keeps it after the other card goes; alone from the start it has none, and once a card takes the keyword away or its text stops, it is not regained alone (Q-0291). Test the other card leaving play and a round the keyword decides (the fierce round). Ravaging Wild Man 15_91 has the same words and the same `spotsItself` (Q-0290: A, engine pass 30); no other definition has the shape.

**"Spot a race" is a choice of a character of that race; "name a race" is a choice of a race's name (Relentless Warg 17_89; Úlairë Nertëa 19_38).** "When you play this possession, spot a race" is `chooseActiveCards` of `character,hasRace` then `storeRaceFromCard` (CR 4.2 "spot"): companions, allies, the Ring-bearer and minions are all offered, every card with a race is a character, so no printed card is an invalid candidate; read the choice's minimum (1). "Name a race" is `chooseARace` then `storeRace`. Either stores nothing when cheated into play, so the tests play the card (pitfalls, "A card that names something as it is played").

**A bare type word in a COST names no side, on a Free Peoples card as on a Shadow card (Q-0058, F-0491, Q-0012).** "Skirmish: Discard a follower from play to make bearer strength +4" (Erkenbrand's Horn 18_96, built `choose(follower)`) may be paid with the Shadow player's follower in play (Saruman, Servant of Sauron 17_38): a literal reading the game already takes is no finding and no question. Lay the other side's card as a VALID candidate and read it offered (`Card_18_096_Tests.TheShadowPlayersFollowerMayBeDiscardedToPayForTheSkirmishAbility`). Gamling's Horn 18_100, Horn of Boromir 18_53 and Librarian 18_22 print the same words. An erratum that narrows to "your" (68_96) is its own text (dec-b11_244). A bare NAME in a cost is the same: "Exert Sméagol or Gollum" (Follow Sméagol 5_23, `choose(or(name(Sméagol),name(Gollum)))`) is paid by the Shadow player's Gollum, every card titled Gollum being a minion; lay him as a valid candidate (`Card_05_023_Tests`). Sméagol, Old Noser 5_28 is built the same ("Exert Sméagol (or Gollum) twice").

## What is offered and what is asked

**The no-effect flag: which cards assert it.** The no-effect flag exists (gemp-testing, "The no-effect flag"; test-generation, "Rulings, interactions and formats"): `FreepsActionNoEffect(card)` / `ShadowActionNoEffect(card)` (and with words of the action's text) answer "false", "effect", "limit" or "skirmish". Do NOT assert it on every card: only where the definition declares `noEffectUnless`, or the rulings log names the card for it. A no-cost ability or optional trigger that is offered though it would do nothing is still a finding (Q-0034), flag or no flag: since engine pass 26 a finding against the engine's list of effects (next entry). An ability WITH a cost is flagged only where the engine can tell that its effect will do nothing: "Exert Lurtz twice to assign it to an unbound companion" (Lurtz 18_118) with no unbound companion is offered (Q-0034) and, since engine pass 32 (an assignment says it will assign nobody), reads `"effect"` (mechanics/actions/NoEffectFlagTests); where the flag reads `"false"` on such a board that is still no finding, and nothing about the flag is asserted in a card's file. A ruling that names an ERRATUM for the flag does not reach the Decipher original whose definition has none, by the default of Q-0111: Q-0127's "so long as it's marking it as noeffect" is 52_101's, and Filibert Bolger 2_101, offered in a regular skirmish and reading `"false"`, asserts nothing about it (dec-b1_49).

**An optional trigger or ability that costs nothing and would do nothing is withheld by the engine (Q-0224: A2; Q-0231: B; engine pass 26).** The game itself (`IdleOffers`) does not offer an optional trigger (a killed, a removed-from-play and an added one too), a special ability or Response of a card in play, or an ability one card gives another, where it pays nothing (a spot, a choice or a number that is only remembered, a yes/no and a line in the log pay nothing; a limit on uses is no cost) and every one of its effects says it will do nothing. So an author no longer corrects a Q-0034 offer with a requirement. He lays the board on which the effect would do nothing, TESTS that the offer is not made, with what the game is waiting for instead (the `-` the Standard asks for), files nothing and writes no line in the definition. Where the offer IS still made on that board, the effect is of a kind the engine cannot ask: that is a finding as before (it names the kind, for the engine's list) and is corrected by a requirement as before. Kinds that say it of themselves: a heal, wound, exert, exhaust or discard from play with nobody to act on, a strength or keyword given to nobody, burdens or threats to remove where there are none, a skirmish to cancel where there is none; ## Limits

**Limits on uses and limits on what the effect gives, as built.** LIMITS, as built in this clone since 2026-10-07 (engine pass 8; Q-0041, Q-0082, Q-0093; hjson-reference "Limits" and test-generation's L check have the full text). A limit on USES ("limit once per turn", "limit once per phase", "limit N times": fields limitPerPhase / limitPerTurn / limitPerSite) WITHHOLDS the ability at its limit, cost or no cost: the test reads it not offered, with its cost still payable and something on the board the effect could still act on. A limit on WHAT THE EFFECT GIVES ("(limit +3)", "add a threat (limit 1)", "(limit 2 per phase)" where it counts things made: fields effectLimitPerPhase / effectLimitPerTurn) leaves an ability with a cost on offer at the limit, cost paid, nothing done, flagged `limit` with FreepsActionNoEffect / ShadowActionNoEffect, and that card gets the R clause for the flag. A cap written inside the effect (a CardPhaseLimit or CardAffectedLimitPerPhase value, limitPerCardThisPhase, or an `If` on a `perPhaseLimit` check whose `true` branch does `incrementPerPhaseLimit`, as Úlairë Attëa 1_229's "(limit +5)") also leaves the ability on offer. The engine sets the `limit` word for it only where the cap is on a strength bonus, on the amount of a keyword or on a number of cards drawn, and nothing else the action does would happen either (mechanics/actions/NoEffectFlagTests shows the first two; only 33_48 and 33_49 cap a draw); a cap on anything else is not read (Sting 51_313, a capped RemoveTwilight after a reveal, reads "false" at its cap). Either way the card asserts no flag and writes no R clause for it: that is the mechanic's (test-generation, "Rulings, interactions and formats"); test the cap itself under L. A definition whose field does not match its text's kind of limit is a finding, fixable at landing. Park nothing on Q-0093.

**"Limit once per site" (Q-0084, F-0146, F-0145).** "Limit once per site" is once for each site in a turn (Q-0084) and is the engine's since 2026-10-07: `limitPerSite: 1` on the ability or trigger (PerSiteLimit / IncrementPerSiteLimit written by hand build the same, which no card does and no test exercises), counted for the site the fellowship is at, cleared when it moves and at the end of the turn; an exertion on ARRIVING belongs to the site arrived at (F-0146). It is a limit on uses: withheld at the limit. A required trigger with a limit on uses is carried out no more often than its limit even when one event raises it twice at once (two companions exerting together draw a Cirith Ungol Watcher one card, F-0145). 103_92 and 103_103 are built so, and since engine pass 26 Stronghold of Minas Morgul 7_206 and Stronghold of Cirith Ungol 7_314, which counted under the regroup phase (F-0454, F-0455: no board told that from the count by site, the counter being cleared as each regroup phase ends, and each has a test that holds the limit over two sites of one turn). A definition still built from per-phase limits and MoveCountMinimum is a finding.

**"(limit +N)" is the card's for the whole phase (F-0135, F-0125).** "(limit +N)" is the card's for the whole phase: cumulative over uses (F-0135, a per-use cap is a finding) and counted per source card, not per target (F-0125, FIXED in this clone). A cap built as `cardAffectedLimitPerPhase` counts for each card affected wherever it is used outside ModifyStrength (on AddKeyword it gave each Dwarf its own +3 damage, F-0213): a "(limit +N)" keyword bonus built so is a finding, fixable at landing with a `cardPhaseLimit` (limit N, amount 1). Realm of Dwarrowdelf 2_12 is built so since engine pass 26, as its erratum was (F-0456).

**A printed "(limit (N))" or "(limit -N)" is the card's for the whole phase, but not on a play's discount;
"(limit X)" is per use (Q-0134, Q-0141, Q-0152).** A printed limit on a count inside an effect reads like
"(limit +N)" (F-0135): the card's limit for the whole phase. A cap built per use is a finding, fixed with a
cardPhaseLimit wrapper. A limit on a play's twilight discount is the exception ("its twilight cost is -1 for each
... (limit -2)", Uruk Regular 54_192): Ketura struck F-0245 on Q-0152 ("I'm not convinced that a restriction on
a discount is treated the same as a strength bonus limit"), so it is tested as built, a cap on each play (the
count's own `limit` in the `discount`), and a per-use cap there is no finding. A cardPhaseLimit is not written in
a `discount`: the engine works a discount out every time it asks what can be played, and the asking would spend
the limit. An event is one card played once, and an ability that discards its own card is used once by each copy
(Strength of Arms 4_82, Fortress Never Fallen 54_276), so the readings agree there: nothing is parked and a line
in Notes says so. A "(limit X)" where X is what that use paid caps that use only (Merry's Sword 57_242).

**A bar that a trigger lays "while at <a place>" has the place in it twice (92_35).** "While at a sanctuary, you
may heal no more than one wound from each character" is built as a trigger on each heal that lays a CantHeal on
the healed character for the turn. The place stands in the trigger's `requires` (which heals count) and in the
modifier's (where the bar holds), and each owes its own `-`: a heal away from the place earlier in the turn does
not use up the one wound (healed at site 2, then again at the sanctuary in the regroup phase), and a character
barred at the place is healed again once the fellowship has left it. With the second alone, the trigger's
requirement can go and no test notices. "You" stands in it twice as well (F-0319, built by engine pass 20):
`player: owner` on the `Heals` trigger (whose heals count) and on the CantHeal (whose heals the bar stops; with
no `player` it stops every heal of the card, whoever makes it). Each owes its own `-` on a card both players
heal, a minion: the Shadow player heals it and its owner then heals his one wound (Scaling Ladder 5_57, then
Hrethel 6_95), and the owner heals it and the Shadow player then heals it again (Hrethel, then the Ladder).

**"At the start of the first <phase> ... (limit once per turn)" (F-0517, Wizard's Vale 4_359).** "The first Shadow
phase" is the first Shadow PLAYER's phase, a gate against several Shadow players, not the turn's first move (Ketura,
2026-10-21): plain `startOfPhase` with the `perTurnLimit`, no `moveCountMinimum`. A site reached by the turn's second
move still adds (Card_04_359_Tests.ASecondMoveOfTheTurnToWizardsValeStillAddsFour). The limit binds nothing a board can
reach (no turn has two Shadow phases at one site), so the ledger writes `no L` with that reason.

**"Limit 1 per bearer" is a Restriction that lists `L`.** Typed `Restriction [+ - L]`, as on Frostbite 101_27
and The Weight of a Legacy 51_282. One test can claim all three: a bearer who has a copy is not
offered for the next copy; a bearer of a DIFFERENT card of the same kind (another condition of that culture,
which does not count against the limit) and a bearer of nothing are; and with every legal bearer bearing one the
card cannot be played though its other requirements are met (Card_01_282_ErrataTests).

**The CR 4.2 "loops" example (Sam, Faithful Companion 1_310; Q-0321).** The action has no limit: offered again after
its use in the same phase, and used again
(Card_01_310_Tests.FellowshipAbilityIsUsedAgainInTheSamePhaseAndWithNoBillInTheDeckPlaysNothing). A loop one player
makes alone in his own phase is the engine's (Ketura, answers/2026-10-16-c.md; engine pass 53,
`OnePlayerLoopTracker`): three uses on an unchanged board go unasked, and the fourth is "LOOP DETECTED: Choose a
number of times to repeat this action.", after which the ability is barred to the end of the phase. A card's own
file stays below four uses; the loop itself is shown in `mechanics/actions/OnePlayerLoopTests` (Sam, The Bridge of
Khazad-dûm 1_349, two Hobbit Party Guests 1_297).

The test reads the event as the top card of the draw deck after the play, the deck one larger, and the event's own effect carried out; a response with a cost is offered again for the same play while the cost can be paid (Q-0034), and a second use changes nothing more.

**"Each time you play a [culture] skirmish event" and an event printed with two phases (F-0292, built by engine pass 20).** An event printed with several phases is, while it is being played, an event of the phase it is played in and of no other (CR 4.2 "phase action"; Ketura: "Definitely a bug, go ahead and fix"). So `timeword(skirmish)` in a `played` trigger or response is false of Sword-wall 4_84 ("Skirmish or Regroup") played in the regroup phase and true of it played in a skirmish, with nothing more written in the definition: no `requires: phase` is wanted for it. On a card of the shape that such an event can reach (Skirmish with another phase: 1_315, 4_84, 4_110, 4_234, 5_30, 17_32, 103_6, 103_15, 103_16, 103_36, 103_69): assert the play in the named phase and the play in the other phase, both in active tests (Card_18_011_ErrataTests has both), and park nothing. A `modifyCost` written with the word reads the same way, before the play and during it ("Each skirmish event you play is twilight cost -1", Orc Skulker 12_95: nothing off Two-faced 103_15 in the maneuver phase; Q-0198, kept unless struck). A card that is NOT being played (in a discard pile or a hand: "take an [elven] skirmish event into hand", Gil-galad 68_12) is read by its printed words: Sword-wall is offered. An event printed with one phase is never narrowed, "Response" beside it or not (Eye of Barad-dûr 5_96, "Skirmish" and "Response", is a skirmish event and a response event however it is played). A definition that writes both the timeword and `requires: phase` (Orc Ambusher 1_261, "If the Free Peoples player plays a regroup event": `timeword(regroup)` and `phase: regroup`) cannot be told by Sword-wall from one with either half alone; what shows the timeword is a Free Peoples RESPONSE event played in that phase, which is not offered for (We Must Go Warily 3_48 answering the fellowship's regroup move; Card_01_261_Tests, dec-b1_261).

**"A special ability is used" against "a <phase> special ability is used" (Q-0039, Q-0119, Q-0203, F-0201).** `UsesSpecialAbility` with no `phase` answers every special ability, a Response ability included (Storied Homestead 13_76 against Isengard Shaman 3_59); with `phase: skirmish` it answers only abilities whose boldfaced word is Skirmish, so a Response ability used during a skirmish is not answered (War-warg 5_64; `mechanics/actions/ResponseAbilityIsASpecialAbilityTests`). The engine needed no change for this: F-0201 is not a fault. On a card of the first wording a Response ability is a valid candidate of F, and on one of the second it is the invalid candidate nearest the text (the Shaman, with an [isengard] Orc about to take a wound in the skirmish, serves for both). Boromir's Cloak 51_98 ("a skirmish event or special ability is used during a skirmish involving Boromir") is of the first wording (Q-0203: 1A, which replaced 1B of Q-0119): its trigger names no `phase`, and its `requires` (Boromir in a skirmish) is what holds it to the skirmish, so the Shaman's Response used there is answered and a "wins a skirmish" Response is too while Boromir is still in play (Uruk Bloodlust 1_144, read in a probe).

**A `DoWhile` is ended by what its effect did, never by what is left on the board (F-0322, Race Text 94_4).** "Transfer each possession attached to that companion to another eligible bearer" was a `Transfer` repeated in a `DoWhile` whose check spotted a possession still on the companion. One that nobody else may bear (Ranger's Sword 1_112) stays there, the check stays true, and the engine loops with nothing asked: the game hangs. The check is `MemoryMatches` on what the effect memorized (`memorizeTransferred: moved` on the Transfer; `memory: moved`, `filter: possession`): a choice with nothing to choose leaves the memory empty and the loop ends. The board for any "each X" built as a loop has an X the effect cannot act on beside one it can. A test of a hang is written `@Test(timeout = 20000)` (lint accepts it) and keeps the limit once the fault is fixed: without one it never returns when un-parked, and every test after it waits (`mutate.py` stops a run that has not returned in 180 seconds, `--timeout` to change it, and says so).

## Hinder, the support area, sites, Maps and the Ring

**"A card", "a Shadow card", "a Free Peoples card" may pick a hindered card (Q-0098).** RULED 2026-10-07 (Q-0098): an effect whose text picks "a card", "a Shadow card" or "a Free Peoples card" and nothing more specific may pick a HINDERED card; the definition's effect then needs `override: hindered` (see 103_93). Anything more specific (a type, culture, keyword, title) cannot reach a hindered card, and hinder choices still leave hindered cards out (Q-0060). A definition that misses the override is a finding the ruling covers: park the test on F-0000 and say it is fixable at landing; do not write a question.

**A card hindered as its own cost still carries its ability out (F-0151).** FIXED in this clone since 2026-10-07 (F-0151): a card that has hindered itself as a cost, or been hindered in answer to its ability, still carries the ability out and is still bound by every "cannot" (burdens cannot be added while the Ring-bearer is hindered, and the like). Assert that; park nothing on F-0151.

**Q-0098 as built: the words in a definition.** Q-0098 as built (engine passes 8 and 10): `override: hindered` on a Discard or ReturnToHand, `hindered: true` on ChooseActiveCards / Memorize / CanSpot / ForEachYouCanSpot, and the same word on the requirement that gates the offer. War Howdah 103_62 keeps the stacked Man's text and keywords as a snapshot (`snapshot: true` on DuplicateGameText and AddKeywordFromCards, F-0160).

**A number in a filter and a hindered card (CR 5.0 "hinder", Grond 8_103).** A hindered card has no strength, vitality, resistance or twilight cost to be found by ("he has no twilight cost at all"), and the game gets that from one thing only: a hindered card is not among the active cards a choice, a spot or a count looks through. The numbers themselves are still read from the print: under `override: hindered` or `hindered: true` a `minStrength(6)` or a `minVitality(4)` offers a hindered Aragorn 1_89 (probed, at-mech-hinder-a). So a text that names a number beside "a card" or a side ("a Free Peoples card (except a companion) with a twilight cost of X") is "more specific" in Q-0098's sense and its definition carries NO hindered override, as Grond's has none; no definition pairs the two today. `mechanics/hinder/HinderedCardHasNoStatsTests` holds Grond to the rules' own example (a hindered Elrond 1_40 at X = 4 and at X = 0).

**"Borne by a <race or type>" and a hindered bearer (Q-0108, F-0212; in the engine since engine pass 26, Q-0220: E).** A card on a HINDERED bearer is not "borne by a companion" or "borne by a Dwarf": a hindered card has no title, culture, type, race or number to be found by (CR 5.0 "hinder"). The engine now says so: `attachedTo(...)` finds a hindered bearer only where its one word names the bearer as a card (`self`, `bearer`, `memory(...)`, `ring-bearer`, `any`), so `attachedTo(Dwarf)`, `attachedTo(companion,inSkirmish)`, `attachedTo(name(Gandalf))`, `attachedTo(or(ally,and(unbound,companion)))` and `attachedTo(memory(loser),companion)` all leave one out. Write NO `not(hindered)` beside a kind, and file no finding for its absence. A second word of any sort beside one of those five leaves a hindered bearer out as well, so `attachedTo(memory(chosenCompanion),not(hindered))` (Gríma 54_154, 55_51) reads as it did. The `not(hindered)` written by hand in Gorgoroth Wastes 103_124 and Make Light of Burdens 52_11 stays and now says what the engine says: a mutation that takes it out survives, and that is no gap in their tests. The rule is shown in `mechanics/hinder/BorneByAHinderedBearerTests`; a Decipher card owes no hindered bearer of its own (Q-0237), and a PC card's test of one stays. A `not(...)` by itself is the exception (Q-0252, engine pass 28): `attachedTo(not(ally))` only rules a kind of bearer out and is asked of a hindered bearer as he is printed, so Asfaloth 1_31 goes from a hindered companion underground and stays on a hindered ally. Joined to another word (`attachedTo(companion,not(name(Frodo)))`) it is inside an `and` and leaves a hindered bearer out as before.

**"Support" and support(<whose>) (Q-0099, Q-0101).** Q-0099 is BUILT in this clone (engine pass 9): the filter `support` is a "support card": a card lying in a support area or borne, at any depth, by a card that is (what an ally or a controlled site bears; not what a companion or minion bears). **"Discard <X> he bears" beside a bar on X ("cannot bear", "may not be played on") is a standing discard, built as Shadowfax 17_24 is (Q-0223: B; Q-0229: C; built by engine pass 26).** The two sentences are two effects. **The One Ring is not discarded with a Ring-bearer who is killed: it lies where he was (Q-0232: "B+"; Q-0109; built by engine pass 26).** "The Ring-bearer and One Ring should never be discarded for any reason" (Q-0109) now holds against the rules' own "any cards ... borne by ... that card are discarded" (CR 4.2 "leaving play") as it does against every card: when the Ring-bearer is killed the cards he bore are discarded and the Ring is not. **"While bearer is at a <terrain> site", "if that <character> is at a <terrain>": the character's place is `atSite(<terrain>)`, and an ally is at his home site (F-0380, F-0503, F-0518; built by engine pass 48).** CR 4.2 "ally": "Each ally in your support area is considered to be at his home site.
