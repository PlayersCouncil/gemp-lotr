# Shapes already settled

What the campaign has learned about particular shapes of card text: what a ruling means for a definition and a test, what the engine now does, and what to read on the board. Each entry was first written in the orchestrator's notes to authors and moved here so that every phase has it. A definition that departs from an entry here is a finding (fixable at landing unless the entry says otherwise); do not raise these shapes as questions again. The words of the rulings themselves are in `rulings-log.md`; `rulings-index.md` lists both files' entries.

## How card text is read

**Any X of exertions is at least 1 (Q-0076).** Any X of exertions is at least 1: "exert X <cards>" as well as "exert X times" (Q-0076). X of zero by spotting, or of anything else, is still allowed.

**A card that makes itself a minion (Q-0077, Q-0078).** A card that makes itself a minion from the support area offers that ability from the support area only (Q-0077, the Bladetusks); its definition's requirement is right. A sentence after the ability that calls the card "she"/"it" as the minion is part of the ability (Q-0078).

**Reinforcing more than one token (F-0113).** Reinforcing more than one token is one token and one choice of card at a time (F-0113: `Repeat` round `ReinforceTokens`; `times:` on ReinforceTokens puts them all on one card and is a finding unless the text says "reinforce a token twice").

**"Its skirmish" on a minion (F-0114).** "Its skirmish" on a minion is the skirmish that minion is in; assigned and waiting, its text is not active (F-0114). A modifier counting over `InSkirmish` with only `filter: self` is a finding. So is one with a race or type filter and no `InSkirmish` of its own, wherever the text says "its skirmish" (F-0321, Race Text 93_8: `filter: wraith` gave every Wraith on the table the wounds of the skirmish being fought; now `wraith,InSkirmish`). The test is a card of the kind assigned and waiting while another skirmish with something to count is fought.

**"By another card" (Q-0079).** "By another card" in a card's text is a card other than THAT card (Q-0079, Corsair Helmsman 103_46): a [raider] minion hindering itself when played IS answered by a Helmsman.

**A spot that stands after "to" is an effect (Q-0085).** A spot that stands after "to" is an effect: the ability is offered though nothing can be spotted (Q-0085). A definition that has such a spot among its costs is a finding of its own.

**"Remove X ... to <do something to> a card costing X or less" (Q-0087, F-0133).** "Remove X ... to <do something to> a card costing X or less" has no cap on X but what there is to remove (Q-0087). A cap by "the highest cost" is a finding; ForEachTwilightCost over HighestTwilight adds up ties (F-0133).

**"The <other> player may make you <do X> to prevent this" (Q-0088).** "The <other> player may make you <do X> to prevent this": "you" chooses what X is done to (Q-0088). Built the other way is a finding.

**"A <thing>" is one (F-0136).** "A <thing>" is one: a choice of one, not all that match (F-0136).

**"The second time ... each Shadow phase" (F-0130).** "The second time ... each Shadow phase" needs a Shadow-phase requirement (F-0130): a finding where missing. So does "the first X played each Shadow phase" on a cost modifier (F-0390, Dimrill Dale 1_350: `{ type: phase, phase: shadow }` beside the `perPhaseLimit`, as Great Chasm 2_118 and Breeding Pit of Isengard 15_188 have it); the test is the same play in the regroup phase (the helper table, "A minion played outside a Shadow phase").

**"May X, and must Y for each X"; "copies of that card"; "is considered a <race>" (Q-0091, Q-0092).** "May X, and must Y for each X" is not a price: X is done, then Y as far as it can be (Q-0091). "Copies of that card" counts the card itself and goes by title (Q-0092). "Is considered a [culture] <race>" ADDS the race.

**"A minion" on a Skirmish event that reaches only minions in the skirmish (Q-0095).** "A minion" on a Skirmish event that reaches only minions in the skirmish: the TEXT is changed to "a skirmishing minion" (Q-0095, done on 103_84). On another card of the shape, say so as a finding of kind gametext, naming Q-0095.

**"Wound X twice" written as an exertion (F-0148).** RULED 2026-10-07 (F-0148, Take it Down! 103_89): "wound X twice" written as an exertion in a definition is a definition fault (a wound can kill and can fall on an exhausted character, an exertion cannot): fixable at landing.

**"Make allies take wounds from archery fire instead of companions" (Q-0136, Q-0137, Q-0147, F-0243, F-0244, F-0275).** Built with the one modifier `AlliesTakeArcheryFireWoundsInsteadOfCompanions` (Come Down 4_146 and 54_146), which the engine reads wound by wound: an ally while one can take it, otherwise as usual. Not with `AllyCanParticipateInArcheryFire` beside `CantTakeArcheryWounds` on companions: the first also counts an archer ally for the fellowship archery total, which "take wounds" does not say, and the second loses the wounds no ally can take. The ability has no cost, so its definition requires an ally it can send a wound to (`canSpot` with `ally,not(barredFromArcheryFire)`; Q-0034), and tests show it withheld with no ally in play and with only a barred one. An ally who "may not participate in archery fire" (Streets of Edoras 4_340, `AllyMayNotParticipateInArcheryFireOrSkirmishes`) takes none of these wounds: a bar on participating bars taking archery wounds as well as adding to the total (Q-0147, F-0275). The test of that pair of cards is an `Interaction` clause owed in 4_340's file when that card is done (Streets is the card named first), with a line in each Come Down's Notes; `mechanics/AlliesTakeArcheryWoundsTests` shows it meanwhile.

**"Participates in archery fire and skirmishes" against "may not participate" (Q-0150, F-0281).** Nothing for a definition to say: the engine answers that no card allows an ally another card bars (`AllyMayNotParticipateInArcheryFireOrSkirmishes`, Streets of Edoras 4_340 only), so `AllyCanParticipate...`, `EnableParticipation...` and an assignment with `ignoreAllyLocation` (Breaching Shot 102_21) all pass a barred ally over, for the archery total, archery wounds and assignment by either player or by an effect; the rest of the granting card (a strength bonus) still happens. Not a finding against the granting card. The test of a granting card with the Streets is the Interaction clause owed in 4_340's file (Streets is the card named first), with a line in the other card's Notes; `mechanics/AllyBarredFromTakingPartTests` shows the rule, and 53_42 and 102_21 each carry one test of it as an `R` clause, written by engine pass 18. A no-cost ability whose whole effect is to have an ally fight and assign its minion to him (Dunlending Ravager 4_15, Dunlending Warrior 4_18) is withheld when it could assign the minion to nobody: only a barred ally in play, every ally already assigned, the minion itself already assigned, or a minion that is not fierce in the fierce round (F-0283: A; built by engine pass 21). Its requirement and its choice are both `ally,AssignableToSkirmishAgainstIgnoringAllyLocation(self)` (hjson-reference), so only allies it could be assigned to are offered; `mechanics/MinionThatAssignsItselfToAnAllyTests` and the Warrior's test in `AllyBarredFromTakingPartTests` show it. Assert it withheld on those boards and park nothing.

**"Choose X ... (or you may play it ...)" plays the card chosen (F-0152).** RULED 2026-10-07 (F-0152, Ominous Sky 103_97, fixed): "choose X ... (or you may play it ...)" plays the card chosen; a definition that asks a second time which card is a finding, fixable at landing.

**Ruled 2026-10-08 and built by engine pass 15 (F-0197, F-0196, Q-0115).** F-0197 (Q-0113): "If a minion loses this skirmish to that <character>, <effect>" is ONE effect however many minions lose (read as "at the end of this skirmish, if at least 1 minion lost ..."); built with `limitPerPhase: 1` on the `addTrigger` (1_29, 51_29), and a definition of that shape without it is a finding. F-0196 (Q-0112): a shuffle FROM PLAY of "any number" with none chosen shuffles the deck all the same; Mumakil Commander 18_71 requires a [men] mount of his to be spotted. Assert both; park nothing on them. Q-0115: a Decipher ruling about a WORD ("free") does not reach an erratum that dropped the word.

**"While ..., the move limit is +1 (-1)" with no "for this turn" lasts the turn (Q-0179: B; F-0333, F-0334).** CR 4.2 "move limit": a change to the move limit "is in effect for the whole turn, even if the conditions for the modification change". Decipher's cards are built so whether they say "for this turn" or not, as a once-a-turn trigger that adds the modifier `until: endOfTurn` (The Number Must Be Few 1_260, Derndingle 4_330, Radagast 9_26, Théoden 13_137 "While you can spot Théodred, the move limit is +1"). Race Text 91_8 and 91_12 were a plain `ModifyMoveLimit` with a `requires`, which turns off and on with its condition; Ketura ruled that they "follow the basic rules around move limits, which is that they are permanent", and both are now 1_260's trigger (`ConstantlyCheck` with `PerTurnLimit` 1 and the condition; `IncrementPerTurnLimit`, then `AddModifier` ... `until: EndOfTurn`). The one plain `ModifyMoveLimit` with a `requires` left is 51_260, by its own second sentence (Q-0122); another that turns up with no such sentence is a finding, not a question. Its tests: the condition ended in the regroup phase and the limit read unchanged; a D test in which the condition ends in the fellowship phase (Saved From the Fire 8_20), the fellowship moves twice and the limit is read in the Shadow phase after the second move, then on the other player's turn (a -1 needs Durin's Secret 1_71 first, to have a second move at all); the change given in the fellowship phase read again in the regroup phase (with no `until` it ends with the phase it was given in); and the limit read over three turns with the condition holding throughout, since "once a turn" is the build's and not the text's. The amount itself is shown on a board the floor of 1 cannot hide (pitfalls, "The move limit never reads below 1"). A +1 that a trigger buys ("Each time your fellowship moves, you may kill a companion ... to make the move limit +1", Race Text 93_3) is the turn's as well and writes `until: endOfTurn`: `until: end(regroup)` was gone once the second move was made, and no kill bought a third (F-0317, fixed). Its D test kills on the first move, declines on the second, reads 3 in the Shadow phase after it, makes the third move, and reads 2 on the next turn. The standing check is a required action that is ordered beside others of its moment and run against its condition again as it is carried out, so a +1 is lost when the card it spots leaves play ahead of it (F-0389, open, the engine's: Rivendell Waterfall 1_342 with a ranger whom Blade Tip 1_209 kills at the start of the fellowship phase, his death taken first). On another card of this build that is the same finding: park the one test of that order on F-0389, keep the order that gives the +1 active, and write no second trigger into the definition to get round it.

**"<Do> N ... If you do, ..." wants all N (Q-0169, F-0330).** "If you do" asks for what the sentence before it says: where that sentence takes 2 cards, as much as can be done is still done with one (CR 4.2 "effect"), and the "If you do" part does not follow. A New Light 67_15 ("choose 2 minions, then return those minions ... If you do, reveal that hand ...") checks `ForEachInMemory` `IsGreaterThan` 1; a check of "more than 0" copied from a card that takes one (17_15) is the fault. Its test lays exactly N-1 candidates and reads the first part done and the second not.

**"Frodo is corrupted" on a card that names Frodo (F-0356, Held 6_109).** `corruptRingBearer` takes no filter: it corrupts the Ring-bearer whoever he is. Where the text names Frodo the trigger carries `requires: canSpot` with `ring-bearer,title(Frodo)`, the card that is both (Ketura, Q-0213 row L). Two lone tests: borne by Frodo the Ring-bearer the move to site 9 ends the game (read from the game log); borne by Frodo, Frenzied Fighter 13_149 beside `GimliRB` it corrupts nobody and the Shadow phase at site 9 follows. A hindered Frodo cannot be spotted, so he is not corrupted while hindered (as "on Frodo", Q-0103); no test reads that.

**"While a card titled X is removed from the game" names no owner (F-0375, Gollum, Her Sneak 13_50).** `hasCardInRemoved` looks in one player's removed pile and defaults `player` to `you` (for a modifier, the card's owner); its code for every player's pile is never reached. A text with no "your" writes an `or` of the requirement as it stands and the same with `player: free people`, which is both players of a two-player game while the card is a minion. The test: the Free Peoples player's own Déagol 13_46 laid in his removed pile (`MoveCardToZone(card, Zone.REMOVED)`; in a mirror match his Gollum put it there), and the Shadow player's Gollum reads strength 7 and fierce. The sentence after it does say "in your discard pile", and is the owner's alone.

## What is offered and what is asked

**The no-effect flag: which cards assert it.** The no-effect flag exists (gemp-testing, "The no-effect flag"; test-generation, "Rulings, interactions and formats"): `FreepsActionNoEffect(card)` / `ShadowActionNoEffect(card)` (and with words of the action's text) answer "false", "effect", "limit" or "skirmish". Do NOT assert it on every card: only where the definition declares `noEffectUnless`, or the rulings log names the card for it. A no-cost ability or optional trigger that is offered though it would do nothing is still a finding (Q-0034), flag or no flag: since engine pass 26 a finding against the engine's list of effects (next entry).

**An optional trigger or ability that costs nothing and would do nothing is withheld by the engine (Q-0224: A2; Q-0231: B; engine pass 26).** The game itself (`IdleOffers`) does not offer an optional trigger (a killed, a removed-from-play and an added one too), a special ability or Response of a card in play, or an ability one card gives another, where it pays nothing (a spot, a choice or a number that is only remembered, a yes/no and a line in the log pay nothing; a limit on uses is no cost) and every one of its effects says it will do nothing. So an author no longer corrects a Q-0034 offer with a requirement. He lays the board on which the effect would do nothing, TESTS that the offer is not made, with what the game is waiting for instead (the `-` the Standard asks for), files nothing and writes no line in the definition. Where the offer IS still made on that board, the effect is of a kind the engine cannot ask: that is a finding as before (it names the kind, for the engine's list) and is corrected by a requirement as before. Kinds that say it of themselves: a heal, wound, exert, exhaust or discard from play with nobody to act on, a strength or keyword given to nobody, burdens or threats to remove where there are none, a skirmish to cancel where there is none; a take into hand from a discard pile, a hinder, threats or a burden that cannot be added, a site that cannot be taken or liberated; and the kinds taught for this rule: reinforcing a token, a transfer (a card that goes "from your support area" only while it lies there: F-0407), an assignment to a skirmish (by the effect's own choice, which leaves defender out), making an ally take part in skirmishes, stacking from play and from a discard pile, a restore, putting on the Ring, a discard from the top of a draw deck (empty, or barred to that card by another: F-0408), a card in play or in a discard pile chosen for a later effect, a `DoWhile`, a number named, a discard at random from a hand (empty, or barred to that card by another: Frodo, Courteous Halfling 4_301, Q-0231), and, since the sweep of engine pass 26 (F-0402 to F-0406): twilight removed as an effect from an empty pool, a card stacked from hand, a chosen discard from hand, a card placed from a hand on top of a draw deck (each with no card in that hand to take; of ANOTHER player's hand only an empty one is said), and a card placed from a discard pile beneath a draw deck. Known NOT to, so these keep their requirement or get one: an amount counted from the board that comes to zero ("add a threat for each ...", "draw a card for each ...": Q-0033; corrected by a requirement that spots one of what is counted, or N+1 for "over N": F-0411 to F-0417), a number the player names and the game remembers ahead of a `Repeat` that reads it ("wound a minion for each unbound Hobbit you spot", Aragorn, Wingfoot 4_364, F-0428: neither nobody to count nor nobody to wound is seen through it, so two requirements, `canSpot` of what is counted and `canSpot` of what the repeated effect chooses from, with a test for each), a card placed from a hand BENEATH a draw deck or revealed from a hand (Mallorn-trees 1_54, unworked and not corrected), a card placed from a discard pile on TOP of a draw deck, a kill (Úlairë Cantëa 12_174, F-0413), a search of a draw deck (Wolf Pit 102_36; Q-0104), `storeWhileInZone` and anything else that only records (Feel His Blade 7_177, F-0410), a standing modifier with nothing to act on (Come Down 4_146; the table-wide flag Saruman's Staff 4_174 throws, with no card in play that reads it: F-0426), a card's own arithmetic (A Wizard Knows Better 103_40, Elrond 53_13), a culture token placed where a card bars tokens (Secret Folk 4_34 at Redhorn Pass 13_194: F-0423, confirmed and the engine's, since no requirement reads the bar; test the offer absent, parked on F-0423, and file nothing new for the same sentence on another card, Stout and Strong 4_57). No fault, and so nothing to write: a card SHUFFLED from a discard pile into a draw deck with none to shuffle in (the deck is shuffled: Q-0102; whether "shuffle A card", with no "up to", is the same is asked of Hatred Stirred 11_210: Q-0243, open; it is offered as built), a keyword given to a card that has it already (Band of Wild Men 4_4 offered fierce again at a second win: Q-0245, open; test it offered as built, and write no "not fierce already" requirement, which is wrong under Hillman Horde 4_22), an ability whose own words discard the card it is on ("... Discard this condition": Secret Folk 4_34, Good Work 4_305), and The One Ring's own Response. NEVER withheld: an event, a play from hand, an ability used from a discard pile, a draw deck or a stack, anything with a cost, an action whose definition writes `noEffectUnless`, an optional draw or reveal that fails only because a draw deck is empty (Q-0029, Q-0104), a draw that the Rule of 4 stops (the draw's forecast reads the rule only as the draw is about to be made, for a prevention, and leaves an action on offer as it was: `DrawCards.forecast`; whether such an offer should be made was never asked, and Barrows of Edoras 4_337 keeps it off the menu by hand, F-0429), and a "you may" INSIDE another action (`Optional`: taken out by A2, so F-0379's shape is still corrected by hand). The requirements written before the rule stay; one is still wanted where it says what no effect can (Desolation of Ash 103_122, Q-0107). `mechanics/AnOfferThatCostsNothingAndWouldDoNothingIsNotMadeTests` has each taught kind both ways. `GEMP_IDLE_OFFERS=off` before a `cardtest.py run` turns the rule off for that run: a "not offered" test that still passes rests on a requirement or on another rule, not on this one.

**A choice whose every branch has nothing to act on (F-0116).** A choice whose every branch has nothing to act on is not put to the player (F-0116): a pointless prompt is a finding. Since engine pass 19 (F-0293) the word `choice` withholds the question itself where every branch is a single effect that can say it will do nothing as it comes up (exert, heal, wound, discard, hinder, modifyStrength, addKeyword, removeBurdens, removeThreats, addBurdens, addThreats, drawCards, returnToHand, liberateSite, takeControlOfSite and their like): such a definition needs no guard, and the test asserts that "Choose action to perform" is not asked and nothing happened. A branch of several effects, one that holds others (`if`, `costToEffect`, `multiple`, a nested `choice`) and one whose effect cannot tell (a play from a pile, `chooseActiveCards`) still ask: there the prompt is still a finding, mended in the definition by an `If` around the choice (Savage Fury 103_39). `takeOffRing` could not tell before engine pass 25 (F-0370), which is why "to take off The One Ring or to cancel a skirmish involving the Ring-bearer and a Nazgûl" (O Elbereth! Gilthoniel! 2_108, F-0366) is the choice inside an `If` on `RingIsOn`, with the cancel alone as its `false`. It now says that it will do nothing while the Ring is not on, so the plain two-branch choice asks nothing where neither branch can act (tried on 2_108, whose seven tests pass with it); 2_108 keeps its `If`, which does no harm. The cancel wants no `If` of its own: `cancelSkirmish` with `involving` says by itself when it can do nothing, nobody of the kind in the skirmish and the format's bar on the Ring-bearer's skirmish included, and an `If` round it only takes that away.

**"<Do X> or <do Y>": each branch is written as the effect itself (F-0310, Here Lies Balin 1_19).** Ketura: a choice must be one the player is "fully capable" of performing; where neither branch is, "the player should be given a choice and attempt to perform either branch"; with nothing to do on either, nothing is asked. The word `choice` already does all three (it has since before the campaign; landed tests assert it: Boromir 59_31, Isildur 59_33, Uruk Rager 51_150, A Shadow of the Past 101_49), from what each branch says of itself. So a branch is the effect itself (`wound` with `times: 2`: Here Lies Balin 1_19, Deep Hatred 13_3), never a `chooseActiveCards` whose effect stands after the choice: such a branch answers only "can a card be picked", so it is offered when it cannot be done in full and cannot say that it will do nothing. A definition of that shape is a finding. The tests of an "X or Y" card lay the board where one branch cannot be done in full (it is not offered, and the other is carried out with no question) and, where one exists, the board where neither can (both are offered, and what can be done of the one chosen is done). Where a branch is "wound X twice" with X chosen, the board for B and F lays an X of 1 vitality beside ones able to take both wounds and reads it as not offered once that branch is taken: the picking is an extension of the choice (Q-0209: B, built by engine pass 23 in `Choice` and `Wound`; the definition says nothing for it, and a vitality filter there would be a finding, since it would also bar the X of 1 vitality on the board where neither branch can be done in full). A choice of "pay A to X, or pay B to Y" goes by the costs alone (F-0222, below).

**A parenthesis with its own price (Q-0089).** A parenthesis with its own price the player elects to pay ("(or X if you also exert ...)") is the player's choice (Q-0089). Do not ask.

**One thing left to do is done without a second question (Q-0090).** Where taking an offer leaves only one thing to do, it is done without a second question (Q-0090). A second yes/no there is a finding. "When you play X, for each <thing> over N, you may play 1 ... from your discard pile" (Úlairë Nertëa 51_234, Q-0121; the original 1_234, F-0387): the trigger's offer is the first play, a `playCardFromDiscard` whose choice is exactly one, and each later play is the same effect with `optional: true` inside a `Repeat` over N+1, a choice of none or one. Each is a "may" of its own: a play declined does not end the later ones, and only a board with three plays (N+3) and the second declined tells that from a definition that stops at the first "no". A test of another card that answered the old yes/no loses those lines (Ford of Bruinen 1_338's did).

**A yes/no before a choice whose minimum is 0 (Q-0143, F-0277).** "<Player> may <do X to> any number of / up to N cards" built as a yes/no (`Optional`) and then a choice of 0 to N lets the player say no twice, and is a finding: the choice is asked alone, and its text says plainly that none need be chosen (Ketura: "let's get rid of the pop-up and just make sure the message displayed to the user is sufficiently clear"). Read the choice's minimum of 0 and, where the definition gives the choice a `text` of its own, the words that say so (`FreepsDecisionAvailable("you may choose none")`, The Palantir of Orthanc 53_67); no other wording of the game's is an expectation. Taking the `Optional` away takes its context away too: what was inside it ran as the player it named, so check afterwards who is asked each further thing (53_67's order of the cards on the deck went to the wrong player until `PutCardsFromHandOnTopOfDeck` was mended). Gwaihir 101_15 was the other such card (F-0278): `putCardsFromDiscardIntoHand` takes a `text` for it, and a choice from a discard pile is asked in a definition's own `text` only since engine pass 18 (F-0285; with none it reads "Choose card from discard"). With no card to choose from, a 0-to-N choice is not asked at all. NOT this shape: an optional trigger's own yes/no before "shuffle up to N ... into your draw deck" (Orkish Smith 61_132). Its two noes differ under Q-0102 (declined: no shuffle; taken with none chosen: the deck is shuffled), so both steps stay and no finding is raised. An optional trigger's own offer before a choice of 0 to N whose two noes end the SAME way ("each Hobbit companion may heal", Valley of the Silverlode 2_120; Longbottom Leaf 1_300; Uruk-hai Healer 14_14) is F-0385, a candidate asked of Ketura and open: read the game as built, keep the test active, name that number and file nothing again.

**A number that can only be one number is not asked for (F-0147).** A number that can only be one number is not asked for (F-0147, in this clone): where X runs from 1 to 1, or "how many" has one answer, the game takes it. Do not answer such a question in a test (the answer would go to the NEXT decision); to read the bounds of an X, arrange a board where two numbers can be named. A correction that lifts a choice's minimum to its maximum takes the question away at that board: an old test that answers the choice there cannot be driven both before and after the correction, so its play at that board is parked in the form it will have afterwards (all taken, nothing asked) and the same play is kept active on a board that leaves a choice (Power and Terror 2_70, F-0392).

**"You may make <the other player> do X" (Q-0106).** Q-0106 (ruled): "you may make the <other player> do X" is asked of "you" as a yes/no, only when X can be done, and the other player still makes the choices the text gives him. Where the game never asks, that is an error: fix or park it as a finding, not a question.

**Known shapes from the Dwarven errata (F-0181, F-0194, F-0182).** KNOWN SHAPES from the Dwarven errata, each still a finding on its card (show the correction; it is fixed at landing): (1) "the first time <X happens> each turn" built as PerTurnLimit + IncrementPerTurnLimit is counted only when the offer is TAKEN, so a declined offer comes again; the requirement `playedCardThisTurn` (filter, max 1) is the right word (F-0181). (2) A `preventable` after a choice that can come up empty was asked "to prevent NONE"; since engine pass 14 the engine itself does not ask (F-0194), so this is no longer a finding. (3) An optional trigger with no cost whose whole effect would do nothing is to be withheld by a requirement (Q-0034; F-0182); for "you may take a <card> into hand from your discard pile" the requirement is `HasCardInDiscard` with the choice's own filter (Beorning Axe 68_18 and 18_18, F-0295: taking the empty offer also spent its once per turn). For "you may transfer this <card> to a <kind>" it is `canSpot` with the kind and `not(hasAttached(self))` (Brought Down from Inside 69_24 and 19_24, F-0304: the `transfer` effect leaves the present bearer out of its choice by itself, with or without `another` in `where`, and `checkTarget` narrows the choice without gating the offer). ## Limits

**Limits on uses and limits on what the effect gives, as built.** LIMITS, as built in this clone since 2026-10-07 (engine pass 8; Q-0041, Q-0082, Q-0093; hjson-reference "Limits" and test-generation's L check have the full text). A limit on USES ("limit once per turn", "limit once per phase", "limit N times": fields limitPerPhase / limitPerTurn / limitPerSite) WITHHOLDS the ability at its limit, cost or no cost: the test reads it not offered, with its cost still payable and something on the board the effect could still act on. A limit on WHAT THE EFFECT GIVES ("(limit +3)", "add a threat (limit 1)", "(limit 2 per phase)" where it counts things made: fields effectLimitPerPhase / effectLimitPerTurn) leaves an ability with a cost on offer at the limit, cost paid, nothing done, flagged `limit` with FreepsActionNoEffect / ShadowActionNoEffect, and that card gets the R clause for the flag. A cap written inside the effect (a CardPhaseLimit or CardAffectedLimitPerPhase value, limitPerCardThisPhase) also leaves the ability on offer. The engine sets the `limit` word for it only where the cap is on a strength bonus, on the amount of a keyword or on a number of cards drawn, and nothing else the action does would happen either (mechanics/NoEffectFlagTests shows the first two; only 33_48 and 33_49 cap a draw); a cap on anything else is not read (Sting 51_313, a capped RemoveTwilight after a reveal, reads "false" at its cap). Either way the card asserts no flag and writes no R clause for it: that is the mechanic's (test-generation, "Rulings, interactions and formats"); test the cap itself under L. A definition whose field does not match its text's kind of limit is a finding, fixable at landing. Park nothing on Q-0093.

**"Limit once per site" (Q-0084, F-0146, F-0145).** "Limit once per site" is once for each site in a turn (Q-0084) and is the engine's since 2026-10-07: `limitPerSite: 1` on the ability or trigger (or PerSiteLimit / IncrementPerSiteLimit), counted for the site the fellowship is at, cleared when it moves and at the end of the turn; an exertion on ARRIVING belongs to the site arrived at (F-0146). It is a limit on uses: withheld at the limit. A required trigger with a limit on uses is carried out no more often than its limit even when one event raises it twice at once (two companions exerting together draw a Cirith Ungol Watcher one card, F-0145). 103_92 and 103_103 are built so. A definition still built from per-phase limits and MoveCountMinimum is a finding.

**"(limit +N)" is the card's for the whole phase (F-0135, F-0125).** "(limit +N)" is the card's for the whole phase: cumulative over uses (F-0135, a per-use cap is a finding) and counted per source card, not per target (F-0125, FIXED in this clone). A cap built as `cardAffectedLimitPerPhase` counts for each card affected wherever it is used outside ModifyStrength (on AddKeyword it gave each Dwarf its own +3 damage, F-0213): a "(limit +N)" keyword bonus built so is a finding, fixable at landing with a `cardPhaseLimit` (limit N, amount 1).

**A printed "(limit (N))" or "(limit -N)" is the card's for the whole phase, but not on a play's discount;
"(limit X)" is per use (Q-0134, Q-0141, Q-0152).** A printed limit on a count inside an effect reads like
"(limit +N)" (F-0135): the card's limit for the whole phase. A cap built per use is a finding, fixed with a
cardPhaseLimit wrapper. A limit on a play's twilight discount is the exception ("its twilight cost is -1 for each
... (limit -2)", Uruk Regular 54_192): Ketura struck F-0245 on Q-0152 ("I'm not convinced that a restriction on
a discount is treated the same as a strength bonus limit"), so it is tested as built, a cap on each play (the
count's own `limit` in the `discount`), and a per-use cap there is no finding. A cardPhaseLimit is not written in
a `discount`: the engine works a discount out every time it asks what can be played, and the asking would spend
the limit. An event is one card played once, and an ability that discards its own card is used once by each copy
(Strength of Arms 4_82, Fortress Never Fallen 54_276), so the readings agree there: nothing is parked and a line
in Notes says so. A "(limit X)" where X is what that use paid caps that use only (Merry's Sword 57_242).

**A bar that a trigger lays "while at <a place>" has the place in it twice (92_35).** "While at a sanctuary, you
may heal no more than one wound from each character" is built as a trigger on each heal that lays a CantHeal on

**"Make each ... until" is a snapshot on an event; on an ability only where the cards it names are on the table when it is used (Q-0011, F-0144, F-0217).** The snapshot is the rule for EVENTS (CR 4.2 "event"): a "make each ... until" or "each of your ... gain(s) ... until" on an event reaches only the cards in play when it is played. An ABILITY of a card in play is not held to it by the rules (Ketura on F-0217, 2026-10-09: "As a non-event, it's not beholden to the snapshot rule RAW"). Before calling a standing filter on an ability a finding, ask whether the cards it names can be on the table when it is used.

**Keywords copied "until ..." are a snapshot (Q-0096); race and "unloaded keywords" (Q-0097).** Keywords copied from a card "until ..." are a SNAPSHOT taken when the ability is used (Q-0096, built: `snapshot: true` on AddKeywordFromCards inside an AddModifier; a definition of that wording without it is a finding, fixable at landing). **"Exert X twice" sets off an "each time ... exerts" trigger twice.** An "exert X twice" is two exertions, one after the other, and each is an event that can be answered (CR 4.2 "cost": Unheeded answers Grishnákh's first exertion before his second). Such a trigger's `R` has one test in which a single effect exerts one character twice, beside the test of two separate exertions. Two characters exerting at once is another matter (F-0145, under Limits).

**"Place that event on top of (beneath) your draw deck" on a card that answers the play of an event (F-0287).** `putPlayedEventOnTopOfDrawDeck` or `putPlayedEventOnBottomOfDrawDeck` with `filter: played`, as the trigger's one effect and with no `memorize` (built by engine pass 19; Shrouded Elf 63_23 and 13_23). The test reads the event as the top card of the draw deck after the play, the deck one larger, and the event's own effect carried out; a response with a cost is offered again for the same play while the cost can be paid (Q-0034), and a second use changes nothing more.

**"Each time you play a [culture] skirmish event" and an event printed with two phases (F-0292, built by engine pass 20).** An event printed with several phases is, while it is being played, an event of the phase it is played in and of no other (CR 4.2 "phase action"; Ketura: "Definitely a bug, go ahead and fix"). A `modifyCost` written with the word reads the same way, before the play and during it ("Each skirmish event you play is twilight cost -1", Orc Skulker 12_95: nothing off Two-faced 103_15 in the maneuver phase; Q-0198, kept unless struck). A card that is NOT being played (in a discard pile or a hand: "take an [elven] skirmish event into hand", Gil-galad 68_12) is read by its printed words: Sword-wall is offered. An event printed with one phase is never narrowed, "Response" beside it or not (Eye of Barad-dûr 5_96, "Skirmish" and "Response", is a skirmish event and a response event however it is played).

**"A special ability is used" against "a <phase> special ability is used" (Q-0039, Q-0119, Q-0203, F-0201).** `UsesSpecialAbility` with no `phase` answers every special ability, a Response ability included (Storied Homestead 13_76 against Isengard Shaman 3_59); with `phase: skirmish` it answers only abilities whose boldfaced word is Skirmish, so a Response ability used during a skirmish is not answered (War-warg 5_64; `mechanics/ResponseAbilityIsASpecialAbilityTests`). The engine needed no change for this: F-0201 is not a fault. On a card of the first wording a Response ability is a valid candidate of F, and on one of the second it is the invalid candidate nearest the text (the Shaman, with an [isengard] Orc about to take a wound in the skirmish, serves for both). Boromir's Cloak 51_98 ("a skirmish event or special ability is used during a skirmish involving Boromir") is of the first wording (Q-0203: 1A, which replaced 1B of Q-0119): its trigger names no `phase`, and its `requires` (Boromir in a skirmish) is what holds it to the skirmish, so the Shaman's Response used there is answered and a "wins a skirmish" Response is too while Boromir is still in play (Uruk Bloodlust 1_144, read in a probe).

**A `DoWhile` is ended by what its effect did, never by what is left on the board (F-0322, Race Text 94_4).** "Transfer each possession attached to that companion to another eligible bearer" was a `Transfer` repeated in a `DoWhile` whose check spotted a possession still on the companion. One that nobody else may bear (Ranger's Sword 1_112) stays there, the check stays true, and the engine loops with nothing asked: the game hangs. The check is `MemoryMatches` on what the effect memorized (`memorizeTransferred: moved` on the Transfer; `memory: moved`, `filter: possession`): a choice with nothing to choose leaves the memory empty and the loop ends. The board for any "each X" built as a loop has an X the effect cannot act on beside one it can. A test of a hang is written `@Test(timeout = 20000)` (lint accepts it) and keeps the limit once the fault is fixed: without one it never returns when un-parked, and every test after it waits (`mutate.py` stops a run that has not returned in 180 seconds, `--timeout` to change it, and says so).

## Hinder, the support area, sites, Maps and the Ring

**"A card", "a Shadow card", "a Free Peoples card" may pick a hindered card (Q-0098).** RULED 2026-10-07 (Q-0098): an effect whose text picks "a card", "a Shadow card" or "a Free Peoples card" and nothing more specific may pick a HINDERED card; the definition's effect then needs `override: hindered` (see 103_93). Anything more specific (a type, culture, keyword, title) cannot reach a hindered card, and hinder choices still leave hindered cards out (Q-0060). A definition that misses the override is a finding the ruling covers: park the test on F-0000 and say it is fixable at landing; do not write a question.

**A card hindered as its own cost still carries its ability out (F-0151).** FIXED in this clone since 2026-10-07 (F-0151): a card that has hindered itself as a cost, or been hindered in answer to its ability, still carries the ability out and is still bound by every "cannot" (burdens cannot be added while the Ring-bearer is hindered, and the like). Assert that; park nothing on F-0151.

**Q-0098 as built: the words in a definition.** Q-0098 as built (engine passes 8 and 10): `override: hindered` on a Discard or ReturnToHand, `hindered: true` on ChooseActiveCards / Memorize / CanSpot / ForEachYouCanSpot, and the same word on the requirement that gates the offer. "All Shadow cards borne by ..." and "each Free Peoples card borne by ..." reach hindered cards as "a Shadow card" does (Ketura: "Good call on all/each"). **A number in a filter and a hindered card (CR 5.0 "hinder", Grond 8_103).** A hindered card has no strength, vitality, resistance or twilight cost to be found by ("he has no twilight cost at all"), and the game gets that from one thing only: a hindered card is not among the active cards a choice, a spot or a count looks through. The numbers themselves are still read from the print: under `override: hindered` or `hindered: true` a `minStrength(6)` or a `minVitality(4)` offers a hindered Aragorn 1_89 (probed, at-mech-hinder-a). So a text that names a number beside "a card" or a side ("a Free Peoples card (except a companion) with a twilight cost of X") is "more specific" in Q-0098's sense and its definition carries NO hindered override, as Grond's has none; no definition pairs the two today. `mechanics/HinderedCardHasNoStatsTests` holds Grond to the rules' own example (a hindered Elrond 1_40 at X = 4 and at X = 0).

**"Borne by a <race or type>" and a hindered bearer (Q-0108, F-0212).** Q-0108 is built in the definitions, not in the engine: `AttachedTo(Dwarf)` (or any race or type) still finds a HINDERED bearer, though a hindered card has no race or type (CR 5.0 "hinder"). Write `AttachedTo(Dwarf,not(hindered))`, as Gorgoroth Wastes 103_124 and Make Light of Burdens 52_11 are built; a definition without it is a finding, fixable at landing: test a card borne by a hindered bearer of the right race beside one borne by an unhindered one.

**"Support" and support(<whose>) (Q-0099, Q-0101).** Q-0099 is BUILT in this clone (engine pass 9): the filter `support` is a "support card": a card lying in a support area or borne, at any depth, by a card that is (what an ally or a controlled site bears; not what a companion or minion bears). Assert it: a Shadow condition on an ally is a valid candidate for "a Shadow support card", the same card on a companion the invalid one beside it (mechanics/SupportAreaTests has the boards). `zone(support)` is the zone alone ("transfer this from your support area", "if this is in your support area"). Never write or(support,AttachedTo(support)). Decipher's "in / from a player's support area" is ruled the same way (Q-0101) and is built: `support(<whose>)`, below.

**A character hindered while assigned or skirmishing leaves it, and a minion so freed is not a leftover minion (CR 5.0 "hinder"; CR 4.2 "assignment phase").** `mechanics/HinderedCharacterCannotSkirmishTests` holds the rule on real cards: neither character is assigned afterwards; a skirmish that loses its only minion, or its companion, is over with no wound to anyone; one that keeps a second minion goes on with that minion's strength alone. Where the assignment was made by an assignment ACTION, the character that was NOT hindered is on the Free Peoples player's list again at the ordinary assignment that follows, and the hindered one is not. Where it was made at the ordinary step (Úlairë Toldëa 103_79 hindering the stronger companion given to him), the freed minion is NOT offered to the Shadow player, and that is the rule, not a doubt: "If an assigned minion subsequently becomes unassigned as a result of a card's game text (e.g. a triggered ability), it is not a leftover minion and is not eligible to be assigned by the Shadow player." Do not raise it as a question.

**A hindered Ring-bearer cannot be discarded or returned to hand (F-0174).** FIXED in this clone (F-0174): a hindered Ring-bearer cannot be discarded or returned to hand by anything; a definition needs no not(ring bearer) for that.

**"Discard <X> he bears" beside a bar on X ("cannot bear", "may not be played on") is a standing discard, built as Shadowfax 17_24 is (Q-0223: B; Q-0229: C; built by engine pass 26).** The two sentences are two effects. The discard is a required `Trigger` with `trigger: {type: ConstantlyCheck}`, `requires: {type: canSpot, filter: <X>,attachedTo(bearer)}` and `effect: {type: discard, select: all(<X>,attachedTo(bearer))}`, and no `played` or `restored` trigger beside it; the `canSpot` is what keeps it from being carried out, and blipping in the client, on every action (Ketura), so it is never left off. The bar is the `cantBear` or `cantPlayCardsOn` modifier, and refuses new bearings only. Built so: Hearken to Me 4_94, Shadowfax 4_100, Andúril 7_79, Black Dart 8_69 (its Archery ability now only transfers it), Shadowfax 17_24 (possessions only: an artifact hand weapon borne before it is played stays, F-0427) and 67_24; Gandalf's Staff 102_77 has the discard with no bar. **Whose support area: support(you), support(opponent) and the rest (F-0177, Q-0101).** BUILT (engine pass 12, F-0177, Q-0101): "in / from <a player's> support area" is written `support(you)`, `support(opponent)`, `support(free people)`, `support(shadow)`, `support(owner)`. **What a `packet.py --find` pattern is tried against.** Each card is up to three lines, as printed: the id line (id, name, culture, type, race or item class, `unique`, the numbers, an ally's home, a signet), `keywords: ...`, and the game text with no markup in it (`Regroup:</b>` matches nothing: write `Regroup:`). The pattern is tried against those lines with a newline between them, whatever its capitals, so `.` and `.*` stay inside one line and `\n` steps to the next: `'keywords:.*Ranger'` is the keyword and never the word in a game text (Uruk Scout 2_47), and a culture with a keyword is one search, `'\[shire\].*\n.*keywords:.*Tale'`. `^` is the start of the id line. Every card of one title is `'^\S+  Sting(,|  )'`: after the id and two spaces the title runs to a comma where there is a subtitle and to two spaces where there is none, so `Sting$` and `^Sting` find nothing and a bare `Sting` finds Sting of Shelob as well (dec-b2_109). A search that finds nothing says so when its pattern has a tag in it or runs a `.*` across lines (engine pass 25; dec-b1_87, dec-b1_247, dec-b1_350). With `--skip`, the ids given AND every other version of each are left out, of `--find` and of `--defs` (the original of an erratum, the errata of an original: 51_221 beside the batch card 1_221; dec-b1_195), and `--defs` also keeps shut a card whose game text is a batch card's own with a word or two changed (Uruviel 1_67 beside Silinde 1_60, the same `copyCard`: dec-b1_53): choose another helper, or read it in stage 2 without `--skip`. `lookup.py <terms> --skip <ids>` does the like for the reference files: an entry of the shapes or the pitfalls that names a batch card, and such a finding's row, is named by its heading and not printed; the rulings log and a question's entry are printed whatever they name (dec-b1_311).

**cardtest.py run ends with a blank line.** `cardtest.py run` ends with a blank line: the result line is the one before it (`| tail -2`). Its `OK (N tests)` counts the active tests only: a parked (`@Ignore`) test is an `I` among the dots and is not in N. `run` takes a test file of the logic or the common module as well as the server's (`cardtest.py run gemp-lotr/gemp-lotr-logic/src/test/java/<...>Tests.java --out <name>`, alone or beside card ids: it reads the class's package from the source, as `probe` does; engine pass 24, at-server). A test of `gemp-lotr-async` is beyond its classpath: it does not compile, `run` says why in a line, and Maven runs it. What runs the tests is JUnit 4; a class whose tests are JUnit 5's `@ParameterizedTest` over a `@CsvSource` (library/FormatLibraryTests, the server module's only one) is run all the same by `run`, `suite` and `probe`, a row a test, so `suite all` counts its seven rows; anything else of JUnit 5 in such a class shows as a failing test that says Maven runs it (engine pass 25, at-sweep). `cardtest.py probe --help` prints a usage line.

**Grep before calling a property unique to a block (Aldor 4_262).** Before saying "no card of block X has this property, so a Hobbit-set helper it is", grep the definitions: Aldor 4_262 has homes 3T and 6T. `packet.py --find` prints an ally's home on its id line (`home 6F`, `home 3T/6T`), so `--find 'ally.*home \S*6'` lists the allies whose home is a site 6 in stage 1, with no grep (engine pass 25, dec-b1_31). The PC errata were in that style too until engine pass 21 wrote them out (Q-0158: 2A): every erratum now has `unique:`, `side:` where it has a culture, the Decipher capitals, and `keywords:` / `timewords:`.

**A definition's `revision:` can be behind the sheet (F-0294, Shadowfax 67_24).** The packet prints the sheet row of the definition's own revision number, and since engine pass 19 any row of a higher revision under "A later row of the sheet", with the address of the picture the client shows today (from `PC_Cards.js`). **lint's `second-copy` and `ruled` warnings.** `second-copy`: the card is not unique, its text says "this <type>" or "here", and no scenario has two aliases of its blueprint. Put a second copy in play wherever it could change a reading (the Standard's F), or say in Notes why none could; the warning then stands for the reviewer to read. `ruled`: a test is parked on a question, and the warning names the rulings-log entries that name the card or that question; an entry on the card's sheet row or revision answers nothing, one that names the question has answered it.

**lint's `not-asked`, `not-in-hand` and `conditional` warnings (engine pass 21).** Each is a reading or an answer that may show nothing. `not-asked`: an offer read as absent with nothing at that moment of the test that shows the player being asked; put his `Awaiting...PhaseActions()` or his decision beside it, or move the reading to where he is asked (for the `...NotAvailable` twin: a card read as offered in the same choice, or the choice's minimum). For a response EVENT read as not offered to a player who is asked nothing at that moment (the Shadow player's event after a skirmish, when the next thing asked is the Free Peoples player's "Choose next skirmish to resolve"), read `assertFalse(scn.ShadowAnyDecisionsAvailable())` beside the other player's pending decision, with another test showing that he IS asked there when the event can be played (Card_01_247_Tests). `not-in-hand`: a play read as refused for a card no cheat took out of its draw deck. `conditional`: an answer under an `if` or a `while` on a reading of the table; assert what is asked and answer it. A warning that is wrong on its board is let off on its line with `// lint-ok: <why>`, which the reviewer reads. On an error's side: a `field` in a test class, `@Ignore` sharing a line with `@Test`, an empty `FreepsChoose("")`, and a Race to Mount Doom stats test without its `Intensity:` line or `getIntensity()` are refused.

**lint's `title-kinds` warning, and a `no C-` it passes over.** `title-kinds`: the definition names a title (`name(Gollum)`, `title(Merry)`) that cards of more than one culture or type carry, and every card of that title the test file names is of one kind. Where the text has no culture or type word, give each kind a test of its own (the Standard's F; never two cards of one unique title on a board), or say in Notes why the text shuts the others out; the warning then stands for the reviewer. The Hobbit sets are not counted, nor a card naming its own title. A `no C-:` line under a clause whose only cost is the card discarding itself gives no `waived` warning, and the Standard no longer asks for the line.

**lint's `race-culture` warning (Q-0213: B).** It no longer asks for a board. It fires only where a definition goes by a race (`elf`, `dwarf`, `hobbit`, `wizard`, `wraith`) and its text names that race's culture and never the race, or goes by `culture(...)` where the text names the race and never the culture. Then the definition is at fault (F-0019, F-0291): file the finding; lay no board and give no card a culture or a race. It says nothing where text and definition agree.

**lint's `cardinfo` warning (Q-0145).** `lint.py` raises a warning of kind `cardinfo` when a definition's cardInfo block departs from the rest of the definition or from what the block is on the other cards of its kind (`cardinfo.py <id>` shows the same, `cardinfo.py --survey` what each kind's fields are). No test reads cardInfo and none is written against it: put the definition's line right and report it as a `data` finding. On a PC erratum `//id:` is the erratum's own id and revision (`54_192.1`), never the original's (`4_192.1`): "`id` is for the card's unique identifier, and parent is supposed to point to the original" (Q-0158: 1A; the 143 that named the original were changed by engine pass 21, and the check takes the one form). An image name's revision number is not compared with `revision:`.

**lint's `census` warning (Q-0220: B).** A word of the game text that nothing in the definition holds, read three ways (`census.py <id>` prints the same; `--all` for every definition). `phrase`: "[culture] <kind>" with no one filter that has both ("heal a [rohan] ally" built as any ally, Supplies of the Mark 4_290; "another [men] Man" as any other [men] card, Furious Hillman 14_10). `kind`: a kind of card the text names (companion, ally, minion, a race, exhausted, wounded, a possession ...) that no filter has ("and neither is exhausted", A Promise 12_128; an ally where the text says companion, Houses of Lamentation 10_61). `done`: a thing the text speaks of doing or reading (exert, roaming, killed, a spot that stands before a "to" ...) with nothing of its family anywhere in the effects (Pressing On 7_325 had no exertion, Vilya 3_27 no Shadow condition to spot). A match is a place to read, not a fault. Read the definition against the text there: where the definition is at fault, file the finding (`hjson-logic`) and show it by a test; where something else carries the word (a trigger only such a card can set off, a rule of the game, an effect of the engine's own: Elessar's Edict 8_33 has no "prevent" because its discard is the prevention), say what in Notes, and the warning stands for the reviewer. It says nothing where text and definition agree, on a definition with no `gametext:` (the Hobbit sets), or on a Race to Mount Doom modifier.
